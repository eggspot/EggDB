## D673 A Finnish stemmer and the finnish text-search configuration — Decided by the code-only session (2026-11-12, D355); roadmap M8, stemmers for every major language (D30)

- `eggdb-search/src/finnish.rs` follows the published Snowball description of the Finnish stemmer: R1 and R2 over
  the vowels `a e i o u y ä ö`; step 1 removes the particles `-kin -kaan -kään -ko -kö -han -hän -pa -pä` after a
  vowel, `n` or `t`, and `-sti` in R2; step 2 the possessives with their conditions (`-ni` after `kse` leaves `ksi`);
  step 3 the case endings, longest first, falling back to a shorter ending when a longer one's condition fails (so
  `adressiin` loses only `n`); step 4 the comparative endings in R2; step 5 the plural `i`/`j` after a case ending, or
  a `t` after a vowel otherwise; step 6 tidies long vowels, a final `a ä e i` after a consonant, `oj`, `uj` and `jo`
  in R1, and a double consonant before the final vowels anywhere.
- The stop word list is Snowball's Finnish list: the forms of `olla`, the negative verb, the personal, demonstrative,
  interrogative and relative pronouns in their cases, and the common conjunctions and postpositions.
- `to_tsvector('finnish', ...)` and the other text-search functions accept the configuration name, as PostgreSQL's
  `finnish` configuration does.
