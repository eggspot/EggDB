## D685 SQL*Plus COLUMN text wrapping in eggdb shell — Decided by the code-only session (2026-11-12, D355); roadmap M5, SQL*Plus in `eggdb shell` (D150, D633)

- A text value longer than its column's `FORMAT An` width now continues on the following lines of the same column,
  as SQL*Plus's default `WRAPPED` does, instead of being cut (D633). The other columns of those continuation lines
  are blank. Line breaks inside a value start new lines too.
- `COLUMN name WORD_WRAPPED` breaks at the last space that fits and drops the spaces at the break, and
  `COLUMN name TRUNCATED` keeps D633's cut (first line only). `WRAPPED`, `WORD_WRAPPED` and `TRUNCATED` are read in
  their abbreviations (`WRA`, `WOR`, `TRU`), and `COLUMN name` lists them with the other options.
