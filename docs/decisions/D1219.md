## D1219 PERMUTE in row patterns — Decided by the code-only session (2026-11-17, D355); roadmap M16, `MATCH_RECOGNIZE` for complex event processing (D55, D768)

- **What users get.** `PERMUTE(A, B, C)` in a `MATCH_RECOGNIZE` pattern (and in `row_pattern_matches` and `eggdb_streams.match_recognize`) matches its items in any order. Items may be any pattern (`PERMUTE(A+, (B | C))`).
- **How.** The pattern parser expands `PERMUTE` into an alternation of every ordering, in the lexicographic order of the listed positions, which SQL:2016 and Oracle give as the preference among them; the matcher tries alternatives in order, so the listed order wins a tie. Six items at most (720 orderings), keeping the program within the matcher's instruction limit.
- **Left.** More than six items, and pattern exclusion (`{- -}`).
- **Proof.** `row_pattern.rs`: two variables matched in the reverse order, a repeated item, seven items refused, an unclosed list refused.
