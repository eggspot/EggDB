## D1059 A benchmark against Mosquitto and EMQX — Decided by the code-only session (2026-11-13, D355); roadmap M16 (D33, D215)

- `eggdb-broker-compare mqtt` (D1056's crate) speaks MQTT 3.1.1 with a client of its own: `CONNECT` (with EggDB's user
  and password, anonymous for the others), `PUBLISH` at QoS 1 counting `PUBACK`s, and `SUBSCRIBE` at QoS 0.
- One run publishes 20,000 messages of 100 bytes with at most 100 unacknowledged, timing each window, while a
  subscriber on a second connection receives them, timing every hundred; it reports the publishing and the receiving
  side against Mosquitto and against EMQX, with EggDB's ratio for each.
- CI's `broker-compare` job runs EMQX 5.6 as a service and Mosquitto 2 in a container with its no-authentication
  configuration, next to EggDB's `--mqtt-listen`; reported, not gated. A broker that is not running is skipped.
- Learned from the MQTT 3.1.1 specification; no code was read.
