## D532 MongoDB capped and time-series collections — Decided by the code-only session (2026-11-11, D355); roadmap M9

`create` (createCollection) now reads its options instead of ignoring them.

- **Capped:** `capped: true` needs `size` (bytes, rounded up to 256 and at least 4096, as
  MongoDB does; `InvalidOptions` 72 without it) and takes `max` (documents). Each insert records
  the document's place in an insertion-order list kept beside the documents (a new record kind,
  3, in the documents keyspace: the collection, a sequence number and the `_id` key), and when the
  collection then holds more than `max` documents or more than `size` bytes, the oldest documents
  are removed first. A `find` without a sort returns a capped collection in insertion order
  (natural order). Drop and rename carry the list along.
- **Time-series:** `timeseries: {timeField, metaField, granularity}` is kept, `listCollections`
  reports the collection with `type: "timeseries"` and its options, and every insert must hold
  a UTC datetime in `timeField` (BadValue otherwise, MongoDB's message). Documents are stored as
  they come, not in MongoDB's bucketed form.
- **Listing:** `listCollections` shows the options the collection was created with.
- **Left out:** tailable cursors and `awaitData`, the checks that refuse growing updates of
  capped documents, time-series bucketing, `expireAfterSeconds` and its TTL deletions, and
  `convertToCapped`.
