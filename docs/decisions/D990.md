## D990 GridFS checks — Decided by the code-only session (2026-11-13, D355); roadmap M9, GridFS (D197)

- GridFS is a convention drivers follow over two ordinary collections (`<bucket>.files` and `<bucket>.chunks`, with a
  unique index on `files_id` and `n`), so it needs no server feature of its own: inserts, finds sorted by `n`,
  unique indexes (D529), deletes and updates of the file document cover it.
- CI's `resp-clients` job now checks it with pymongo's own `gridfs` module against `eggdb serve --mongo-listen`: a
  file of 13 chunks put, read back and found by name with the legacy `GridFS` API, and with `GridFSBucket` a custom
  bucket and chunk size, upload and download by stream and by name, a read after `seek`, `find`, `rename` and
  `delete` removing every chunk.
