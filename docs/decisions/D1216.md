## D1216 pgRouting's Bellman–Ford, DAG shortest path and biconnected components — Decided by the code-only session (2026-11-17, D355); roadmap M12, topology and routing at pgRouting's level (D57)

- **What users get.** After `CREATE EXTENSION pgrouting`: `pgr_bellmanFord(edges_sql, start_vid, end_vid [, directed])` and `pgr_dagShortestPath(edges_sql, start_vid, end_vid)` with `pgr_dijkstra`'s columns, and `pgr_biconnectedComponents(edges_sql)` (`seq`, `component`, `edge`, a component named by its smallest edge).
- **How.** In `routing/structure.rs`. Bellman–Ford relaxes every arc once per node, over a graph built by the same edge reader with negative `cost` and `reverse_cost` kept as costs (`graph_of`, the reason Bellman–Ford exists next to Dijkstra), and fails with `22023` when a relaxation still changes a cost after as many rounds as there are nodes, or lowers the start's own cost: a negative cycle reachable from the start. The DAG shortest path checks the directed graph has no cycle (Kahn's count) and then walks it with D782's Dijkstra, whose answer on a DAG with costs of zero or more is the same; its negative costs mean no way, as in `pgr_dijkstra`. Biconnected components come from the same iterative walk as D1215's articulation points, keeping a stack of the edges seen and closing a component each time a child cannot reach above its parent. The path rows of `dijkstra`, Bellman–Ford and the DAG walk come from one `path_json`.
- **Decided by research.** pgRouting's own data marks a missing reverse direction with a negative `reverse_cost` (often -1); Bellman–Ford here reads such a value as a cost, so those queries should give `reverse_cost` as NULL or leave it out. To check against pgRouting's own results once a built tree can run its samples.
- **Left.** The DAG walk's negative costs, self-loops in biconnected components, turn restrictions and contraction.
- **Proof.** Unit tests in `routing/structure.rs` (two triangles and a bridge as three components, a negative edge taken, an unreachable end, a negative cycle refused by both, a DAG path) and SQL tests in `pgrouting.rs`.
