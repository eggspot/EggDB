## D1341 Vectorized filter and aggregate kernels over column values, with AVX2 chosen at runtime — Decided by the code-only session (2026-11-25, D355); roadmap M5 "Vectorized scans, filters and projections ..." (D40, D179, D201)

- **What it adds.** `eggdb-columnar` gets kernels that work on a column's values at once: `filter_integers` and `filter_floats` compare every value with a constant (`=`, `<>`, `<`, `<=`, `>`, `>=`) into a `Selection`; `sum_integers` (exact, in `i128`, never overflowing), `sum_floats`, `min_max_integers` and `Selection::count` aggregate the selected values; selections combine with `and`. `IntegerChunk::count_where` answers from the chunk's statistics when the whole chunk is in or out, and filters the decoded values otherwise.
- **SIMD chosen at runtime.** Each kernel is a plain loop over eight lanes that the compiler vectorizes. On x86-64 the same loops are compiled a second time with AVX2 enabled and picked when the processor reports AVX2, so one binary runs on every x86-64 and uses 256-bit vectors where they exist; elsewhere the baseline vectors (SSE2, NEON, WebAssembly SIMD where enabled) carry them. No hand-written intrinsics: the only `unsafe` is calling the AVX2 copies after the check.
- **Where it stands.** These are the building blocks the vectorized executor runs; the executor itself, over columnar tables (D201 leaves table storage open) and over row batches, numeric (decimal) kernels, string kernels and grouped aggregates are left, with the benchmarks against DuckDB and ClickHouse.
- **Proof.** Tests compare every comparison's selection, count and sum, and `count_where` on an encoded chunk, with plain loops over 1,000 mixed-sign values; a sum of twenty `i64::MAX` stays exact; float filters, sums and the minimum and maximum of a selection are checked.

**Status:** code written, unbuilt (code-only mode, D355).
