## D782 Routing at pgRouting's level: Dijkstra, driving distance and connected components — Decided by the code-only session (2026-11-12, D355); roadmap M12, topology and routing at pgRouting's level (D57)

- The graph algorithms are EggDB's own Rust (`routing.rs`), taking edges in pgRouting's documented shape (`id`,
  `source`, `target`, `cost`, optional `reverse_cost`, a negative cost meaning no way in that direction, the
  directed and undirected readings pgRouting documents): Dijkstra's algorithm with a binary heap, ties broken by the
  smaller edge id so a route is repeatable; driving distance (Dijkstra cut at a cost); and connected components by
  an iterative depth-first walk (no recursion over the graph). Up to 5,000,000 edges per call.
- `CREATE EXTENSION pgrouting` adds `pgr_dijkstra`, `pgr_drivingDistance` and `pgr_connectedComponents` with
  pgRouting's names, arguments and result columns, written from its public documentation, so routing queries written
  for PostGIS and pgRouting run unchanged: each runs the edges query, gathers its rows as JSON and expands the
  answer into rows.
- The rest of pgRouting (A*, many-to-many and turn restrictions, `pgr_createTopology` and the topology functions that
  need geometry endpoints, TSP and flow algorithms) is left.
