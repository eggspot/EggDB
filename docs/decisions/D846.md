## D846 Geometry typmods — Decided by the code-only session (2026-11-12, D355); follow-up of D845

- `geometry(subtype[, srid])` columns (PostGIS's typmod) are `geometry` columns (D845) with a check, as `vector(n)`
  carries one (D796): `ST_GeometryType(col) = 'ST_<Subtype>'` for `Point`, `LineString`, `Polygon`, `MultiPoint`,
  `MultiLineString`, `MultiPolygon` or `GeometryCollection` (`geometry` takes any), and `ST_SRID(col) = srid` when an
  SRID other than 0 is given. A value of another subtype or SRID is refused on write like any check.
- Z and M subtypes (`PointZ`, `PointM`, `PointZM`) are refused at `CREATE TABLE`, since coordinates beyond two are
  not kept yet. `geometry` and `geometry(...)` were text columns before (the typmod was skipped); `geography` still
  is.
