## D887 A SPARQL 1.1 query and update reader — Decided by the code-only session (2026-11-12, D355); follow-up of D886

- `eggdb-rdf` reads SPARQL 1.1 with its own lexer and recursive-descent parser written from the grammars of the
  SPARQL 1.1 Query Language (section 19) and SPARQL 1.1 Update (section 3), and translates group graph patterns into
  the algebra as section 18.2 describes: triple blocks become basic graph patterns, `OPTIONAL` a left join (with the
  optional group's filter as its condition), `MINUS`, `UNION`, `GRAPH`, `BIND` (extend), inline and trailing `VALUES`,
  sub-`SELECT`s, and a group's `FILTER`s applied to the whole group.
- Queries: `SELECT` (with `DISTINCT`/`REDUCED`, `*` or variables and `(expression AS ?v)`), `ASK`, `CONSTRUCT` (both
  forms) and `DESCRIBE`; `PREFIX` and `BASE`; `FROM` and `FROM NAMED`; `GROUP BY`, `HAVING`, `ORDER BY`, `LIMIT`,
  `OFFSET`. Patterns take property paths (`/`, `|`, `^`, `*`, `+`, `?`, negated sets), predicate-object and object
  lists, blank node property lists, collections and literals in every form. Expressions take `||`, `&&`, `!`, the
  comparisons, `IN`/`NOT IN`, arithmetic, `EXISTS`/`NOT EXISTS`, the aggregates (`COUNT` with `*` and `DISTINCT`,
  `SUM`, `MIN`, `MAX`, `AVG`, `SAMPLE`, `GROUP_CONCAT` with `SEPARATOR`), and built-in and IRI-named function calls
  by name, which the evaluator answers.
- Updates: `INSERT DATA`, `DELETE DATA`, `DELETE WHERE`, `DELETE ... INSERT ... WHERE` with `WITH` and `USING`,
  `CLEAR`, `DROP` (the same as `CLEAR`, since a graph exists while it holds quads), `CREATE`, `ADD`, `MOVE`, `COPY`,
  separated by `;`. `LOAD` (fetching by IRI) and `SERVICE` (federation) are refused as not supported yet.
- Blank nodes in patterns are non-distinguished variables; in `INSERT DATA` and `INSERT` templates they are blank
  nodes. Requests are limited to 1 MiB and nesting to 64.

**Status:** code written, unbuilt (code-only mode, D355).
