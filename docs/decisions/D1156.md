## D1156 Replies measured before they are built: RESP's EXEC and large values, Bolt records and rows waiting for PULL, HTTP response bodies — Decided by the code-only session (2026-11-14, D355); roadmap M6a (D284, D307)

- **RESP.**
  - `Reply::encoded_bytes_at_most` bounds a reply's encoded size without building it.
  - `EXEC` runs its queued commands with a shrinking reply room: each command gets what the earlier replies left,
    so a transaction's whole reply never passes the room one command alone could take.
  - `GET`, `GETSET` and `GETDEL` ask the new `KeysRead::stored_bytes` for the value's size and answer `-OOM` before
    reading a value larger than the room. `GET` also measures the bulk reply before cloning the value into it.
  - `stored_bytes` defaults to the stored value's length, so every keyspace answers it; a store that keeps value
    lengths can answer without reading the value.
- **Bolt.**
  - A result's rows waiting for `PULL` now hold the budget (`pending_held`) from `RUN` until they are pulled,
    discarded, reset or logged off. A result that cannot be held fails its `RUN` with
    `Neo.TransientError.General.MemoryPoolOutOfMemoryError`, and the connection goes on after `RESET`, as a
    failing `PULL` already did.
  - Each `RECORD` is measured (`record_bytes_at_most`, a PackStream bound per value, node, relationship and path)
    before it is built.
  - The rows a `PULL` takes leave the pending hold before their records are held, so no row counts twice.
- **HTTP.** `eggdb_http::response_room_bytes()` tells a handler how large its response may be before the listener
  refuses it: the budget left plus what the connection already holds, at least the 64 KiB never counted. It is
  set for the length of each handler call on that thread, and is `usize::MAX` outside one. The
  Elasticsearch-compatible document `GET` and `_source` use it to answer `429 circuit_breaking_exception` before
  reading a stored document larger than the room. `_mget` and search already held as they built.
- **Already done in D307.** The queues' leasing counts the stored messages it offers (`Queues::receive_fitting`),
  and an AMQP delivery that cannot fit even alone is refused with `506` and stays unleased rather than being
  offered every round.
- **Tests.** `a_large_value_and_an_exec_s_replies_are_measured_against_the_reply_room` (RESP) and
  `rows_waiting_for_pull_hold_the_budget_until_they_are_pulled_or_discarded` (Bolt). The Bolt test of records
  past the budget now expects the `RUN` to fail.
