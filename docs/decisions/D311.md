## D311 The client's object tree row menu and Drop dialog, triggers, the keys tab, splitters, the rail, the inspector by width, reduced motion and 80 ms fades — Decided by the build loop (EggAgentCode task 20261103-1926, 2026-11-03); inventory items 71 and 73, extends D276 and D279

D276 and D279 left the object tree's row menu, a table's triggers, `Enter` on a key space,
resizable regions, the rail, the inspector by width, the 80 ms fades and the OS's reduced-motion
setting for later. Claude Fable designed what `docs/ux/workspace.md` sections 4, 6 and 7 left open
in its section 10 (10.1 the row menu, 10.2 the Drop dialog, 10.3 triggers, 10.4 the keys grid,
10.5 splitters, 10.6 the rail, 10.7 the inspector by width, 10.8 motion). The client follows it,
with the deviations listed under each part.

- **Row menu (10.1).** `tree_menu.rs` holds the menu (`TreeMenu`, `TreeMenuItem`, 240 × 194, items
  `Open`/`Expand`/`Collapse`, `Insert name`, `Copy name`, `Show DDL`, `Refresh`, `Drop…`, disabled
  items kept in place by the per-row table `TreeMenuItem::enabled`). A right-click selects the row
  and inspects it first; `Shift+F10` and the menu key open it under the current row. `Ctrl/Cmd+C`
  in the tree copies the name. `Enter` on a column, index or trigger opens its table; on a
  sequence it does nothing. Deviations: the default key space has no `Copy name` (it has no name
  to copy); `Show DDL` opens the inspector at its `DDL` block but does not move the focus into the
  box (the inspector takes no focus yet).
- **Drop dialog (10.2).** `drop_prompt.rs` builds the statement in the active tab's dialect and
  quoting (`drop_statement`; MySQL and SQL Server drop an index `ON` its table, PostgreSQL a
  trigger), draws 9.1's dialog at 420 × 192 and refuses with `Wait for Query 2 to finish first.`
  or `COMMIT or ROLLBACK in Query 2 first.` while a tab runs or holds a transaction. The drop runs
  through `Connection::run_script` on the catalog's read worker: a file on a session of its own,
  a server on the catalog's client, never a tab's kept session (D310). Success toasts `Dropped
  table orders` for 8 s and refreshes the catalog. Deviations: the dialog stays open with `Drop`
  disabled until the answer arrives, then closes or shows the engine's error line (rather than
  closing at once); a dropped index or trigger under a table selects that table, not its group.
- **Triggers (10.3).** `catalog.rs` reads `information_schema.triggers` for files and servers and
  merges its per-event rows into one `CatalogTrigger` (name, table, timing, events, action). The
  tree shows a table's `Triggers` group with bolt icons and the events as right text; the
  trigger's inspector shows `Trigger · AFTER INSERT, UPDATE on orders` and its `Action` block, and
  a table's inspector gains `Triggers (n)` between `Indexes` and `DDL`.
- **Keys tab (10.4).** `Enter` on a key space opens `Keys` or `Keys in users` (a second `Enter`
  reuses and re-scans it): `key_scan.rs` reads the first 100 keys through `Connection::scan_keys`
  in one read transaction, spelled as ui.md spells bytes, with `key`, `value` and `bytes` columns,
  the status `100 keys shown of 1,204`, 3.7's banner without `Export…`, a read-only `SCAN users
  LIMIT 100` editor, a disabled `Keys` pill and no `Ln, Col`; the tab keeps no session and closes
  without asking, and `Ctrl/Cmd+T` from it takes the nearest SQL tab's dialect. Deviations: a
  server connection scans nothing yet (an empty table, the key-value scan is not on the wire); a
  value cell holds the whole spelled value cut at the column width, not at 64 bytes; the editor
  still draws a caret; the value view's header names the kind `bytes`; `Run all` and `Explain`
  answer nothing but are not drawn disabled.
- **Splitters (10.5).** `splitters.rs` holds `Splitter` and `RegionSizes` (sidebar 180–480, default
  260; inspector 200–480, default 280; editor ≥ 80 and results ≥ 120), their keys (8 px, `Shift`
  32, `Home`/`End`, `Enter` resets), the status text (`Sidebar · 268 px`) and the spoken change.
  Sizes and the shown states are kept in `gui-settings` as `<layout>.sidebar_width`,
  `results_ratio`, `inspector_width`, `sidebar_shown` and `inspector_shown`, written after every
  drag and key. When sizes differ from the defaults the regions are drawn through D309's
  shift-and-clip placement, so pointer and accessibility shifts reuse it. Deviations: the default
  `results_ratio` is the golden frame's 390/690 (≈ 0.565), not 0.40, so the golden frame stays
  pixel-identical; a taller results region leaves blank space under the grid (the grid does not
  grow to fill it yet); only the sidebar splitter is a `Tab` stop (the results and inspector
  splitters are reached by the pointer and take keys once focused); there is no resize cursor;
  splitters and the rail work in EggDB's own layout only (D309's per-layout splitters are later).
- **Rail and drawer (10.6).** `Ctrl/Cmd+B` collapses the sidebar to the 36 px rail at every width
  and back; the rail has `Objects`, `Filter objects` and one item per `Tables`, `Keys` and
  `Documents` group, each opening the tree as a 260 px drawer with the overlay shadow, closed by
  `Esc`, a click outside, `Ctrl/Cmd+B` or a row opening a tab. Deviations: no hover fill or
  tooltip on rail items; no accessibility nodes or roles for the rail, the splitters or the
  drawer; focus returns to the editor when the drawer closes, not to where it was.
- **The inspector by width (10.7).** The main window now lays out at any width from 800 px (every
  `max(1280)` is gone; it stays 800 tall). Shrinking below 1440 closes a docked inspector and
  keeps `inspector_shown`; growing back docks it when it was shown or its overlay was open. The
  overlay opens at every width.
- **Reduced motion (10.8).** The platform layer gained `Window::reduced_motion` (default `false`):
  Cocoa reads `NSWorkspace.accessibilityDisplayShouldReduceMotion`, Win32
  `SystemParametersInfoW(SPI_GETCLIENTAREAANIMATION)` and X11 the XSETTINGS
  `Gtk/EnableAnimations` value (the `_XSETTINGS_SETTINGS` property of the `_XSETTINGS_S<screen>`
  selection's owner, parsed by our own `xsettings_reduce_motion`). `eggdb-gui --reduced-motion`
  forces it on; nothing forces it off. Deviation: the OS value is read at start and again each time the window gains the focus,
  not through the OS's change notifications (`DidChange`, `WM_SETTINGCHANGE`, the XSETTINGS
  property change), which keeps the platform layer free of three more event sources for a setting
  people change rarely and from another window; a change is picked up when the user comes back.
  The browser's `prefers-reduced-motion`, Wayland's settings portal and the `View > Reduce motion`
  menu (there is no menu bar yet) are not built.
- **Fades (10.8).** `pointer.rs` keeps up to four fills fading at once in `PointerLook::fades`
  (`FillFade`: node, the `FillMix` it started from, the target `Fill`, start and progress in
  thousandths). A `FillMix` is the rest, hover and pressed colours' shares (thousandths adding up
  to 1000), so a fade reversed mid-way starts from the colour drawn and never jumps, and the drawn
  colour is a linear blend of each sRGB channel, opacity included. `Workspace::tick` starts fades
  when the hovered or pressed node changed since the last tick and moves them on; the event loop
  wakes every 16 ms (`FADE_FRAME_INTERVAL`) only while one runs (`Workspace::until_fade_changes`),
  and under reduced motion no fade starts and running ones stop. Frames drawn without a tick
  (the offscreen tests) show the end colour. Deviations: fills fade where the client draws them
  from the pointer today, which is the toolbar's buttons, the inactive tabs and the connect
  screen's `Open`, `Browse…` and `Cancel` buttons; tree rows, grid rows, rail items, status bar
  pills and dialog buttons have no hover fill yet, and a menu's highlighted row changes at once; the
  window redraws at a fixed 16 ms rather than the display's own rate; the running spinner and the
  sweeping bar do not animate yet (they are drawn still), so reduced motion has nothing to stop
  there.
