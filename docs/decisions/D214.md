## D214 Messaging protocols share durable queues in eggdb-queues; AMQP 0-9-1 first — Decided by the build loop (by judgement, 2026-10-31); refines D33

Part 4.M16-2 asks for RabbitMQ clients over AMQP 0-9-1; parts 4.M16-3 to 4.M16-5 add MQTT, SQS,
NATS, AMQP 1.0 and STOMP.

- **One queue store for every messaging protocol.** `eggdb-queues` keeps each queue's messages
  on EggDB's key-value transactions (the collection `queue0`) with a lease per received message
  (SQS's visibility timeout, and the unacknowledged state of AMQP and STOMP), acknowledgement by
  delete, delays and receive counts. A lease that runs out makes the message visible again, so
  delivery is at least once, and a server restart loses no message. A receive first reads, and
  writes only when a message is visible, so idle consumers cost no commits.
- **AMQP 0-9-1 from the published specification**: the handshake with PLAIN, channels, the
  default exchange and direct, fanout and topic exchanges with bindings, queue declare (with
  server-named queues), bind, unbind, purge and delete, publishing with content headers and
  bodies (properties kept with the message), pushed consumers with `basic.qos` prefetch,
  `basic.get`, ack, nack, reject, recover and publisher confirms. Consumers hold leases of 30
  minutes, released at once when their channel or connection closes.
- **Exchanges and bindings live in the server's memory** in this slice; durable declarations need
  a catalog of their own and come next. Queues and messages are durable now.

Left: durable exchanges and bindings, exclusive and auto-delete queues, `tx` transactions,
headers exchanges, dead-lettering and message TTL, heartbeats sent by the server, TLS, and a
benchmark against RabbitMQ.
