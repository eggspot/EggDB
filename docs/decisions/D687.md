## D687 An Indonesian stemmer and the indonesian text-search configuration — Decided by the code-only session (2026-11-12, D355); roadmap M8, stemmers for every major language (D30)

- `eggdb-search/src/indonesian.rs` follows Snowball's Indonesian stemmer, which is Tala's algorithm: a word's measure
  is its count of vowels, and each step runs only while the measure is above two, lowering it by one per removal.
- In order: a particle (`-kah`, `-lah`, `-pun`); a possessive pronoun (`-ku`, `-mu`, `-nya`); then either a
  first-order prefix (`di-`, `ke-`, `me-`, `meng-`, `men-`, `mem-`, `meny-`, `peng-`, `pen-`, `pem-`, `peny-`, `ter-`,
  with `meny-`/`peny-` before a vowel read as `s` and `mem-`/`pem-` before a vowel as `p`) followed by a suffix and a
  second-order prefix, or a second-order prefix (`per-`, `pe-`, `ber-`, `be-` before a consonant and `er`, and
  `pelajar`/`belajar` to `ajar`) followed by a suffix. The suffixes `-kan`, `-an` and `-i` depend on the prefix
  removed, as Tala's rules say (no `-kan` after `ke-`/`peng-` or `pe-`, no `-an` after `di-`/`meng-`, no `-i` after
  `ber-`, `ke-`/`peng-` or an `s`).
- Snowball has no Indonesian stop word list, so the `indonesian` configuration drops none, as PostgreSQL's does.
