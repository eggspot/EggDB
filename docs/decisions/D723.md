## D723 JetStream for key-value buckets: per-subject limits, rollups, filtered purges, `last_per_subject` and direct gets — Decided by the code-only session (2026-11-12, D355); roadmap M16, follow-up of D722

NATS's key-value buckets are streams named `KV_<bucket>` on `$KV.<bucket>.>` that keep a few messages per subject;
clients read and write them with JetStream requests. D722's streams gain what those requests need:

- A subject index per stream (`L` keys: stream, subject, sequence), kept with every store and removal, so the
  messages of one subject are found without reading the stream.
- `max_msgs_per_subject`: a store drops that subject's oldest messages beyond the limit.
- `allow_rollup_hdrs` with the `Nats-Rollup` header: `sub` removes the subject's earlier messages, `all` the
  stream's; on a stream without it the publish is refused.
- `STREAM.PURGE` with `filter`, `seq` (below) and `keep`; `deny_purge` and `deny_delete` refuse purges and message
  deletes.
- `deliver_policy: last_per_subject`: a new consumer first delivers the last message of each subject (under its
  filter), then new messages.
- `$JS.API.DIRECT.GET.<stream>` with `seq`, `last_by_subj` or `next_by_subj`, and
  `$JS.API.DIRECT.GET.<stream>.<subject>`: the message itself, with `Nats-Stream`, `Nats-Subject`,
  `Nats-Sequence` and `Nats-Time-Stamp` headers before its own, or a `404 Message Not Found` status. It is answered
  whether or not the stream sets `allow_direct`, which is kept and shown.
- `interest` retention is kept and shown but removes nothing yet; watches, which need push consumers' idle
  heartbeats and flow control, are still left (D722).
