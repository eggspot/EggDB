## D902 MIME parts and attachments in the email store — Decided by the code-only session (2026-11-12, D355); roadmap M16, follow-up of D892 to D894

- `eggdb_mail::mime` reads a message's MIME tree (RFC 2045, RFC 2046): each entity's header and body ranges in the
  stored message, its content type and parameters (quoted values and escapes), transfer encoding, `Content-ID`,
  `Content-Description` and `Content-Disposition`; multipart bodies split at their boundary lines (preamble and
  epilogue dropped, the line break before each boundary belonging to the boundary); `message/rfc822` and
  `message/global` parts read as the message they hold; `multipart/digest` parts defaulting to `message/rfc822`.
  At most 32 levels and 1,000 parts are read, so a hostile message cannot exhaust memory.
- Part bodies decode from base64 and quoted-printable, and text parts read in UTF-8, US-ASCII, ISO-8859-1 and its
  Windows variant; another charset is read as UTF-8 with replacement characters and flagged.
- IMAP: `BODY` and `BODYSTRUCTURE` now describe the whole tree (multipart lists with their subtype; for
  `BODYSTRUCTURE` the extension data: parameters, disposition, language and location as `NIL`), with envelope and
  inner structure for attached messages; `BODY[<part>]`, `BODY[<part>.MIME]`, `BODY[<part>.HEADER]`,
  `BODY[<part>.TEXT]` and `BODY[<part>.HEADER.FIELDS (...)]` address parts by RFC 3501's numbers.
- JMAP: `Email/get` returns `bodyStructure`, `textBody`, `htmlBody` and `attachments` with each part's `partId` (the
  IMAP part number), `blobId` (`<email id>p<part>`), decoded `size`, `type`, `charset`, `disposition`, `name` and
  `cid`; `hasAttachment`; `preview` and `bodyValues` from the decoded text parts. A text part shows in `textBody` or
  `htmlBody` unless it is an attachment; when a message has only a plain or only an HTML part, both lists use it,
  as RFC 8621 describes. Downloading a part's blob returns its decoded bytes with its content type.
- Left: IMAP `IDLE`, JMAP uploads, submission and push, and TLS.
