## D702 iCalendar and vCard read as jCal and jCard — Decided by the code-only session (2026-11-12, D355); roadmap M16, CalDAV, CardDAV and WebDAV (D56)

- `icalendar_parse(text) → jsonb` reads iCalendar (RFC 5545) into jCal (RFC 7265): `["vcalendar", [properties],
  [components]]` with events, to-dos, alarms and time zones nested as components. `vcard_parse(text) → jsonb` reads
  vCard 3 and 4 (RFC 6350) into jCard (RFC 7095): `["vcard", [properties]]`. Several objects in one text come back
  as an array of them.
- Both unfold continuation lines (a line starting with a space or tab), read parameters (quoted values may hold `;`
  and `:`), drop property groups (`item1.TEL` is `tel`), lower-case names as jCal and jCard write them, and unescape
  text (`\n`, `\,`, `\;`, `\\`). vCard's structured `N`, `ADR`, `ORG` and `GENDER` become arrays of their parts.
- Value types are not looked up in the registries, so every property's type is jCal's `"unknown"` and its value is
  text. Nesting stops at 16 levels and a text at 200,000 lines.
- This gives calendar and contact data to SQL; the CalDAV, CardDAV and WebDAV servers are left.
