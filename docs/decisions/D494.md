## D494 Kafka topic retention — Decided by the code-only session (2026-11-11, D355); roadmap M16, D33 and D213

- `CreateTopics` reads the configs `retention.ms` and `retention.bytes` (whole numbers, `-1` for
  no limit; anything else answers `INVALID_CONFIG`, 40) and keeps them with the topic (its record
  grows from the partition count to partition count, retention time and retention bytes; a
  4-byte record, as auto-created topics and older files have, means no limit).
- Each produce applies its partition's retention after appending: from the oldest batch on,
  batches whose newest record timestamp is older than `retention.ms`, or while the partition holds
  more than `retention.bytes`, are deleted, stopping at the first batch kept. The newest batch is
  never deleted, so the next offset never goes back, as Kafka never deletes the active segment.
  `ListOffsets` earliest and `Fetch` follow, since they read what remains.
- Not written: retention on a timer for partitions that get no writes, `AlterConfigs` and
  `DescribeConfigs`, and `cleanup.policy=compact`.
