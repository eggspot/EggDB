## D469 Text-search weights (`setweight`) and `ts_headline` — Decided by the code-only session (2026-11-11, D355); roadmap M8, D195

- A tsvector's positions carry PostgreSQL's weights `A`–`D` in their two top bits (positions stay
  up to 16,383): the text form writes `'cat':3A,5`, reading takes the letter after a position, and
  matching and phrase distances use the position alone.
- `setweight(vector, 'A')` sets every position's weight; `setweight(vector, 'A', '{cat,rat}')`
  only those of the listed lexemes. An unknown weight letter answers `22023`.
- `ts_rank` and `ts_rank_cd` count each matching position by its weight, relative to `D`, so
  unweighted vectors rank exactly as before (D195); the optional weights array (`{D, C, B, A}`,
  four values in [0, 1], as an array or its text) replaces PostgreSQL's defaults
  `{0.1, 0.2, 0.4, 1.0}`. Ranks stay EggDB's own values with PostgreSQL's ordering.
- `ts_headline([config,] document, query [, options])` marks every word whose lexeme the query asks
  for (prefix terms included) with `StartSel`/`StopSel`, and shows: the whole document with
  `HighlightAll`; up to `MaxFragments` fragments of `MaxWords` words around matches joined by
  `FragmentDelimiter`; or one excerpt of up to `MaxWords` words starting at the first match,
  trimmed of words of `ShortWord` letters or fewer at its ends while longer than `MinWords`, and
  the first `MinWords` words without a match. Options are read as PostgreSQL writes them, with
  its errors for bad values. The excerpt choice is EggDB's own, simpler than PostgreSQL's cover
  search, so excerpts may differ from PostgreSQL's on long documents.
- With three arguments, an options-looking third argument (one holding `=`) means
  `(document, query, options)`, else `(config, document, query)`, since every argument is text.
