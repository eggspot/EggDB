## D441 PromQL's remaining functions — Decided by the code-only session (2026-11-11, D355); roadmap M16, extends D334

- Range functions: `stddev_over_time`, `stdvar_over_time`, `quantile_over_time(φ, range)`,
  `present_over_time`, `mad_over_time`, `absent_over_time`, `changes`, `resets`, `idelta`, `deriv`
  and `predict_linear(range, seconds)`, the last two by least squares as Prometheus fits them
  (`predict_linear` anchored at the evaluation time).
- Instant functions: `clamp`, `clamp_min`, `clamp_max`, `round(v, to_nearest)`, `sort`,
  `sort_desc` (NaN last), `absent` (1 with the selector's equality labels when nothing matches),
  `sgn`, `deg`, `rad`, the trigonometric and hyperbolic functions and their inverses, and the date
  functions `year`, `month`, `day_of_month`, `day_of_week`, `day_of_year`, `days_in_month`, `hour`
  and `minute`, reading sample values as Unix seconds, or `time()` when called without an argument.
- Left from the roadmap item: the `@` modifier and negative offsets, `limitk`, `limit_ratio`,
  string results, named capture groups in `label_replace`, a query timeout, and pushing label
  matchers into the SQL read.
