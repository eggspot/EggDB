## D1350 EXPLAIN ANALYZE measures each step of a join — Decided by the code-only session (2026-11-25, D355); roadmap M6c "Times on the steps inside a join (a counter per level in JoinedRows::extend, inner loops = outer rows) ..." (D327)

- **What users get.** `EXPLAIN ANALYZE` of a join prints measured `(actual time=... rows=... loops=...)` on every step, not only on the top node: the outer scan once, each inner lookup or scan with `loops` equal to the rows that reached it from the outer side and `rows` per loop, and each `Nested Loop` or `Hash Join` with the rows it produced, as PostgreSQL prints them. The same figures feed the other plan forms that read the measured steps.
- **How.** The first join a recorded statement runs claims a meter, as the first `SELECT` claims D327's; `JoinedRows::extend` counts the rows that reach each level after the filters that apply there, with the time of the first and last. Level 0 is the outer table; level `k` is the rows after joining the `k`-th inner table, so an inner step's loops are level `k-1`'s rows and its rows level `k`'s. A statement not under `EXPLAIN ANALYZE` never claims a meter and pays one `None` check per row. A hash join's build scan, which runs once outside the levels, stays unmeasured.
- **Left.** Times on each iterator inside a subplan, on an `UPDATE` or `DELETE`'s scan and on aggregates; per-operator rows in `SET STATISTICS PROFILE`; `DISPLAY_CURSOR` and the rest of the item.
- **Proof.** An `EXPLAIN ANALYZE` test joins three rows to their primary-key matches and reads 3 rows on the join, `rows=3 loops=1` on the outer scan and `rows=1 loops=3` on the inner lookup.
