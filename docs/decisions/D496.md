## D496 LogQL log queries and Loki's label endpoints — Decided by the code-only session (2026-11-11, D355); roadmap M16, D39

- `GET|POST /loki/api/v1/query_range` runs LogQL log queries over the lines pushed through D495:
  a stream selector with `=`, `!=`, `=~` and `!~` matchers (Loki's rule holds: at least one
  matcher must not match the empty value) and line filters `|=`, `!=`, `|~`, `!~` (double-quoted
  or backtick strings), with `start`/`end` in Unix nanoseconds or seconds (default the last hour),
  `limit` (default 100, at most 5,000) and `direction` (`backward`, the default, or `forward`).
  The answer is Loki's `streams` result: one entry per label set with `["<ns>", "<line>"]` values.
- `GET /loki/api/v1/labels` and `/loki/api/v1/label/<name>/values` list label names and values
  seen in the last six hours, so Grafana's Loki data source can browse them.
- Reads take at most a million rows of the window before filtering. Parsers (`| json`,
  `| logfmt`), label filters after the pipe, line formatting, metric queries (`rate`,
  `count_over_time`) and `/loki/api/v1/query` are not written, and TraceQL with Tempo's API stays
  open.
