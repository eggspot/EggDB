## D1160 QUIC of our own, first the wire layer: varints, frames, packet headers, packet numbers and RFC 9001's protection — Decided by the code-only session (2026-11-14, D355); roadmap M6a (D34, D315)

- **What exists already.** D34 names HTTP/1.1, HTTP/2, gRPC and QUIC as shared foundations built once.
  HTTP/1.1 (`eggdb-http`) and HTTP/2 with gRPC framing (`eggdb-http2`) are shared crates already, used by the REST
  APIs, Arrow Flight SQL and etcd. QUIC is new: the workspace crate `crates/eggdb-quic`, written from RFC 9000,
  9001 and 9002 with no third-party code (D1), built in stages.
- **Stage 1, the wire layer:**
  - `varint`: QUIC's variable-length integers, and a checked `Cursor`.
  - `frame`: all 21 frame types of RFC 9000 section 19. ACK ranges are decoded with checked gaps, STREAM frames
    with and without offset and length, and `is_ack_eliciting` is provided.
  - `packet`: long headers (Initial with token, 0-RTT, Handshake, Retry with its integrity tag left for the
    caller), version negotiation, short headers. Packet number lengths follow appendix A.2, and decoding follows
    appendix A.3.
  - `protection`:
    - the Initial secrets from the version 1 salt;
    - `quic key`, `quic iv` and `quic hp` for AES-128-GCM, AES-256-GCM and ChaCha20-Poly1305, and `quic ku` for
      key updates;
    - AEAD protection with the packet number XORed into the IV and the header as associated data;
    - header protection with an AES block or a ChaCha20 keystream over a 16-byte sample, masking the first
      byte's low 4 bits (long) or 5 bits (short) and the packet number.
- **Hostile input.** Every length is checked against the bytes left; connection IDs over 20 bytes, impossible ACK
  ranges and unknown frame types are errors, never panics (D9).
- **Tests.**
  - RFC 9000's varint examples (appendix A.1) and its packet number decoding example (appendix A.3).
  - RFC 9001 appendix A.1's Initial secrets, keys, IVs and header protection keys for both sides.
  - A round trip of every frame.
  - Protected packets opening only under their own keys and unchanged, with AES and with ChaCha20.
- **Next stages**, each its own batch:
  - TLS 1.3 carried in CRYPTO frames per encryption level, through a QUIC mode of `eggdb-tls` with the
    `quic_transport_parameters` extension;
  - the connection state machine with streams and flow control;
  - loss detection and congestion control (RFC 9002);
  - a server on the platform layer's datagram queue (D1114);
  - HTTP/3.

**Status:** code written, unbuilt (code-only mode, D355).
