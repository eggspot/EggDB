## D727 GQL quantified path patterns of one relationship — Decided by the code-only session (2026-11-12, D355); roadmap M13, GQL quantified paths (D333, D535)

- A parenthesized path pattern with a quantifier, `((a:Station)-[r:LINK]->(b:Station)){1,3}`, now matches the
  relationship repeated within the quantifier's bounds (at most 15 hops, D206), with the group's left node pattern
  holding at the start of every hop and its right one at the end, so labels and properties of every node along the
  way are checked, not only of the ends.
- Its variables are group variables, as in GQL: `r` is the list of relationships, `a` the list of each hop's start
  node and `b` the list of each hop's end node.
- The group may start the pattern or follow a node, and the node after it may be left out
  (`MATCH (x)((a)-[:R]->(b)){2}`); missing nodes match any node. A relationship inside a group cannot carry its own
  quantifier.
- A group of more than one relationship (`((a)-[:R]->(b)-[:S]->(c)){1,3}`) and a `WHERE` inside a group are refused
  as not supported yet.

**Status:** code written, unbuilt (code-only mode, D355).
