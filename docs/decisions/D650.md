## D650 A Spanish stemmer and the spanish text-search configuration — Decided by the code-only session (2026-11-12, D355); roadmap M8, D30, D649

After German (D649), Spanish is the next most used language among EggDB's PostgreSQL users with
full-text search (`to_tsvector('spanish', ...)`).

- `eggdb-search` gets a `spanish` module written from Snowball's published Spanish algorithm.
- It uses three regions: RV (after the next vowel when the second letter is a consonant, after
  the next consonant when the first two letters are vowels, else from the fourth letter), R1 and
  R2.
- Step 0 removes an attached pronoun (`me se sa sela selo selas selos la le lo las les los nos`)
  in RV. It must follow `iéndo ándo ár ér ír`, which lose their accent, or `ando iendo ar er ir`,
  or `yendo` after `u`.
- Step 1 removes the standard suffixes, each with Snowball's region and follow-up conditions:
  - `anza … imientos` in R2;
  - `ación`, `ador` and the like, then `ic`;
  - `logía` → `log`, `ución` → `u`, `encia` → `ente`;
  - `amente` in R1, then `iv` (and `at`), or `os`, `ic` or `ad`;
  - `mente`, then `ante`, `able` or `ible`;
  - `idad`, then `abil`, `ic` or `iv`;
  - `iva`/`ivo`, then `at`.
- Only when step 1 removed nothing:
  - step 2a removes the `y` verb forms after `u`;
  - otherwise step 2b removes the other verb endings in RV, dropping the `u` of `gu` before
    `en es éis emos`.
- Step 3 removes `os a o á í ó`, or `e é` (with the `u` of `gu`), in RV. Acute accents are
  dropped at the end.
- Snowball's Spanish stop words (the common part of the list, with the forms of `estar`, `haber`,
  `ser` and `tener` that are most frequent) are dropped. `Config::Spanish` is named `spanish`.
- Test: the module's examples (`chica` → `chic`, `cantando` → `cant`, `niñas` → `niñ`,
  `rápidamente` → `rapid`, `diciéndole` → `dic`). Snowball's Spanish vocabulary file should be
  checked when the build runs.
