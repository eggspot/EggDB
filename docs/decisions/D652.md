## D652 An Italian stemmer and the italian text-search configuration — Decided by the code-only session (2026-11-12, D355); roadmap M8, D30, D650

PostgreSQL's `italian` configuration is the next one EggDB lacked. Snowball's Italian algorithm
has the same shape as its Spanish one (D650), with Italian's own lists.

- Preparation: acute accents become grave, `u` after `q` and `u`/`i` between vowels are marked so
  they count as consonants. RV, R1 and R2 are computed as in Spanish.
- Step 0 removes an attached pronoun (`ci gli la … vene`) in RV after `ando`/`endo`, or replaces
  it with `e` after `ar`/`er`/`ir`.
- Step 1 removes the standard suffixes, each with Snowball's conditions:
  - most in R2;
  - `azione`/`atore` and the like, then `ic`;
  - `logia` → `log`, `uzione`/`usione` → `u`, `enza` → `ente`;
  - `amento`/`imento` in RV;
  - `amente` in R1, then `iv` (and `at`), or `os`, `ic` or `abil`;
  - `ità`, then `abil`, `ic` or `iv`;
  - `ivo` and the like, then `at` (and `ic`).
- Step 2 runs only when step 1 removed nothing: it removes the longest verb suffix in RV.
- Step 3 removes a final vowel in RV, then an `i` before it in RV, and turns `ch`/`gh` in RV into
  `c`/`g`. The marks are lowered at the end.
- Snowball's Italian stop words are dropped. `Config::Italian` is named `italian`.
- Test: `parlando` → `parl`, `ragazzi` → `ragazz`, `velocemente` → `veloc`, `amiche` → `amic`.
  Snowball's vocabulary file should be checked when the build runs.
