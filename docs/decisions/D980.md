## D980 A Kubernetes operator in Rust — Decided by the code-only session (2026-11-13, D355); roadmap M6c, D25 and D45's operator

- `crates/eggdb-operator` is a small separate binary (`eggdb-operator`), not part of `eggdb`: deployment tooling, like
  the Helm chart, so D3's one binary still holds for the database. It is written by us on our own pieces: D920's HTTPS
  client to the API server (trusting the service account's CA through `SSL_CERT_FILE`), the bearer token of the pod's
  service account, and `eggdb_sql::json` to read answers. No Kubernetes client library.
- **The resource.** `EggDB` (`eggspot.app/v1alpha1`, `deploy/operator/crd.yaml`, with a status subresource): `image`,
  `port` (5432), `mysqlPort`, `passwordSecret` (required; `key` defaults to `password`), `tlsSecret`, `storage.size`
  (10Gi) and `storage.className`, and `resources.cpu` and `resources.memory`.
- **Reconciling.** Every `RECONCILE_SECONDS` (15) it lists the resources of its namespace (`WATCH_NAMESPACE`, or its own)
  and server-side-applies (`application/apply-patch+yaml`, field manager `eggdb-operator`) a Service and a StatefulSet
  for each, both with an owner reference to the resource, so deleting the resource deletes them through Kubernetes'
  garbage collector, and the operator never deletes anything itself. The StatefulSet runs `eggdb serve /data/eggdb
  --create` on a persistent volume, listening beyond loopback with TLS (`--tls-certificate` from the TLS secret, or
  `--tls-self-signed`), the password from the secret as `EGGDB_PASSWORD`, and a TCP readiness probe. The status gets
  `phase` (`Pending`, `Ready` or `Failed` with a message), `readyReplicas` and the in-cluster `endpoint`.
- Polling rather than watching keeps the client to whole requests (no streamed watch); a list every 15 seconds is
  cheap at the scale one namespace runs. `deploy/operator/operator.yaml` has its ServiceAccount, the namespaced Role
  it needs (read the resources, write their status, apply Services and StatefulSets) and its Deployment.
- Left: the operator's image in the release workflow, backups, restores and upgrades driven by the resource, and more
  than one replica, which waits for replication under `serve` (M14).
