## D654 A Dutch stemmer and the dutch text-search configuration — Decided by the code-only session (2026-11-12, D355); roadmap M8, D30, D649

PostgreSQL's `dutch` configuration is next among the European languages EggDB's PostgreSQL users
search in.

- `eggdb-search` gets a `dutch` module from Snowball's published Dutch algorithm.
- Preparation drops accents and umlauts. It marks `y` at the start or after a vowel, and `i`
  between vowels, so they count as consonants. R1 starts at least three letters in, then R2.
- Step 1 handles the longest of:
  - `heden` → `heid` in R1;
  - `en`/`ene` in R1 after a non-vowel that does not end `gem`, then `kk dd tt` are undoubled;
  - `s`/`se` in R1 after a non-vowel other than `j`.
- Step 2: a final `e` in R1 after a non-vowel goes, then the undoubling. Whether it went is
  remembered.
- Step 3, in R2:
  - `heid` not after `c` goes, and an `en` before it is treated as in step 1;
  - `end`/`ing` go, then `ig` not after `e`, or else the undoubling;
  - `ig` goes when not after `e`;
  - `lijk` goes, and step 2 runs again;
  - `baar` goes;
  - `bar` goes only if step 2 removed an `e`.
- Step 4 halves a doubled `a e o u` between a non-vowel and a final non-vowel (`maan` → `man`).
- Snowball's Dutch stop words are dropped. `Config::Dutch` is named `dutch`.
- Test: `lichamelijk` → `licham`, `maanden` → `maand`, `bomen` → `bom`,
  `mogelijkheden` → `mogelijk`. Snowball's vocabulary file should be checked when the build runs.
