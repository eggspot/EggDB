## D560 `geo_distance` and `geo_bounding_box` queries — Decided by the code-only session (2026-11-11, D355); roadmap M8, D196, D518

D518 sorts by distance from a point; filtering by place was missing, and Kibana's maps and most
store-locator code send these two queries.

- **`geo_distance`**: `{"distance": "25km", "<field>": point}` with Elasticsearch's distance units
  (a bare number is meters) and `distance_type` `arc` (the default, haversine) or `plane`; a
  document matches when any of its points lies within the distance.
- **`geo_bounding_box`**: corners as `top_left`/`bottom_right`, `top_right`/`bottom_left`, or
  `top`, `left`, `bottom`, `right`; a box whose left edge is east of its right edge crosses the
  180th meridian. A top below the bottom is refused as Elasticsearch does.
- Points are read as D518 reads them (`{"lat","lon"}`, `"lat,lon"`, `[lon, lat]`, or an array of
  those), from the field's path without flattening arrays, so `[lon, lat]` stays one point. Both
  queries score 1 (they are filters), whatever the field's mapping says. Geohashes, WKT and
  `geo_shape` are not read.
