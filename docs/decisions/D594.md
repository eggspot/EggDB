## D594 Index and component templates — Decided by the code-only session (2026-11-12, D355); roadmap M8, D196, D559

Logstash, Beats, Fluent Bit, OpenTelemetry exporters and most log pipelines install an index
template, then write to `logs-<date>` indices that the first write creates. The template is what
makes those indices map `host` as `keyword` and `@timestamp` as `date`. Index templates waited for
mappings, which D559 applied.

- `PUT` or `POST _index_template/<name>` takes `index_patterns`, `priority` (a non-negative
  integer), `composed_of`, `template` (`mappings`, `settings`, `aliases`), `version`, `_meta`,
  `data_stream` and `allow_auto_create`.
- `_component_template/<name>` takes `template`, `version` and `_meta`.
- Both are checked before they are kept: unknown fields and malformed members get
  `x_content_parse_exception`. `?create=true` refuses to overwrite. A body is at most 256 KiB, a
  name at most 255 bytes without `*`, `,`, spaces or quotes, and there are at most 1000 templates
  of each kind.
- They are kept as their JSON text under record kinds 5 and 6.
- `GET` lists them as `{"index_templates": [{"name", "index_template"}]}` (or
  `component_templates`), by name, comma list or glob. `HEAD` answers 200 or 404, and `DELETE`
  answers `acknowledged` or 404.
- When an index is created, explicitly or by its first document, the matching template with the
  highest priority is applied (the first by name on a tie). Its component templates' mappings are
  merged in `composed_of` order, then its own. The request's `mappings` are merged over them,
  member by member, with later values winning. The template's aliases are added, and
  `PUT <index>` now adds the request's `aliases` too.
- Settings are accepted and not applied, as EggDB's indices have no shards or replicas to set.

Not done: legacy `_template` (Elasticsearch's pre-7.8 API), `_simulate_index` and `_simulate`, data
streams, and refusing two templates of the same priority whose patterns overlap.
