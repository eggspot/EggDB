## D559 Elasticsearch mappings shape `term` and `range` in `_search` — Decided by the code-only session (2026-11-11, D355); roadmap M8, D196

Index mappings were stored and returned but never applied, so every field acted as untyped
JSON: `term` on `genre.keyword` (what Kibana and most clients send, since Elasticsearch's
dynamic mapping gives every string a `keyword` sub-field) found nothing, `term` on a `text` field
compared the whole string, and `range` on dates compared text.

`crate::mappings` now reads the mappings of the indices a search covers (`FieldTypes`, the first
index's type winning for a path two indices map differently) and rewrites the parsed query
before it runs:

- **Multi-fields.** `title.raw` mapped under `title`'s `fields`, and `x.keyword` of any field that
  is unmapped or mapped `text` (the dynamic default), read `x`'s value as a keyword: exact, not
  analyzed. `x.keyword` of a field mapped as anything else stays a missing field, as in
  Elasticsearch. This applies to `term`, `terms`, `range`, `exists`, `prefix` and `match`.
- **`text`.** `term` and `terms` on a `text` field match one of its analyzed tokens (so
  `{"term":{"msg":"error"}}` finds "Disk ERROR found" and `"ERROR"` finds nothing, as there).
- **`date`.** `range` compares milliseconds: values may be epoch milliseconds or
  `strict_date_optional_time` strings (UTC without an offset), bounds may use date math (`now`,
  `<date>||`, `+1d`, `-2h`, `/d` with `y M w d h H m s`), and a rounded `gt`/`lte` bound goes to
  the unit's last millisecond as Elasticsearch's does. A bound that cannot be read keeps the old
  text comparison. `format` and `time_zone` are not read yet.
- **`ip`.** `term` takes an address or a CIDR block (`10.0.0.0/8`, IPv6 too) and `range` compares
  addresses, IPv4 mapped into IPv6's space.

`match` on a `keyword` field still analyzes its text (the query keeps only the tokens), and geo,
`nested`, `flattened`, the vector types and `completion` are not applied yet; the by-query APIs and
`_count` do not read the mappings yet.
