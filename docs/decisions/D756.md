## D756 Order-book and double-entry ledger primitives (eggdb_finance) — Decided by the code-only session (2026-11-12, D355); roadmap M11, order-book and double-entry ledger primitives (D50)

- `CREATE EXTENSION eggdb_finance` installs both primitives as tables and PL/pgSQL functions in the `eggdb_finance`
  schema, so they run inside the caller's transaction beside the rest of its data.
- Double entry: `accounts` (asset, liability, equity, income or expense, each in one currency), `entries` and
  `postings`, with debits positive and credits negative. `post(description, lines)` takes a JSON array of
  `{account, amount}`, needs at least two lines, and refuses an entry whose postings don't sum to zero in every
  currency (23514). Entries and postings never change (55000 from a trigger): `reverse(entry)` posts the opposite
  entry once, linked by `reverses`. `balance(account [, at])` and `trial_balance([at])` read them as of a time.
- Order book: `place_order(instrument, side, quantity [, price, owner, time_in_force])` stores the order and matches it
  against the open orders on the other side by best price, then by arrival (`order_id`), each trade at the resting
  order's price, into `trades`. An order without a price is a market order; `gtc` leaves the rest on the book, `ioc`
  cancels it, and `fok` cancels the whole order unless it can fill at once. `cancel_order(order)` and
  `book(instrument [, depth])`, the price levels on each side, complete it.
- Numbers are `numeric`, so prices and amounts keep their scale. Microsecond tail-latency targets in the benchmarks,
  stop orders, and settlement of trades into ledger entries are left.
