## D774 W3C Web of Things Thing Descriptions (eggdb_iot) — Decided by the code-only session (2026-11-12, D355); roadmap M16, IoT models: W3C Web of Things (D57)

- `CREATE EXTENSION eggdb_iot` stores Thing Descriptions (W3C TD 1.0 and 1.1, as JSON) in `eggdb_iot.things` and
  checks the parts the specifications make mandatory: the TD context, `title`, `securityDefinitions`, `security`
  naming defined schemes, and at least one form with an `href` on every interaction affordance. A thing's id is its
  `id`, or a stable `urn:eggdb:thing:` name derived from its title when it has none.
- The affordances' data schemas (the TD's JSON-Schema-like `DataSchema`: `type`, `const`, `enum`, `minimum`,
  `maximum`, `minLength`, `maxLength`, `minItems`, `maxItems`, `items`, `required`, `properties`, nested up to 10
  levels) are enforced on property readings and event data as they are recorded, so a device that sends a value
  outside its own description is refused (22023) instead of stored. Write-only properties take no readings.
- Readings and events are rows (`eggdb_iot.readings`, `eggdb_iot.events`) indexed by thing, property and time, so
  `eggdb_streams` windows, time buckets and retention apply to them.
- Fetching readings over the forms' protocols (HTTP, CoAP, MQTT bindings), Thing Models, `oneOf` and semantic
  annotations, and DTDL (after D58's legal check) are left.

**Status:** code written, unbuilt (code-only mode, D355).
