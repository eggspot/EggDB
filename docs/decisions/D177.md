## D177 A Redis comparison in its own workspace, with our own RESP client, reported and not gated — Decided by the build loop (by judgement, 2026-10-31); refines D7 and D94

Part 4.M4-3 asks to compare EggDB's key-value speed with Redis-class servers.
`crates/eggdb-redis-compare` follows `eggdb-sqlite-compare` (D94): it is a workspace of its own,
it is never shipped, and it reuses `eggdb-bench`'s harness. It talks to a Redis-compatible
server through a RESP client of about a hundred lines of its own, so it needs no third-party
crate. The workloads are `redis_compare.set` (sets in batches of 100: one EggDB transaction
against 100 pipelined `SET`s) and `redis_compare.get` (point reads over 100,000 keys), with
EggDB embedded at `Durability::Normal` against the server's default persistence. An embedded
store pays no network round trip and a server does, so the ratios are reported and not gated
until EggDB serves RESP itself (M7), when the comparison moves to the same protocol on both
sides. CI's `redis-compare` job runs it against a `redis:7` service container and keeps the
results per pull request.
