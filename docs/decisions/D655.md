## D655 A Portuguese stemmer and the portuguese text-search configuration — Decided by the code-only session (2026-11-12, D355); roadmap M8, D30, D650

PostgreSQL's `portuguese` configuration serves Brazilian and Portuguese applications. Snowball's
Portuguese algorithm follows its Spanish one (D650).

- The nasal vowels `ã` and `õ` are written `a~` and `o~` while stemming, as Snowball does, and
  restored at the end. RV, R1 and R2 are computed as in Spanish.
- Step 1 removes the standard suffixes with their conditions:
  - most in R2;
  - `logia` → `log`, `ução` → `u`, `ência` → `ente`;
  - `amente` in R1, then `iv` (and `at`), or `os`, `ic` or `ad`;
  - `mente`, then `ante`, `avel` or `ível`;
  - `idade`, then `abil`, `ic` or `iv`;
  - `iva`/`ivo`, then `at`;
  - `ira`/`iras` → `ir` in RV after `e`.
- Step 2 runs when step 1 removed nothing: it removes the longest verb suffix in RV.
- When either step changed the word, a final `i` after `c` in RV goes. Otherwise the residual
  `os a i o á í ó` in RV goes.
- Last, `e é ê` in RV go, and with them the `u` of `gu` or the `i` of `ci`. A final `ç` becomes
  `c`.
- Snowball's Portuguese stop words are dropped. `Config::Portuguese` is named `portuguese`.
- Test: `falando` → `fal`, `meninas` → `menin`, `rapidamente` → `rapid`, `nações` → `naçõ`.
  Snowball's vocabulary file should be checked when the build runs.
