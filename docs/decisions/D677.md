## D677 InfluxQL arithmetic in the select list — Decided by the code-only session (2026-11-12, D355); roadmap M16, InfluxQL (D220, D521)

- A select item may be an arithmetic expression over fields, numbers and aggregate calls with `+`, `-`, `*`, `/`
  and `%`, the usual precedence and parentheses: `SELECT a + b * 2`, `SELECT mean(used) / mean(total) * 100`.
- An expression's column is named, as InfluxDB names it, by the field and function names it reads joined with `_`
  (`a + b` is `a_b`, `mean(x) * 2` is `mean`), unless it has an `AS` alias.
- `/` divides in floating point, as InfluxDB does, even between integer fields.
- An expression may not mix aggregate calls with raw fields, and `distinct()` may not appear inside one, as in
  InfluxDB; an expression of aggregates groups and fills like any aggregate item.
