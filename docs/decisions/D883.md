## D883 Linear referencing, line intersections and pgr_nodeNetwork — Decided by the code-only session (2026-11-12, D355); roadmap M12, D204 and D782

- `eggdb-geo` gains `linear.rs`, written from PostGIS's documented behaviour: `ST_LineLocatePoint(line, point)` (the
  fraction of the line's length nearest the point), `ST_LineInterpolatePoint(line, fraction)` and
  `ST_LineSubstring(line, from, to)` (a point when the fractions are equal; fractions outside 0 to 1, or `from > to`,
  are errors, as in PostGIS). They take a `LINESTRING` and keep its SRID.
- `ST_Intersection(a, b)` answers for points (the point, or an empty point) and for lines and multi-lines (the points
  where they cross or touch: an empty point, a `POINT`, or a `MULTIPOINT`, ordered by x then y). Collinear overlaps
  are not reported, and areas (polygon overlays) are refused as not supported yet, which is the overlay work still
  open in M12.
- `pgr_nodeNetwork(edge_table, tolerance, id, the_geom, table_ending, rows_where, outall)` reads the edges' lines,
  cuts each where it crosses or touches another (with a tolerance above zero, also where another line's vertex lies
  within it), and writes `<edge_table>_<table_ending>` (`noded` by default) with pgRouting's columns `id`, `old_id`,
  `sub_id`, `source`, `target` and `the_geom` (EWKT text, as the other topology functions keep), leaving `source`
  and `target` for `pgr_createTopology`. At most 20,000 edges; every pair is compared, so large networks wait for a
  spatial index (R-tree, M12).
- Left: polygon overlays (`ST_Intersection`, `ST_Union`, `ST_Difference` of areas), `ST_Split`, `outall`'s
  untouched-edge copy, and Z and M coordinates.

**Status:** code written, unbuilt (code-only mode, D355).
