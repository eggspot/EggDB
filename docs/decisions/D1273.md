## D1273 Nearest and farthest positions — Decided by the code-only session (2026-11-18, D355); roadmap M4 geospatial (D204, D1272)

- **What users get.** PostGIS's `ST_ClosestPoint(a, b)` (the point of `a` nearest `b`), `ST_ShortestLine(a, b)` and `ST_LongestLine(a, b)` (two-point lines from `a` to `b`), `ST_MaxDistance(a, b)` and `ST_HausdorffDistance(a, b)`.
- **How.** `eggdb-geo` finds the nearest pair by the same candidates `ST_Distance` measures (points against points and segments, segment pairs by their ends' projections, the crossing point when segments cross) plus any vertex inside the other's polygon, where the two meet; the farthest pair is always two vertices. The Hausdorff distance is GEOS's discrete one: the farthest a vertex of either geometry is from the other geometry. Both geometries must share an SRID, and an empty one gives NULL.
- **Left.** `ST_HausdorffDistance`'s densify fraction, `ST_FrechetDistance`, and three-dimensional forms.
- **Proof.** A unit test finds a perpendicular foot, a crossing, a point inside a polygon, the farthest vertices, a Hausdorff distance of two parallel lines and NULL for an empty point.

**Status:** code written, unbuilt (code-only mode, D355).
