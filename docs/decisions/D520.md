## D520 `ST_AsMVTGeom` — Decided by the code-only session (2026-11-11, D355); roadmap M12

`ST_AsMVTGeom(geom, bounds [, extent [, buffer [, clip_geom]]])` returns a geometry in a vector
tile's grid, as PostGIS does, the first half of "Vector tiles (`ST_AsMVT`, `ST_AsMVTGeom`)".
`bounds` is usually `ST_TileEnvelope(z, x, y)`, which EggDB already has.

- **Grid:** coordinates are scaled so the bounds span `extent` cells (4096 by default), with y
  pointing down as tiles draw it, and snapped to whole cells.
- **Clipping:** with `clip_geom` (the default) points outside the tile and its `buffer` (256
  cells by default) are dropped, lines are cut with Liang-Barsky and polygons' rings with
  Sutherland-Hodgman against the buffered tile.
- **Clean-up:** repeated points are removed; lines with fewer than two points and rings with
  fewer than three or no area are dropped (a polygon whose exterior ring goes is dropped); a
  polygon's exterior ring is made positive-area and its holes negative, the Mapbox Vector Tile
  2.1 winding. A geometry with nothing left is `NULL`, as PostGIS returns. The result has no SRID.
- **Where:** `eggdb-geo`'s new `tile_geometry` with `TileGrid`; the SQL function in
  `geo_functions.rs`.
- **Left out:** `ST_AsMVT`, the aggregate that encodes rows as a tile's protobuf layer (it needs a
  whole-row aggregate argument), polygon validity repair beyond winding, and H3 and S2.

**Status:** code written, unbuilt (code-only mode, D355).
