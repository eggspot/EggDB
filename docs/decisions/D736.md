## D736 `CREATE EXTENSION timescaledb`: hypertables, `drop_chunks`, retention and continuous-aggregate policies — Decided by the code-only session (2026-11-12, D355); roadmap M11, retention, downsampling and continuous aggregates (D39, D202, D220)

- `CREATE EXTENSION timescaledb` installs, in SQL and PL/pgSQL on the pg_cron scheduler (D200, installed with it
  when missing), the TimescaleDB functions applications call most:
  - `create_hypertable(relation, time_column_name [, chunk_time_interval, if_not_exists, migrate_data])` records the
    table's time column and returns `(hypertable_id, schema_name, table_name, created)`; rows stay in the table, as
    there are no chunks;
  - `drop_chunks(relation, older_than)` deletes the rows whose time is older than `now() - older_than` and returns
    how many it deleted (TimescaleDB returns the dropped chunks' names);
  - `add_retention_policy(relation, drop_after [, if_not_exists, schedule_interval])` and
    `remove_retention_policy` schedule and unschedule a pg_cron job running `drop_chunks` (daily by default);
  - `add_continuous_aggregate_policy(view, start_offset, end_offset, schedule_interval [, if_not_exists])` and
    `remove_continuous_aggregate_policy` schedule a job running `REFRESH MATERIALIZED VIEW` on the view; the
    refresh is whole, so the offsets are kept and not used;
  - the `timescaledb_information.hypertables`, `dimensions` and `jobs` views.
- `CREATE MATERIALIZED VIEW ... WITH (timescaledb.continuous) AS ...` is accepted: storage options before `AS` are
  read and ignored, so a continuous aggregate is a materialized view refreshed by its policy (downsampling).
- Schedules are rounded to cron: under an hour every N minutes, under a day every N hours, otherwise daily at
  midnight UTC. Errors use PostgreSQL's SQLSTATEs (`42704`, `42710`, `42703`).
- Still left: chunks and compression, incremental refresh of continuous aggregates, `time_bucket_gapfill` and
  automatic partitioning by time.
