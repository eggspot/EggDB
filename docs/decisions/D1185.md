## D1185 Client certificates over TLS 1.2 — Decided by the code-only session (2026-11-15, D355); roadmap M6b, follows D803 and D978; security-critical (D35)

- D803 asked for and checked client certificates over TLS 1.3 only, and refused a TLS 1.2 handshake when a certificate was required. Clients that cap at TLS 1.2 (older JDBC and .NET stacks, embedded devices, `ssl_max_protocol_version=TLSv1.2`) could not sign in with one.
- The TLS 1.2 server (`server12.rs`) now follows RFC 5246, section 7.4: with `--tls-client-ca` it sends a `CertificateRequest` (certificate types `rsa_sign` and `ecdsa_sign`, the same signature schemes TLS 1.3 offers, and an empty authority list, which lets the client send any certificate it has) before `ServerHelloDone`; reads the client's `Certificate` before its `ClientKeyExchange`; checks the chain against the trust store for client authentication, as TLS 1.3 does; and, when a certificate came, reads `CertificateVerify` after the key exchange and checks its signature, with the leaf's key and a scheme the server offered, over every handshake message up to and including `ClientKeyExchange` (kept as they were sent, since TLS 1.2 signs the messages, not a transcript hash). The `CertificateVerify` enters the transcript, so the `Finished` messages cover it; the extended master secret's session hash stops at `ClientKeyExchange`, as RFC 7627 says.
- An empty `Certificate` passes when the certificate is optional and fails the handshake (`handshake_failure`, TLS 1.2's alert for it) when required. The checked chain is the session's `peer_certificates`, so `cert` rules sign the user in exactly as over TLS 1.3.
- Proof: the `tls-clients` CI job now signs `carol` in with psql and a client certificate under a `cert` rule over TLS 1.3 and over TLS 1.2 (`ssl_min_protocol_version` and `ssl_max_protocol_version`), and refuses her without one.
- As a D35 change it needs its own security-only review pass once built: the order of messages, that no path skips `CertificateVerify` when a chain was sent, and the scheme checks.
