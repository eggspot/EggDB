## D739 Elasticsearch analyzers: stop words, synonyms, stemming and folding — Decided by the code-only session (2026-11-12, D355); roadmap M8, synonyms, stop words, and fuzzy and phonetic matching (D30, D196)

- `PUT /<index>` keeps the request's `settings`, and `settings.analysis` (or `settings.index.analysis`) defines
  analyzers: `custom` ones with a `standard`, `whitespace` or `keyword` tokenizer and `lowercase`, `asciifolding`,
  `stop` (a list, `_english_` or `_none_`), `synonym`/`synonym_graph` and `stemmer`/`snowball` (by language) filters
  applied in order; `stop` analyzers; and the built-in `standard`, `simple`, `whitespace`, `keyword` and `stop`
  analyzers and the language analyzers EggDB stems (`english`, `french`, `german`, ... `romanian`).
- A `text` field's `analyzer` (and `search_analyzer`) in the mappings picks one: documents' text in that field is
  analyzed with it when matching and scoring, and `match` and `match_phrase` terms go through the search analyzer's
  filters. Fields without one keep the standard analysis.
- Synonyms: `a, b, c` makes the words equal (each is read as the first) and `a => b` replaces `a` with `b`; rules
  with several words on a side are skipped. `asciifolding` folds Latin-1 and Vietnamese letters.
- Analysis runs at search time, as matching does (no inverted index yet), so changing an analyzer changes results at
  once. A `match` over several fields uses the first field's analyzer for its terms. `_analyze`, `index_analyzer`
  defaults in the settings and custom tokenizers and char filters are still left.
