## D1161 QUIC stage 2: TLS 1.3 without a record layer, and transport parameters — Decided by the code-only session (2026-11-14, D355); roadmap M6a (D34, D1160)

- **TLS for QUIC.** QUIC carries the TLS handshake in CRYPTO frames at three encryption levels and protects its
  packets itself (RFC 9001 section 4). So `eggdb_tls::quic::QuicServerHandshake` is a sans-I/O server handshake:
  - `receive(level, data, random)` takes CRYPTO bytes as they arrive, cut anywhere. It reassembles handshake
    messages per level, at most 64 KiB each.
  - It answers with events:
    - `Send { level, data }`: the ServerHello at Initial, then EncryptedExtensions, Certificate,
      CertificateVerify and Finished at Handshake;
    - `Secrets { level, client, server }`: the Handshake and 1-RTT traffic secrets QUIC derives packet keys from
      (D1160);
    - `Complete`, with the agreed ALPN protocol, the client's transport parameters and its server name, once the
      client's Finished verifies in constant time.
  - `suite()` gives the hash and AEAD for the packet keys.
- **Shared with TCP TLS.** It uses the same suite, group, signature-scheme and ALPN choices as TLS over TCP:
  `choose_suite`, `preferred_share`, `choose_signature_scheme` and `choose_application_protocol`.
- **What QUIC requires.** TLS 1.3 only, an agreed ALPN protocol (`no_application_protocol` otherwise), and the
  client's `quic_transport_parameters` extension (`missing_extension` otherwise). The server's own parameters go
  in EncryptedExtensions.
- **Not offered over QUIC yet.** HelloRetryRequest (a client must send a share in a group EggDB uses, as common
  QUIC stacks do with X25519), resumption, 0-RTT and client certificates. A message at the wrong level or time is
  `unexpected_message`.
- **Transport parameters.** `eggdb_quic::transport_parameters::TransportParameters` reads and writes RFC 9000
  section 18's parameters with their defaults.
  - Reading enforces each parameter at most once, refuses the server-only ones (original destination ID,
    stateless reset token, retry source ID) from a client, and checks RFC 9000's bounds: UDP payload at least
    1200, ACK delay exponent at most 20, max ACK delay below 2^14 ms, stream counts at most 2^60, connection ID
    limit at least 2, connection IDs at most 20 bytes, and an empty `disable_active_migration`.
  - Unknown parameters are ignored, as the RFC asks.
  - Writing leaves out the defaults.
- **Tests.**
  - A full handshake driven by a test client built from the crate's own messages and key schedule: the
    ClientHello is split across two `receive` calls, the Handshake secrets match the client's own derivation, and
    the client's Finished completes it with the ALPN, the parameters and the server name.
  - Refusal without transport parameters and without ALPN.
  - Transport parameters round-trip, repeats and bounds are refused, and unknown ones are skipped.
