## D400 Elasticsearch's `if_seq_no`/`if_primary_term` and `retry_on_conflict` — Decided by the code-only session (2026-11-10, D355); roadmap M8, closes one of D348's leftovers

Optimistic concurrency in Elasticsearch: a write carries the sequence number and primary term it
read, and fails with `409 version_conflict_engine_exception` when the document moved on. EggDB's
documents carry a version, and their `_seq_no` is version − 1 with primary term 1 (D331). Now
`PUT`/`POST _doc`, `_create`, `_update`, `DELETE _doc` and `_bulk`'s actions read `if_seq_no`
and `if_primary_term` from the URL and, inside the write's own transaction, refuse when the
document is missing or its `_seq_no` differs or the term is not 1, with Elastic's message
(`required seqNo [..], primary term [..]. current document has seqNo [..] and primary term
[1]`). Only one of the two is `400 action_request_validation_exception`, a non-number `400
illegal_argument_exception`. `retry_on_conflict` is checked to be a number and needs no retries:
EggDB's writes to one keyspace run one at a time, so an update never loses a race it could retry.
