## D911 MongoDB user-defined roles — Decided by the code-only session (2026-11-12, D355); roadmap M9, follow-up of D737

- The MongoDB listener keeps user-defined roles in `admin.system.roles`, one document per `<db>.<role>` with its
  privileges (`{resource: {db, collection} | {cluster: true} | {anyResource: true}, actions: [...]}`) and the roles
  it inherits, built-in or user-defined.
- Commands: `createRole`, `updateRole` (replacing privileges and/or roles), `dropRole`, `dropAllRolesFromDatabase`,
  `grantRolesToRole`, `revokeRolesFromRole`, `grantPrivilegesToRole` and `revokePrivilegesFromRole`, with MongoDB's
  errors for a role that exists (51002), is missing or names a missing role (31), inherits from itself (17166), or is
  built in. `rolesInfo` now lists the database's user-defined roles beside the built-in ones (or the roles named),
  with `showPrivileges` and `showBuiltinRoles`. `createUser`, `updateUser` and `grantRolesToUser` accept
  user-defined roles. The role commands and `admin.system.roles` need `userAdmin` or `dbOwner`, as user commands do.
- Authorization: a user's roles are expanded through their inherited roles (transitively, at most 1,000); the
  built-in roles among them decide as before (D737), and otherwise the user-defined roles' privileges must cover
  every action the command needs on its database and collection: `find` for reads, `insert`, `update`, `remove`,
  `createCollection`, `dropCollection`, `createIndex`, `dropIndex`, `listIndexes`, `listCollections`, `collMod`,
  `dropDatabase`, the user and role actions, and `find` plus `insert` and `remove` for an `aggregate` ending in
  `$out` or `$merge`. An empty `db` or `collection` in a resource stands for any.
- A session reads its user's roles once (D737); privilege changes reach sessions as roles are re-read for each
  command, while a user's own role list is re-read on the next sign-in.
