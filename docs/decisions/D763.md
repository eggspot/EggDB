## D763 Marketplace listings of shared data (eggdb_marketplace) — Decided by the code-only session (2026-11-12, D355); roadmap M6b, marketplace listings of shared data inside EggDB (D52)

- `CREATE EXTENSION eggdb_marketplace` is a listing catalog on top of ordinary privileges: a provider publishes a
  listing of tables and views with a title, description, terms and an approval mode; consumers (roles of the same
  EggDB) request access, accepting the terms; the provider's decision turns into `GRANT USAGE` on the schemas and
  `GRANT SELECT` on the objects, so access is enforced by the engine's privilege checks, not by the extension.
- Requests keep their whole life (`pending`, `granted`, `refused`, `revoked`) with who decided and when, which is the
  audit trail of who was given which data. `revoke_access` and `withdraw` revoke `SELECT`; `USAGE` on a schema stays,
  since other grants may need it.
- Only the provider who published a listing, or a superuser, decides, revokes or withdraws. A listing shares whole
  objects: a provider shares a subset of rows or columns by listing a view over it. A role that already reads an
  object through another grant keeps reading it after a revoke.
- No payments, prices or external marketplace accounts (the roadmap leaves them out); listings across separate
  EggDB servers (Delta Sharing, D58) are a separate item.
