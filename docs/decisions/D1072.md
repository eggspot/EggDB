## D1072 Sparkplug and Modbus ingest as messages arrive — Decided by the code-only session (2026-11-13, D355); roadmap M16 (D57, D701, D775)

- New crate `crates/eggdb-iot` (depends on `eggdb-sql` only), run by `eggdb serve`.
- **Sparkplug B** (`--sparkplug-subscribe mqtt://[user:password@]host[:port][|group]`, repeatable):
  - A small MQTT 3.1.1 subscriber of the crate's own:
    - `CONNECT` with keep-alive 30 and an optional login;
    - a `SUBSCRIBE` to `spBv1.0/<group>/#` at QoS 1;
    - `PUBACK`s for QoS 1 deliveries and `PINGREQ` while idle.
    - Packet lengths are capped at 16 MiB and checked, never trusted.
  - It works with EggDB's own `--mqtt-listen` or any other broker.
  - Each message's topic gives group, message type, edge node and device. Host `STATE` topics and other
    topics are ignored.
  - Decoding happens in SQL, through the existing `sparkplug_decode` (D701), so the protobuf reader stays in
    one place:
    - a birth certificate (`NBIRTH`/`DBIRTH`) first upserts each metric's alias into `sparkplug_aliases`;
    - then one statement stores every metric as a row of `sparkplug_metrics`;
    - a metric sent only by alias gets its name from the recorded birth, through a left join.
  - The payload is passed as a `bytea` literal.
- **Modbus TCP** (`--modbus-poll 'tcp://host[:port]|unit=1|holding=0+10|input=100+4|coils=0+16|discrete=0+8|every=1000'`,
  repeatable):
  - Each device is polled on its own thread with functions 1 to 4. Ranges are limited to the
    specification's 125 registers or 2,000 bits.
  - Responses are matched by transaction id; exception responses and short data are errors.
  - Every bit and register becomes a row of `modbus_values` (`received_at`, `device`, `unit`, `kind`,
    `address`, `value`), one insert per poll.
- Both create their tables at start. A lost connection is logged and retried after 1 second, doubling up
  to 30, as the OPC UA collector does (D1071).
- Left: writing to Modbus devices, Modbus RTU over serial lines (the platform layer has no serial port
  yet), Sparkplug commands (`NCMD`/`DCMD`) sent back, and the Sparkplug host application's `STATE`.
- Learned from: the Eclipse Sparkplug 3.0 specification, MQTT 3.1.1 (OASIS), and the Modbus Application
  Protocol 1.1b3 and Modbus Messaging on TCP/IP guides. No library source was read.
