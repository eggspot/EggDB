## D921 Multipart uploads of large archives — Decided by the code-only session (2026-11-12, D355); roadmap M18, follow-up of D226 and D919

- `eggdb_s3::client::Bucket::put_from(key, reader, retain_until)` stores an object with S3's multipart upload
  (`POST ?uploads`, `PUT ?partNumber=n&uploadId=...`, `POST ?uploadId=...` with the parts' ETags), in parts of 64
  MiB read one at a time, so an archive needs one part's memory whatever its size and may pass S3's 5 GiB limit for
  one `PUT` (up to 10,000 parts, 625 GiB). With a retention date the upload starts under Object Lock (D919) and every
  part carries its `Content-MD5`. A failure part-way aborts the upload (`DELETE ?uploadId=...`) so no parts are left
  billed, and a completion answered 200 with an `<Error>` body counts as the failure it is.
- `eggdb backup ... s3://...` sends a copy larger than 64 MiB with it, and smaller ones with one `PUT` as before; the
  copy's SHA-256 for the manifest is now computed by streaming the file instead of reading it whole.
- Left: streaming the copy to the bucket without the local staging file, and the simulator test of restoring under
  failures.
