## D1073 JMAP submission — Decided by the code-only session (2026-11-13, D355); roadmap M16 (D56, D894)

- The JMAP endpoint offers `urn:ietf:params:jmap:submission` (RFC 8621 §6 and §7) in its session, as an
  account capability (`maxDelayedSend` 0) and as a primary account.
- **`Identity/get`** answers the account's one identity, `i1`: the user's name, the address
  `<user>@<domain>` and no signatures. `--mail-domain` sets the domain (`localhost` by default).
- **`EmailSubmission/set` create** sends a stored Email:
  - The envelope is the one given, or by default the identity's address and every `To`, `Cc` and `Bcc`
    address. The `Bcc` header is removed from what is sent.
  - Recipients in the account's domain (or `localhost`) are delivered straight to the INBOX, with a
    `Delivered-To` line.
  - Other recipients go through `--smtp-relay host:port` (RFC 5321 client in `smtp_client.rs`: `EHLO`
    falling back to `HELO`, `MAIL FROM`, one `RCPT TO` each, dot-stuffed `DATA`, `QUIT`). Each recipient's
    reply is kept.
  - Without a relay they are refused with `forbiddenToSend` before anything is sent. Other refusals are
    `noRecipients`, `tooLarge`, `invalidRecipients` (with the refused addresses) and `invalidProperties`.
  - `emailId` may be a `#creationId` of an Email created or imported earlier in the same request.
  - Each submission is kept (`undoStatus` `final`, `deliveryStatus` per recipient: `yes` for local
    delivery, `queued` for relay acceptance, `no` for refusals). It is numbered `S1`, `S2`, … from a
    counter in the store.
- `onSuccessUpdateEmail` and `onSuccessDestroyEmail` (by `#creationId` or submission id) run as the
  implicit `Email/set` that RFC 8621 asks for. Its response follows the submission's under the same call id,
  so a client can clear `$draft` and file the message in one request.
- `EmailSubmission/get` and `/query` read the kept submissions. Updates are refused with `cannotUnsend`
  (submissions are final at once) and destroys with `forbidden`.
- Left: JMAP push (EventSource and PushSubscription), TLS for SMTP, IMAP and JMAP, and STARTTLS and
  `AUTH` toward the relay. The relay is trusted as given.
- Learned from: RFC 8620, RFC 8621 and RFC 5321. No mail server source was read.
