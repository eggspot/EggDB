## D250 Web map tiles, Web Mercator and geohashes in `eggdb-geo` — Decided by the build loop (by judgement, 2026-10-31); refines D204 and D57

Track 4.X-22 asks for SQL/MDA arrays, Zarr, HDF5 and NetCDF, GIS at PostGIS parity (raster,
topology, routing, GeoTIFF, the OGC APIs, vector tiles, H3 and S2), genomics formats (VCF,
BAM, CRAM, htsget), and IoT models and ingest (DTDL, Web of Things, OPC UA, Modbus,
Sparkplug). This slice does the groundwork every web map needs.

- **`ST_TileEnvelope`, `ST_Transform` and `ST_GeoHash`** by PostGIS's documented names and
  arguments. A tile's square follows the XYZ scheme (y grows southwards) on Web Mercator's
  world square of ±20037508.342789244 metres, the bounds PostGIS documents.
- **Only 4326 and 3857 for now.** Web Mercator is the spherical formula of EPSG:3857 with the
  WGS 84 semi-major axis, so it needs no projection database. Other SRIDs fail with SQLSTATE
  `0A000` saying which pairs work, rather than returning unprojected coordinates. A general
  projection engine needs the EPSG dataset, whose terms of use come first (D30's rule).
- **Geohash** follows its public definition (interleaved longitude and latitude bits in base
  32) and is checked against the published examples.
- **No panics on input:** zoom is capped at 30, x and y must lie inside the zoom's grid,
  latitudes beyond ±85.0511° are refused for Web Mercator, and every error is SQLSTATE `22023`.
