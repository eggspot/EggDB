## D1078 Image renditions with our own codecs — Decided by the code-only session (2026-11-13, D355); roadmap M16 (D56, D731)

- `image_rendition(image bytea, max_width int, max_height int [, format text [, quality int]]) → bytea` makes a
  rendition of a PNG or JPEG:
  - it is turned upright by the Exif orientation (all eight);
  - it is scaled down to fit the box, keeping its shape and never enlarged;
  - it is written in the source's format, or as `png`, or as `jpeg` at a quality from 1 to 100 (85 by
    default).
- The codecs are EggDB's own, in a new `image` module of `eggdb-content`. Both formats are royalty-free,
  and baseline JPEG's patents have long expired.
  - **PNG decoding:** every colour type and bit depth (1–16, palettes, grey and RGB with or without
    alpha), `tRNS` transparency, all five filters, Adam7 interlacing, and CRC-checked chunks. The image
    data is inflated through the existing zlib reader, bounded to the size the header implies.
  - **PNG encoding:** 8-bit RGBA, the `Sub` filter, deflated through the existing `deflate`.
  - **JPEG decoding:** baseline and extended-sequential Huffman JPEGs (ITU T.81), greyscale or YCbCr, any
    sampling factors, restart intervals, 8- and 16-bit quantisation tables, and Exif orientation read from
    APP1.
    - Refused as not read yet: progressive, lossless, arithmetic-coded, multi-scan and CMYK JPEGs.
  - **JPEG encoding:** baseline JFIF with 4:2:0 subsampling and Annex K's example tables scaled by quality
    (the usual 50-point rule). Transparency is laid over white.
  - **Scaling:** area averaging with alpha premultiplied, so thumbnails stay sharp without ringing and
    transparent edges stay clean.
- Images over 64 megapixels are refused, and every length and index is checked before use, so a hostile
  file is an error, never a panic (D9).
- Tested: PNG round-trips exactly, JPEG within its loss, renditions fit their box, cut-short files are
  errors, and an orientation turns the picture.
- Left: progressive JPEG, WebP, AVIF/HEIF and GIF, and storing renditions next to their originals
  automatically (a trigger or job can call the function today).
- Learned from: the PNG specification (W3C, third edition), ITU T.81 and JFIF 1.02, and Exif 2.3's
  orientation tag. No codec source was read.

**Status:** code written, unbuilt (code-only mode, D355).
