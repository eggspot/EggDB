## D743 Service discovery in `eggdb_coordination` — Decided by the code-only session (2026-11-12, D355); roadmap M15, watches and service discovery (D54, D247)

- `eggdb_coordination` gains a service registry: the `services` table (service, instance, address, metadata,
  registration and expiry times) and `register`, `heartbeat`, `deregister`, `discover` and `expire_services`, in
  PL/pgSQL beside the leases and flags of D247. Instances stay live while their time to live is renewed, as etcd's
  leases and Consul's TTL checks do, by the database clock.
- Watching is by notification: every registration of a new instance, deregistration and expiry sends
  `pg_notify('eggdb_services', ...)` with the service, instance and event, so a client that runs
  `LISTEN eggdb_services` hears changes as they commit; ZooKeeper clients have the watches of D742.
- Expiry happens when `expire_services()` runs (a pg_cron job every minute, say) or when a lookup skips expired rows;
  `discover` never returns an expired instance. Databases that installed the extension before keep the earlier
  objects; the registry comes with a new `CREATE EXTENSION`.
