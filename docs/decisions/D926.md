## D926 CalDAV times in the object's own time zones — Decided by the code-only session (2026-11-12, D355); roadmap M16, follow-up of D901 and D925

- A calendar object's `VTIMEZONE` components are read (`ical::zones`): each `STANDARD` and `DAYLIGHT` part's local
  `DTSTART`, `TZOFFSETTO` and yearly `RRULE` (`BYMONTH` with an ordinal `BYDAY` such as `-1SU` or `2SU`, the form time
  zone definitions use). The UTC time of a wall-clock time in a zone takes the offset of the latest onset at or before
  it, from this year's and last year's transitions.
- `DTSTART`, `DTEND`, `DUE`, `RDATE`, `EXDATE` and `RECURRENCE-ID` given with a `TZID` the object defines are turned
  into UTC before `time-range` filters (D901) and `expand` (D925) read them; expanded output writes those times in UTC,
  as the `VTIMEZONE` it leaves out can no longer resolve them. A `TZID` the object does not define is read as UTC, as
  before; dates stay dates.
- Recurrences are still stepped in UTC from the converted start, so an instance after a daylight-saving change can sit
  an hour from its wall-clock time; stepping in local time is left with the scheduling work.

**Status:** code written, unbuilt (code-only mode, D355).
