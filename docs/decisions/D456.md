  PostgREST documents: items are `column.operator.value` (with `not.` before the operator), groups
  nest (`or=(a.eq.1,and(b.eq.2,c.eq.3))`, `not.and(...)`), commas inside parentheses (`in.(...)`)
  and double-quoted values don't split items, and a quoted value loses its quotes. Each item goes
  through the same checked identifiers and quoted literals as top-level filters (D235), and groups
  nest at most 16 deep.
