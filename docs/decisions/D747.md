## D747 Reports to `https://` targets and by e-mail — Decided by the code-only session (2026-11-12, D355); roadmap M11, reports sent to `https://` targets and by email through the operator's mail server (D46, D238)

- `eggdb serve`'s report delivery (D238) now also posts to `https://` targets over `eggdb-tls`'s client, checking
  the certificate chain against `EGGDB_REPORT_CA_FILE` (a PEM bundle) or the first system bundle found
  (`/etc/ssl/certs/ca-certificates.crt`, `/etc/pki/tls/certs/ca-bundle.crt`, `/etc/ssl/cert.pem`,
  `/usr/local/etc/openssl/cert.pem`) and the host name, as libpq's `verify-full` does. The allow list
  (`EGGDB_REPORT_TARGETS`) applies to them as to `http://`.
- `mailto:<address>` targets are sent through the operator's mail server only, `EGGDB_SMTP_SERVER`, with ESMTP:
  `EHLO`, `STARTTLS` (then `EHLO` again) when offered, `AUTH PLAIN` from `EGGDB_SMTP_USER` and
  `EGGDB_SMTP_PASSWORD_FILE` only once the connection is encrypted, `MAIL FROM` (`EGGDB_SMTP_FROM`), `RCPT TO`,
  `DATA` with dot-stuffing and `QUIT`. The message is UTF-8 plain text: the report's summary line and its rows as
  JSON, with "EggDB report: <name>" as the subject. Addresses must be allowed, one by one or by domain
  (`mailto:@example.com`); EggDB never looks up MX records or sends to other servers.
- A delivery that fails stays queued and is retried, as before. Sharing and embedding reports in other applications
  is left.

**Status:** code written, unbuilt (code-only mode, D355).
