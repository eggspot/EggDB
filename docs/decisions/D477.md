## D477 STOMP `/exchange/` destinations, forwarded headers and selectors — Decided by the code-only session (2026-11-11, D355); roadmap M16, D217

- `SEND` to `/exchange/<name>/<routing key>` delivers to every current subscription on
  `/exchange/<name>/<pattern>` whose pattern matches the key with AMQP topic rules (`.`-separated
  words, `*` one word, `#` zero or more; no pattern means `#`), as RabbitMQ's STOMP plugin routes a
  topic exchange. Exchanges are STOMP-only and transient like `/topic/`: they are not the AMQP
  listener's exchanges, and nothing is kept for absent subscribers.
- Topic and exchange messages now carry the sender's own headers (everything but the frame's
  routing and server headers), with `content-type` among them, as STOMP 1.2 asks, instead of
  `content-type` alone. Queue messages keep their body only, as `eggdb-queues` stores them.
- A `SUBSCRIBE` on a topic or exchange may carry `selector`, a JMS-style SQL-92 condition over
  message headers (`=`, `<>`, `<`, `>`, `<=`, `>=`, `AND`, `OR`, `NOT`, `LIKE ... ESCAPE`, `IN`,
  `BETWEEN`, `IS [NOT] NULL`, string, number and boolean literals, numeric comparison when a side is
  a number), as ActiveMQ and Artemis read it; only messages for which it is true (not false or
  unknown) are delivered. Selectors are bounded to 4 KiB and 32 levels of nesting; a selector on a
  `/queue/` subscription is refused, since filtering a shared queue needs the queue store's help.
