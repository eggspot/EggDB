## D376 SQLite's `group_concat` and `total`, and MySQL's `GROUP_CONCAT` — Decided by the code-only session (2026-11-10, D355); roadmap M5

All three are built on aggregates EggDB already has, at parse time (`parser/group_concat.rs`):

- **`group_concat`** (SQLite, `group_concat([DISTINCT] X [, Y] [ORDER BY ...])`) is `string_agg`
  of `CAST(X AS TEXT)` with the separator `Y` (text) or `,`; NULLs are skipped and a group of only
  NULLs gives NULL, as in SQLite. `DISTINCT` takes one argument, as SQLite requires.
- **`GROUP_CONCAT`** (MySQL, `GROUP_CONCAT([DISTINCT] e1 [, e2 ...] [ORDER BY ...] [SEPARATOR
  'sep'])`) concatenates its expressions per row (NULL when any is NULL, so the row is skipped)
  and joins the rows with `sep` or `,`. MySQL cuts the result at `group_concat_max_len` (1024
  bytes by default) with a warning; EggDB does not cut it yet.
- **`total(X)`** (SQLite) is `coalesce(sum(CAST(X AS REAL)), 0.0)`: always a floating-point
  value, 0.0 over no rows, never an integer overflow.
