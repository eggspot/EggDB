## D191 Our own GUI toolkit: a software rasterizer, our own TrueType reader over the system's fonts, frames tested offscreen first — Decided by the build loop (by judgement, 2026-10-31); refines D8 and D15

Part 4.M6e-1 asks for `eggdb gui` on every desktop OS from our own toolkit, with golden-image
tests from an offscreen surface. Claude Fable designed the client (`docs/ux/gui.md`).

- **`eggdb-gui`**, a crate depending only on the platform layer: an RGBA canvas with
  antialiased rectangles, rounded rectangles, circles, rings and lines; text from TrueType fonts
  read by our own reader (the `head`, `hhea`, `maxp`, `hmtx`, `cmap` formats 4 and 12, `loca`
  and `glyf` tables, composite glyphs, `.ttc` collections) and drawn by an exact-area coverage
  rasterizer; a PNG encoder (stored deflate, so no compressor is needed); and the main window
  drawn from the design's geometry and both themes.
- **Fonts are the system's**, never shipped: the platform layer lists each OS's candidates for
  the interface, bold and monospace roles (`eggdb_platform::fonts`), as the design names them.
  PostScript-outline (CFF) fonts are refused for now.
- **Tests without fonts**: a box typeface draws every character as a box of its advance, so the
  frame's regions, colours and the caret's place are checked pixel by pixel on any machine; with
  the system's fonts a test also draws the frame and writes `target/gui/main_light.png` and
  `main_dark.png`. Golden files with pinned fonts wait for a decision on shipping test fonts.

Left: windows and input on macOS (AppKit), Windows (Win32) and Linux (Wayland, X11) behind the
platform layer, the `eggdb gui` command (it needs its place in the command model), hinting,
kerning, right-to-left and complex scripts, CFF outlines, HiDPI scales, the connect screen,
interaction, and the WebAssembly build.
