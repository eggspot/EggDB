## D901 CalDAV `time-range` filters over recurring events — Decided by the code-only session (2026-11-12, D355); roadmap M16, follow-up of D895

- `eggdb_dav::ical` reads what RFC 4791 section 9.9 needs from an iCalendar object (RFC 5545): unfolded content
  lines, every component with its own properties, `DATE` and `DATE-TIME` values, `DURATION`s, and recurrences.
- A `calendar-query` `comp-filter` with a `time-range` child (either bound may be missing) keeps a calendar object
  only when a component of that name has an instance overlapping the range, by RFC 4791's table: `DTSTART` with
  `DTEND` or `DURATION` overlaps when it starts before the range ends and ends after it starts; a `DATE` start alone
  lasts a day; a `DATE-TIME` start alone is an instant; a to-do with only `DUE` is checked against `DUE`; a component
  with neither always matches.
- Recurrences: `RRULE` with `FREQ=DAILY|WEEKLY|MONTHLY|YEARLY`, `INTERVAL`, `COUNT`, `UNTIL` and, for weekly rules,
  `BYDAY` without ordinals, plus `RDATE` dates and less `EXDATE` dates. Instances are expanded from `DTSTART` only up
  to the range's end (at most 100,000). A monthly or yearly rule skips the months without its day (31st, 29
  February), as RFC 5545 says. A rule with parts this reader does not follow (`BYSETPOS`, `BYMONTHDAY`, ordinals in
  `BYDAY`) counts as overlapping, so the client gets the object and filters it itself: a CalDAV client tolerates an
  extra object, never a missing one.
- Times with a `TZID` and floating times are read as UTC; a calendar's own time zone (`VTIMEZONE`) is not applied
  yet, which can move an instance by the zone's offset at the range's edges.
- Left: `expand` and `limit-recurrence-set` in `calendar-data`, `TZID` time zones, scheduling (RFC 6638) and ACLs.

**Status:** code written, unbuilt (code-only mode, D355).
