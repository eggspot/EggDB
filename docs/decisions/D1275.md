## D1275 ST_Collect, ST_Points and geometry properties — Decided by the code-only session (2026-11-18, D355); roadmap M4 geospatial (D204, D1272)

- **What users get.** PostGIS's `ST_Collect(a, b)` and `ST_Collect(geometry[])` (a multipoint, multilinestring or multipolygon when the inputs are all points, lines or polygons, else a collection; NULL items left out; mixed SRIDs refused), `ST_Points` (every vertex as a multipoint), `ST_IsClosed`, `ST_IsCollection`, `ST_Dimension` (0, 1 or 2, the highest in a collection), `ST_NDims` and `ST_CoordDim` (2, as EggDB's geometries are two-dimensional) and `ST_NumPoints` (a line's points, NULL for other types).
- **How.** Scalar functions beside the others in `geo_functions.rs`, on the geometry model's own shapes; the result keeps the inputs' SRID.
- **Left.** `ST_Collect` as an aggregate (EggDB's aggregates are its own fixed set), `ST_IsSimple` and `ST_IsRing`.
- **Proof.** A test collects two points and reads a polygon's dimension, a closed line and a line's point count.

**Status:** code written, unbuilt (code-only mode, D355).
