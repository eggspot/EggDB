## D734 GZIP and ZSTD Parquet pages — Decided by the code-only session (2026-11-12, D355); roadmap M11, Parquet compression (D202)

- Reading: Parquet pages compressed with GZIP (codec 2) are read with `eggdb-content`'s gunzip and pages compressed
  with ZSTD (codec 6) with `eggdb-storage`'s Zstandard decoder, beside the uncompressed and Snappy pages read
  before, within the same 1 GiB page bound.
- Writing: `eggdb_parquet::write_compressed(columns, rows_per_group, Compression)` writes each data page
  uncompressed, as GZIP (D733's DEFLATE writer) or as ZSTD (`eggdb-storage`'s encoder), with the page headers'
  uncompressed and compressed sizes and the column chunks' codec and total sizes filled in. `write` keeps writing
  uncompressed files.
- `eggdb export --table <name> --parquet <out> --parquet-compression none|gzip|zstd` picks it; the default stays
  uncompressed.
