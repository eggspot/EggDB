## D653 A Russian stemmer and the russian text-search configuration — Decided by the code-only session (2026-11-12, D355); roadmap M8, D30, D649

PostgreSQL's `russian` configuration is widely used. It is the first non-Latin script among
EggDB's stemmers.

- `eggdb-search` gets a `russian` module from Snowball's published Russian algorithm.
- `ё` is read as `е`. RV starts after the first vowel, and R2 is computed as usual (R1, then R2).
- Step 1, in RV:
  - a perfective gerund (`в вши вшись` after `а`/`я`, or `ив … ывшись`) ends the step;
  - otherwise a reflexive ending (`ся сь`) goes first, then the first that matches of these: an
    adjective (with a participle before it, again in its two groups), a verb (in its two groups),
    or a noun.

  A group 1 ending counts only when the `а`/`я` before it is in RV too. Among matches the longest
  wins.
- Step 2 removes a final `и` in RV. Step 3 removes `ост`/`ость` in R2.
- Step 4 removes a superlative `ейш`/`ейше`, then halves `нн`, or else drops a final `ь`.
- Snowball's Russian stop words are dropped. `Config::Russian` is named `russian`.
- Test: `красивая` → `красив`, `бегать` → `бега`, `книги` → `книг`, `новейший` → `нов`. Snowball's
  vocabulary file should be checked when the build runs.
