## D481 MQTT shared subscriptions — Decided by the code-only session (2026-11-11, D355); roadmap M16, D215

- `SUBSCRIBE` accepts `$share/<group>/<filter>` (MQTT 5, also from 3.1.1 clients, as most brokers
  do): the group name is non-empty without wildcards, and the inner filter follows the usual rules.
- Each message matching a group's filter goes to one member of the group, chosen round-robin
  across messages; members keep their own granted QoS. A client that also has a plain matching
  subscription gets that copy too, and one message reaches a client once at the highest QoS of
  what it holds.
- Retained messages are not replayed on a shared subscription, as MQTT 5 specifies.
  `UNSUBSCRIBE` takes the full `$share/...` filter.
