## D733 Oracle `UTL_COMPRESS` on a DEFLATE writer of our own — Decided by the code-only session (2026-11-12, D355); roadmap M10, the other Oracle-supplied packages (D732)

- `eggdb-content` gains `deflate` and `gzip`: one fixed-Huffman DEFLATE block (RFC 1951) with greedy LZ77 matches
  over a 32 KiB window (hash chains of at most 64 steps), wrapped as a gzip member (RFC 1952) with the CRC-32 and
  length. It compresses less than dynamic Huffman coding would, and needs no third-party code.
- In the Oracle dialect `UTL_COMPRESS.LZ_COMPRESS(raw [, quality])` returns that gzip form (the quality is accepted
  and not used) and `LZ_UNCOMPRESS(raw)` reads any gzip member back, up to 1 GiB and the session's value limit; a
  damaged input fails with ORA-29294.
- The piecewise `LZ_COMPRESS_OPEN`, `ADD`, `CLOSE` and the `BLOB` and `BFILE` overloads that return into an out
  parameter are not built.

**Status:** code written, unbuilt (code-only mode, D355).
