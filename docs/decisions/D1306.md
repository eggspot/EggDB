## D1306 The thesaurus text search template — Decided by the code-only session (2026-11-24, D355); roadmap M4 synonyms and stop words (D30)

- **What users get.** `CREATE TEXT SEARCH DICTIONARY ... (TEMPLATE = thesaurus, DictList = '...', Dictionary = <subdictionary>)` makes PostgreSQL's thesaurus dictionary: each rule `sample words : substitute words` replaces a phrase in the text as a whole, so `to_tsvector`, `plainto_tsquery` and `phraseto_tsquery` under a configuration that maps it see `supernovae stars` as `'sn'`. As in PostgreSQL, the subdictionary normalizes both the rules and the text, the longest matching sample wins, `?` in a sample stands for any stop word, `*` before a substitute word keeps it as written, and a sample word that is a stop word or unknown to the subdictionary is refused naming its rule. The substitute lexemes take the positions from the phrase's first word on. Rules are listed in `DictList` (one per line or comma-separated, `#` starts a comment), as EggDB's other templates list their files' contents (D983, D1212); `DictFile` names a file EggDB does not keep and says so. A dictionary a thesaurus names as its subdictionary cannot be dropped while it does (2BP01), and renaming it renames the thesaurus's reference.
- **How.** `eggdb_search::Thesaurus` keeps the normalized rules and the subdictionary; `Dictionary::Thesaurus` answers single words for `ts_lexize`-style calls with a one-word rule's first lexeme. The `Lexer` trait gained `lexemes(words)`, which by default asks for each word's lexeme in turn; the configuration analyzer overrides it to try the thesaurus at each word before the word's own lexeme, and `TsVector::from_text`, `TsQuery::plain` and `TsQuery::phrase` take their lexemes from it. A thesaurus cannot be another thesaurus's subdictionary, which keeps the lookup from looping.
- **Left.** Thesaurus phrases inside `to_tsquery`'s operators and `websearch_to_tsquery`, and phrase positions in `ts_debug`.
- **Proof.** Search tests match the longest sample, a `?` stop word and a one-word rule, refuse a stop-word sample and a rule without `:`, and replace phrases in a vector and a query; catalog tests resolve a subdictionary and refuse a nested thesaurus and a missing `Dictionary`; an SQL test builds a configuration with a thesaurus, checks `to_tsvector` and `plainto_tsquery`, refuses dropping its subdictionary and a stop-word sample, and follows a rename.
