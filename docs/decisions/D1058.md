## D1058 A benchmark against RabbitMQ — Decided by the code-only session (2026-11-13, D355); roadmap M16 (D33, D214)

- `eggdb-broker-compare rabbitmq` (D1056's crate) speaks AMQP 0-9-1 with a client of its own: the handshake with PLAIN,
  one channel, a durable queue, publisher confirms, `basic.qos` and `basic.consume`. The same client measures EggDB's
  `--amqp-listen` and RabbitMQ.
- **Publish:** 200 batches of 100 persistent messages of 100 bytes, each batch waiting for its confirmations, which is
  how a producer that must not lose messages runs; **consume:** a prefetch of 1,000, acknowledging every hundredth
  delivery with `multiple`. Both report messages per second, p50 and p99 per batch, and EggDB's ratio.
- CI's `broker-compare` job adds RabbitMQ 3.13 as a service and runs the comparison after Kafka's; reported, not gated.
- Learned from the AMQP 0-9-1 specification and RabbitMQ's documentation of publisher confirms; no code was read.
