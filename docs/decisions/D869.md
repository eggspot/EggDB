## D869 CDC webhooks over HTTPS — Decided by the code-only session (2026-11-12, D355); follow-up of D868 and D210

- `eggdb serve --cdc-webhook https://...` now posts change events over TLS. The HTTPS posting D868 gave the OTLP
  exporter became one `WebTarget` (an `http://` or `https://` URL) in the report-delivery module, used by the
  exporter and the webhook alike, each with its own trust variable: `EGGDB_CDC_CA_FILE` for the webhook, falling back
  to the system's trusted roots.
- A webhook that fails certificate verification is treated like one that is down: the event stays in the outbox and
  is retried, so delivery stays at-least-once and in commit order.
