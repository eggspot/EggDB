## D718 The `romanian` text search configuration — Decided by the code-only session (2026-11-12, D355); roadmap M12, stemmers for every major language (D30, D195)

- `to_tsvector('romanian', ...)` and the `to_tsquery` family take a `romanian` configuration: lower case words
  stemmed by Snowball's Romanian algorithm, written from its published description like the other stemmers (D649
  onward), with no third-party code.
- The stemmer reads the comma-below letters `ș` and `ț` as the cedilla letters `ş` and `ţ` its suffix lists use,
  so both spellings stem alike; its stems keep the cedilla forms.
- No stop words are dropped: PostgreSQL ships no Romanian stop word list, so its `romanian` configuration drops none
  either.
- Turkish, the other Snowball languages PostgreSQL ships (Arabic, Armenian, Basque, Catalan, Greek, Hindi, Irish,
  Lithuanian, Nepali, Serbian, Tamil, Yiddish) and word segmentation are still left.
