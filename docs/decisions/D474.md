## D474 memcached's meta flags and `stats` counts — Decided by the code-only session (2026-11-11, D355); roadmap M7, D194

- The meta commands move to `eggdb-memcached`'s `meta` module and take memcached's flags:
  - every command: `b` (the key is base64), `k` (echo the key, with `b` when encoded), `O<token>`
    (echo an opaque) and `q` (quiet: `EN` of `mg` and `HD` of `ms`/`md`/`ma` are not sent);
  - `mg`: `v`, `f`, `c`, `t`, `s`, `h` and `l` (reported as `h0`/`l0`, since EggDB keeps no
    access history), and `T<ttl>` to touch while reading;
  - `ms`: `F<flags>`, `T<ttl>`, `C<cas>` (compare and swap), `M<mode>` (`S`et, `E` add, `A`ppend,
    `P`repend, `R`eplace), `c` (return the new CAS), with `NS`/`EX`/`NF` answers, and the data
    block must end in `\r\n`;
  - `md`: `C<cas>` (delete only that version, `EX` otherwise);
  - `ma` (new): `D<delta>`, `M<mode>` (`I`/`+` increment, `D`/`-` decrement), `N<ttl>` with
    `J<initial>` (create a missing counter), `C<cas>`, `T<ttl>`, and `t`/`c`/`v` in the answer;
  - `me` (new): memcached's debug line `ME <key> exp= la= cas= fetch= cls= size=`.
- `stats` counts per server since it started: uptime, connections (current and total) and
  memcached's `cmd_*`, `*_hits`/`*_misses` and `cas_badval` counters for the text and meta
  commands; `stats reset` zeroes them, `stats settings` lists the item size limit, and
  `stats items|slabs|sizes|conns` answer an empty `END`. `curr_items` and `bytes` are not
  reported, as counting them means a scan of every item.
- Not written: `mg` `N`/`R` (vivify and recache with win/stale flags), `I` invalidation, the
  binary protocol's counts, and LRU eviction under a memory limit (EggDB's items stay until they
  expire or are deleted).

**Status:** code written, unbuilt (code-only mode, D355).
