## D882 pgRouting's TSP and graph analysis — Decided by the code-only session (2026-11-12, D355); follow-up of D782 and D831

- `CREATE EXTENSION pgrouting` adds `pgr_TSP(matrix_cell_sql, start_id, end_id)` and `pgr_TSPeuclidean(coordinates_sql,
  start_id, end_id)`, which return pgRouting's `(seq, node, cost, agg_cost)` rows of a closed tour that starts at
  `start_id` (the smallest vertex when 0), visits `end_id` last when it is not 0, and ends back at the start.
  `pgr_TSP` reads `start_vid, end_vid, agg_cost` cells (a missing direction takes the other's cost, as pgRouting does;
  a pair with no cost either way is an error); `pgr_TSPeuclidean` reads `id, x, y` and uses straight-line distances.
- The tour comes from EggDB's own code in `routing.rs`: nearest neighbour from the start, then 2-opt moves (counting
  the reversed segment's cost for asymmetric matrices) until none shortens it, at most 1,000 passes. pgRouting uses
  simulated annealing; both are heuristics, so a tour may differ from pgRouting's while being as short or nearly so.
  At most 2,000 vertices.
- `pgr_analyzeGraph(edge_table, tolerance, the_geom, id, source, target, rows_where)` fills the vertices table's
  `cnt`, `ein`, `eout` and `chk` (1 for a dead end within `tolerance` of an edge it is not on, a likely gap), reports
  pgRouting's notices (isolated segments, dead ends, potential gaps, intersections without a shared vertex, ring
  geometries) and returns `OK`, or `FAIL` when `pgr_createTopology` has not made the vertices table.
- Left: `pgr_nodeNetwork` (it needs `ST_LineLocatePoint`, `ST_LineSubstring` and line intersections), `pgr_analyzeOneWay`,
  and pgRouting's other families (flows, spanning trees, `pgr_withPoints`, `pgr_trsp`).
