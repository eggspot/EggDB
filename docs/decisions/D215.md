## D215 MQTT in our own eggdb-mqtt: an in-process broker with retained messages in EggDB — Decided by the build loop (by judgement, 2026-10-31); refines D33 and D57

Part 4.M16-3 asks for MQTT 3.1.1 and 5 clients.

- **Written from the OASIS MQTT 3.1.1 and 5.0 specifications**: `CONNECT` with username and
  password (refused with return code 5, or reason 0x87 in version 5), `PUBLISH` at QoS 0, 1 and 2
  (the receiver side of the QoS 2 exchange, deduplicated by packet id), `SUBSCRIBE` and
  `UNSUBSCRIBE` with `+` and `#` filters (and `$` topics kept out of wildcards), will messages
  sent when a client goes away without `DISCONNECT`, keep-alive enforced at one and a half times
  the interval, and `PINGREQ`. Version 5 packets are read with their property sections skipped
  and answered with empty ones.
- **Retained messages are durable** in the collection `mqtt0`; live subscriptions are in the
  broker's memory, and deliveries to subscribers are at most QoS 1 without redelivery.
- **Not the durable queues of D214**: MQTT is publish and subscribe without queues unless a
  session persists, which is the next slice (persistent sessions would keep each client's
  messages in `eggdb-queues`).

Left: persistent sessions (clean session false, session expiry), QoS 1 and 2 redelivery to
subscribers, version 5 features (topic aliases, shared subscriptions, message expiry, reason
strings), TLS and WebSockets, and a benchmark against Mosquitto.
