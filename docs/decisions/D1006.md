## D1006 An end-to-end retrieval benchmark — Decided by the code-only session (2026-11-13, D355); roadmap M12 (D7, D205, D740)

- `eggdb-bench` gains `retrieval.hybrid_recall`: 2,000 synthetic documents of 12 words from a 400-word vocabulary are
  remembered through `eggdb_ai.remember` (embedding with the local `eggdb_embed`, full-text vector, insert), then 200
  queries made of four words of a known document run through `eggdb_ai.recall` (reciprocal rank fusion of embedding
  distance and full-text rank).
- It reports `recall_at_10` (the share of queries whose source document is among the ten recalled) and
  `ns_per_query`, measured with the bench harness's calibrated median. Both are reported, not gated, until a
  baseline exists; the Saturday performance pass (D7) watches them.
- Documents and queries come from a fixed-seed generator, so runs on one machine compare. The benchmark measures the
  whole path an application uses (SQL, the extension's functions, exact search), which an ANN index (left on the
  roadmap) will speed up; the recall figure then shows what approximation costs.
