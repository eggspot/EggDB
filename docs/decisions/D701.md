## D701 Sparkplug B payloads decoded — Decided by the code-only session (2026-11-12, D355); roadmap M16, OPC UA, Modbus and Sparkplug ingest (D57)

- `sparkplug_decode(bytea) → jsonb` reads a Sparkplug B payload (Eclipse Tahu's protobuf `Payload`, which edge nodes
  publish on `spBv1.0/...` MQTT topics) with a small protobuf reader: the payload's `timestamp`, `seq`, `uuid` and
  `body`, and each metric's `name`, `alias`, `timestamp`, `historical` and `transient` flags, `datatype` (by its
  Sparkplug name, `Int32`, `Double`, ...) and `value`.
- Values follow the data type: signed integers are read back from their unsigned wire form by width (an `Int32` of
  -5 is -5, not 4294967291), floats and doubles as numbers, booleans and strings as themselves, bytes as hex,
  templates as nested `metrics`, and a metric flagged null as `null`. Data sets and extensions are left as `null`.
- Payloads kept as `bytea` can be decoded with it in SQL; reading Sparkplug messages off EggDB's MQTT broker into the
  metrics store as they arrive, and OPC UA and Modbus, are left.
