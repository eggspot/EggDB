## D1227 Subqueries in MySQL's tabular EXPLAIN — Decided by the code-only session (2026-11-17, D355); roadmap M6c, subqueries in the tabular plans (D327)

- **What users get.** MySQL's traditional `EXPLAIN` of a query with subqueries lists each subquery's tables as rows of their own, as MySQL does: the outer query's rows become `PRIMARY` with `id` 1, each subquery gets the next `id` and `select_type` `SUBQUERY`, or `DEPENDENT SUBQUERY` when it reads the outer row, nested subqueries after the one that holds them. Queries without subqueries keep `SIMPLE`.
- **How.** MySQL's tabular style now plans with D327's subplans like PostgreSQL's and SQLite's forms (D1226); the rows of each select come from the same per-table code as before.
- **Left.** MySQL's semijoin rewrites of `IN` (MySQL often shows `IN (SELECT ...)` as a join with `select_type` `SIMPLE` and `FirstMatch` or `LooseScan` in `Extra`), `MATERIALIZED` and `DERIVED` rows, and the `UPDATE`/`DELETE` forms' subqueries.
- **Proof.** `explain/session_tests.rs`: an `IN` subquery and a correlated scalar subquery listed as `SUBQUERY` and `DEPENDENT SUBQUERY` under a `PRIMARY` row.
