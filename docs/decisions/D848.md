## D848 Restoring roles and privileges — Decided by the code-only session (2026-11-12, D355); roadmap M6a, D118

- `eggdb restore --roles` (`RestoreOptions::roles`) runs a PostgreSQL dump's role and privilege statements —
  `CREATE`, `ALTER` and `DROP ROLE`, `USER` and `GROUP`, `GRANT` and `REVOKE`, as `pg_dumpall` and `pg_dump` write
  them — through the session like any other DDL, now that stored roles, memberships and grants exist (D183, D827).
- Without it they are passed over as before, so restoring a dump that names roles the target lacks still works.
  With it, a statement EggDB cannot run (an option it does not keep, say) fails like any other, and `--keep-going`
  lists it and goes on.
- Left: `pg_dump --clean`'s drops of objects that do not exist yet, several databases in one `pg_dumpall` file,
  large objects, and dumps in other encodings.
