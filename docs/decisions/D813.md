## D813 MySQL's statement digest summary — Decided by the code-only session (2026-11-12, D355); roadmap M6c, D25 and D286

- In the MySQL dialect, `performance_schema.events_statements_summary_by_digest` reads the statement statistics
  `pg_stat_statements` and `sys.dm_exec_query_stats` read (D286, D489): `schema_name`, `digest` (the 64-bit statement
  id in hex), `digest_text` (the normalized text, with `$n` placeholders rather than MySQL's `?`), `count_star`, the
  `*_timer_wait` times in picoseconds as MySQL keeps them, and the rows in `sum_rows_sent` for a `SELECT` or in
  `sum_rows_affected` otherwise. `first_seen` and `last_seen` are null, since the statistics keep no times of day.
- MySQL's catalog views now each name their schema, so `information_schema` and `performance_schema` views resolve
  only under their own schema. The rest of `performance_schema` and turning `@@performance_schema` on are left.
