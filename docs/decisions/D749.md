## D749 Data profiling and master data in `eggdb_catalog` — Decided by the code-only session (2026-11-12, D355); roadmap M6c, data profiling (D42, D51) and master data: entity resolution, match and merge, golden records (D51)

- Profiling: `eggdb_catalog.profile(schema, table [, top_n])` reads each column once for its row, null and distinct
  counts and average text length, takes its smallest and largest values in the column's own order (as text when the
  type has no order), and its `top_n` most common values with their counts, and upserts them into
  `eggdb_catalog.profiles`. Quality rules (D244) check what profiles describe.
- Entity resolution: `match_rules` (entity, column, method, weight) with `exact`, `levenshtein` (normalized by the
  longer text), `similarity` (trigram) and `soundex` comparisons from D470's fuzzy functions;
  `resolve_entities(entity, schema, table, key, threshold)` scores every pair of records (a self-join, so it suits
  tables up to the tens of thousands of rows), links those at or above the threshold and gives each connected group
  the smallest member key as its cluster, in `entity_members`.
- Golden records: `golden_records(entity, schema, table, key, target [, survivorship])` creates `target` with one row
  per cluster, its `golden_key`, how many source records it merges, and each column chosen by its survivorship rule
  (`first` non-null by key, `last`, `longest`, `max`, `min`).
- Blocking keys for large tables, manual merge and split decisions, and stewardship workflows are left.
