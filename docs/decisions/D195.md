## D195 Full-text search: our own analyzers, vectors, queries and ranking in eggdb-search, PostgreSQL's text-search SQL first — Decided by the build loop (by judgement, 2026-10-31); refines D2 and D30

Part 4.M8-1 asks for indexed text search with ranking in every dialect's syntax, analyzers per
language, inverted indexes and BM25.

- **`eggdb-search`**, a crate with no dependencies that every query language and protocol uses:
  analyzers (`Config::English`, `Config::Simple`), the English stemmer written from the
  published Snowball English ("Porter2") algorithm description, an English stop-word list of our
  own, text-search vectors (lexemes with positions) and queries (`&`, `|`, `!`, `<->`, `<N>`,
  prefixes, `plainto`, `phraseto` and `websearch` forms) in PostgreSQL's text forms, matching
  with phrase positions, and ranking (`ts_rank` by term frequency, and Okapi BM25).
- **PostgreSQL's SQL**: `to_tsvector`, `to_tsquery`, `plainto_tsquery`, `phraseto_tsquery`,
  `websearch_to_tsquery`, `ts_rank`, `ts_rank_cd` and the `@@` operator, with the `english` and
  `simple` configurations; `text @@ text` reads its sides as PostgreSQL does. Vectors and queries
  are carried as their text forms for now, so `pg_typeof` says `text`; `ts_rank`'s values are
  our own and order plain term queries as PostgreSQL's do.
- **No index yet**: `@@` evaluates row by row. An inverted index on the engine (a GIN-like
  index type) comes next, with MySQL's `MATCH ... AGAINST`, SQL Server's `CONTAINS`/`FREETEXT`
  and SQLite's FTS5 tables on the same crate.

Left: `tsvector`/`tsquery` as types of their own, weights (`setweight`, `A`-`D`), `ts_headline`,
other languages' stemmers, the inverted index and BM25 in SQL, the other dialects' syntax, and
the benchmark against SQLite FTS5.
