## D178 Table statistics from ANALYZE, and approx_count_distinct by HyperLogLog — Decided by the build loop (by judgement, 2026-10-31); refines D40 and D109

Part 4.M5-1 asks for a cost-based optimizer. Its parts that stand alone come first:

- **`ANALYZE`** in every dialect's form counts each named table's rows, or every table's. It
  keeps the count in a catalog record (catalog prefix `0x10`, the table's name; record version
  1 and the count, 8 bytes little-endian), which `pg_class.reltuples` shows and which moves and
  goes with its table. MySQL's form answers with MySQL's result rows.
- **`approx_count_distinct`** is an aggregate in every dialect: a HyperLogLog sketch of 2^12
  one-byte registers over a 64-bit hash (FNV-1a of the value's order-preserving key encoding,
  then the SplitMix64 finalizer), with linear counting for small counts. The tests hold it within
  5% from 0 to 300,000 distinct values.

Left for the optimizer itself: column statistics with most-common values and histograms in
`pg_stats`, ordering inner joins by estimated cost (the executor runs joins in the order
written today, and reordering needs the column slots renumbered), adaptive re-planning, and
`TABLESAMPLE`.
