## D674 A Hungarian stemmer and the hungarian text-search configuration — Decided by the code-only session (2026-11-12, D355); roadmap M8, stemmers for every major language (D30)

- `eggdb-search/src/hungarian.rs` follows the published Snowball description of the Hungarian stemmer. R1 starts
  after the first consonant of a word that begins with a vowel, counting the digraphs `cs dz dzs gy ly ny sz ty zs`
  as one consonant, and after the first vowel of a word that begins with a consonant.
- In R1, nine steps run in turn, each removing the longest of its suffixes: the instrumental `-al -el` and the factive
  `-á -é` only after a double consonant (`bb`, `ccs`, `ssz`, ...), which is then undoubled; the frequent cases
  (`-ban`, `-nak`, `-tól`, `-ként`, `-n`, `-t`, ...), after which a final `á` or `é` becomes `a` or `e`; the special
  cases `-án`, `-ánként` and `-én`; `-astul`, `-estül` and their kin; the owned `-ké` forms; the singular and plural
  owner suffixes; and the plural `-k` forms, where an `á` or `é` before the suffix comes back as `a` or `e`.
- The stop word list is Snowball's Hungarian list.
- `to_tsvector('hungarian', ...)` and the other text-search functions accept the configuration name, as PostgreSQL's
  `hungarian` configuration does.
