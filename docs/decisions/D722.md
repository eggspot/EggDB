## D722 NATS JetStream: streams and durable consumers in the database — Decided by the code-only session (2026-11-12, D355); roadmap M16, NATS JetStream (D33, D216)

- `eggdb serve --nats-listen` now serves JetStream (`serve_with_jetstream`) on the server's database under its own
  `nats` collections prefix, and `INFO` says `"jetstream":true`. Requests are JetStream's JSON API on
  `$JS.API.>` subjects, answered on the request's reply subject, written from NATS's public JetStream API
  documentation:
  - `INFO`; `STREAM.CREATE`, `UPDATE`, `INFO`, `DELETE`, `PURGE`, `NAMES`, `LIST`, `MSG.GET` (by `seq` or
    `last_by_subj`) and `MSG.DELETE`;
  - `CONSUMER.CREATE` (both the plain and the named forms), `CONSUMER.DURABLE.CREATE`, `INFO`, `DELETE`, `NAMES`,
    `LIST` and `MSG.NEXT`.
- Streams take `subjects` (no two streams may overlap), `limits`, `interest` or `workqueue` retention, `max_msgs`,
  `max_bytes`, `max_age`, `max_msg_size`, `discard` `old` or `new`, and `duplicate_window`. A message published to a
  stream's subject is stored with its headers and time, still reaches core subscribers, and a publish with a reply
  subject gets the `PubAck` (`{"stream":..., "seq":...}`), with `Nats-Msg-Id` deduplication and
  `Nats-Expected-Stream`. `storage: memory` is accepted and kept on disk like `file`.
- Consumers take `deliver_policy` (`all`, `last`, `new`, `by_start_sequence`), `ack_policy` (`explicit`, `none`,
  `all`), `ack_wait`, `max_deliver`, `max_ack_pending` and `filter_subject`. Pull consumers answer `MSG.NEXT` with up
  to `batch` messages; with `no_wait` and nothing to send they answer a `404 No Messages` status; otherwise the pull
  waits until `expires` (at most an hour) and is served as messages arrive. Push consumers (`deliver_subject`)
  receive new messages as they are stored. Each delivery's reply subject is
  `$JS.ACK.<stream>.<consumer>.<deliveries>.<stream seq>.<consumer seq>.<time>.<pending>`, which takes `+ACK`,
  `-NAK` (with an optional `delay`), `+WPI`, `+TERM` and `+NXT`. Unacknowledged messages are delivered again after
  `ack_wait`, on the consumer's next pull or the stream's next store; acknowledged messages leave a `workqueue`
  stream.
- Errors use JetStream's `{"error":{"code", "err_code", "description"}}` shape with its error numbers (stream not
  found 10059, consumer not found 10014, name in use 10058, no message 10037, subjects overlap 10065, bad request
  10003).
- Left out: ephemeral consumers are kept until deleted (JetStream removes them when idle), no idle heartbeats or
  flow control for push consumers, no redelivery timer of its own (redelivery happens on the next pull, store or
  acknowledgement), no key-value or object stores, mirrors, sources, or multiple filter subjects, and the JetStream
  domain form of the API and acknowledgement subjects.
