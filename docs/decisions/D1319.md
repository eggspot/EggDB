## D1319 Per-role limits for WebAssembly functions — Decided by the code-only session (2026-11-25, D355); roadmap M5 `LANGUAGE wasm` (D793, D1313)

- **What users get.** `ALTER ROLE r SET eggdb.wasm_fuel = n` (instructions per call), `eggdb.wasm_memory_pages = n` (64 KiB pages a module's memory may grow to) and `eggdb.wasm_call_depth = n` cap what the role's `LANGUAGE wasm` calls may use. A session may lower its own limits with `SET` of the same names, never raise them above its role's ceiling or EggDB's defaults (10 million instructions, 256 pages, depth 512); a call past its fuel traps with 38000 as before.
- **How.** At login the role's settings (D1313) give the session a ceiling; every wasm call takes the smallest of the default, the ceiling and the session's setting for each limit.
- **Left.** Host functions with effects behind permissions, and changing a logged-in session's ceiling when its role's settings change.
- **Proof.** A unit test lowers fuel through a ceiling, keeps a session from raising it, lowers call depth through the session, and ignores a value that is not a number; a session test traps the set-returning module for a role whose fuel ceiling is 2 while a superuser's call finishes.
