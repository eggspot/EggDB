## D539 AMQP 1.0 idle time-out enforced — Decided by the code-only session (2026-11-11, D355); roadmap M16

The AMQP 1.0 listener now announces its own `idle-time-out` (120 s) in its `open` and closes a
connection that sends nothing (no frame, not even an empty heartbeat frame) for longer, with
`amqp:resource-limit-exceeded` and the description `local-idle-timeout expired`, as the
specification's section 2.4.5 lets a peer do. Clients keep such a connection alive by sending
empty frames at half the announced time, which every AMQP 1.0 client library does. The empty
frames EggDB sends to keep the client's own time-out satisfied (D352) are unchanged, so this item
is now whole.
