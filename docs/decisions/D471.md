## D471 Elasticsearch aggregations: sub-aggregations, bucket and stats kinds — Decided by the code-only session (2026-11-11, D355); roadmap M8, D196

- Aggregations move to `eggdb-esrest`'s `aggregations` module and nest: every bucket writes
  `doc_count` and its own `aggs`/`aggregations`, up to 16 levels deep.
- Bucket kinds: `terms` (`size`, `min_doc_count`, `order` by `_count` or `_key`, and a real
  `sum_other_doc_count`), `histogram` (`interval`, `offset`, `min_doc_count`, empty buckets filled
  between the first and last), `date_histogram` (`fixed_interval` in ms/s/m/h/d,
  `calendar_interval` minute to year with calendar months, quarters and years, and the old
  `interval`; numbers as epoch milliseconds or ISO-8601 dates with offsets; `key_as_string` in
  UTC), `range` (`from` inclusive, `to` exclusive, `key`, `keyed`), `filter`, `filters` (named or
  listed) and `missing`. At most 65,536 buckets per aggregation.
- Metric kinds: `avg`, `sum`, `min`, `max`, `value_count` (every non-null value, strings too),
  `cardinality` (exact distinct count), `stats`, `extended_stats` (population and sampling
  variance and deviation, ±2σ bounds) and `percentiles` (`percents`, default 1, 5, 25, 50, 75, 95,
  99; exact values with linear interpolation, where Elasticsearch approximates with t-digest).
- Not written: `date_range`, `ip_range`, `geo_*`, `composite`, `significant_terms`, `top_hits`,
  `global`, `nested`/`reverse_nested` (no `nested` mapping yet), pipeline aggregations and
  `extended_bounds`.

**Status:** code written, unbuilt (code-only mode, D355).
