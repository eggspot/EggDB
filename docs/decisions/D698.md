## D698 DICOM files read as DICOM's JSON model — Decided by the code-only session (2026-11-12, D355); roadmap M16, ISO 20022 and DICOM (D50)

- `dicom_parse(bytea) → jsonb` reads a DICOM Part 10 file (the 128-byte preamble and `DICM`, or a bare data set) into
  DICOM's JSON model (PS3.18 annex F), the shape DICOMweb answers with: one member per element keyed by its tag in
  hex (`"00100010"`), holding its `vr` and its `Value` array.
- The file meta group is read as explicit VR little endian and its transfer syntax picks the data set's encoding:
  implicit VR little endian, explicit VR big endian, or explicit VR little endian (the uncompressed one and every
  compressed one, whose pixel data is encapsulated). The deflated transfer syntax is refused.
- Values: strings split on `\` (dates, times, codes, UIDs, text), person names as `{"Alphabetic": ...}`, `IS` and `DS`
  as numbers, the binary numbers (`US`, `SS`, `UL`, `SL`, `UV`, `SV`, `FL`, `FD`) and attribute tags (`AT`), and
  sequences as nested data sets, defined or undefined length, including `UN` sequences of undefined length. Bulk
  binary values (`OB`, `OW`, `OF`, `OD`, `OL`, `OV`, `UN` and pixel data, encapsulated or not) keep their `vr` and no
  value, so a large image is skipped, not copied.
- With implicit VR, a small dictionary of 30 common tags (patient, study, series and image attributes) gives their
  VRs; other elements are `UN` without a value, and group lengths are `UL`.
- Limits: sequences nest at most 16 deep and a file holds at most 100,000 elements.
- Left: ISO 20022 messages and a full DICOM data dictionary.
