## D1212 The ispell text search template — Decided by the code-only session (2026-11-17, D355); roadmap M8, synonyms, stop words and text search configurations (D30, D983)

- **What users get.** `CREATE TEXT SEARCH DICTIONARY ... (TEMPLATE = ispell, DictList = '...', AffixList = '...')` turns a form of a listed word into its base word (`books` into `book`, `unbooks` into `book` through a cross product), with `StopWords` or `StopWordList`; a word the dictionary does not know goes to the next dictionary of the mapping, as in PostgreSQL.
- **How.** A new `eggdb_search::Ispell` reads the Hunspell format PostgreSQL's `ispell` template also reads: the word list (`word/FLAGS`, a leading count line skipped) and the affix file's `FLAG` (`long`, `num`), `PFX` and `SFX` header and rule lines, with `0` for nothing, `/flags` after an affix dropped, and conditions of characters, `.`, `[...]` and `[^...]`. A word is reduced by trying it as listed, each suffix rule (and, for cross-product rules, each cross-product prefix rule on the result), then each prefix rule, keeping a base whose flags hold the rule's flag. As with `Synonyms` (D983), EggDB keeps no `tsearch_data` files: `DictFile` and `AffFile` give an error naming the `DictList` and `AffixList` options that take the text inline.
- **Left.** The original ispell affix format (`suffixes` / `flag *S:` blocks), compound words (`COMPOUNDFLAG`), `AF` flag aliases, and more than one lexeme per word: `Lexer::lexeme` returns one lexeme, so a word with several base words gives the first, where PostgreSQL gives all of them. The `thesaurus` template.
- **Proof.** Unit tests in `eggdb-search/src/ispell.rs` (suffixes, prefixes, cross products, long and numeric flags, bad lines) and in `text_search_catalog.rs` (listed words and affixes, stop words, the error for files).
