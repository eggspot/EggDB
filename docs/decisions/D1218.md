## D1218 SUBSET union variables in MATCH_RECOGNIZE — Decided by the code-only session (2026-11-17, D355); roadmap M16, `MATCH_RECOGNIZE` for complex event processing (D55, D1067, D1217)

- **What users get.** `SUBSET name = (var, ...)` after `PATTERN`: `MEASURES` reads the union variable like a pattern variable, over the rows any of its members matched (`COUNT(MOVE.*)`, `MIN(MOVE.price)`, `FIRST(MOVE.at)`, `LAST(MOVE.price)`), with SQL:2016's errors for a union variable that repeats a pattern variable's name or lists a name the pattern does not have.
- **How.** The clause's rewrite already picks a variable's rows by its classifier; a union variable picks them with `IN` over its members' names instead of `=`. `DEFINE` conditions keep reading only the primary variables.
- **Left.** Union variables in `DEFINE`, and in `PREV` and `NEXT`.
- **Proof.** `streams.rs`: a `SUBSET` of the down and up rows counted and its lowest price read; a `SUBSET` naming an unknown variable refused.
