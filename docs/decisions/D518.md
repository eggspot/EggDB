## D518 Elasticsearch `_geo_distance` sort — Decided by the code-only session (2026-11-11, D355); roadmap M8

`"sort": [{"_geo_distance": {"<field>": <origin or origins>, "order": ..., "unit": ..., "mode":
..., "distance_type": "arc" | "plane"}}]` orders hits by their distance from the origin.

- **Points:** documents and origins as Elasticsearch writes `geo_point`: `{"lat": .., "lon": ..}`,
  `[lon, lat]`, `"lat,lon"`, or arrays of them; geohashes and WKT are not read yet. Out-of-range
  latitudes and longitudes are ignored.
- **Distance:** the haversine great-circle distance on a sphere of Elasticsearch's mean earth
  radius (6,371,008.7714 m) for `arc`, the equirectangular approximation for `plane`, in the
  `unit` asked for (`m` by default; `km`, `mi`, `yd`, `ft`, `in`, `cm`, `mm`, `nmi`). With several
  origins each document point takes its nearest origin; with several document points `mode`
  chooses (min ascending and max descending by default).
- **Missing:** a document without the field sorts as `Infinity`, shown as the string
  `"Infinity"` in its `sort` values, as Elasticsearch shows it.
- **Left out:** `_script` sorts (they wait for Painless beyond D331's subset), `nested` sorts,
  and refusing sorts on unmapped fields once mappings apply.

**Status:** code written, unbuilt (code-only mode, D355).
