## D1244 pgRouting's pgr_dijkstraNear and pgr_dijkstraNearCost — Decided by the code-only session (2026-11-17, D355); roadmap M12, topology and routing at pgRouting's level (D57, D782)

- **What users get.** `pgr_dijkstraNearCost(edges_sql, start_vid, end_vids [, directed, cap])` gives the `cap` (default 1) cheapest reachable ends from the start, cheapest first, and `pgr_dijkstraNear` the paths to them (`seq`, `path_seq`, `start_vid`, `end_vid`, `node`, `edge`, `cost`, `agg_cost`), as pgRouting's one-to-many forms do: the nearest hospital, depot or charger.
- **How.** SQL functions over the existing `pgr_dijkstraCost` and `pgr_dijkstra` (D782, D865): the costs to every listed end, ordered by cost then end and cut at `cap`, then each chosen path in that order.
- **Left.** pgRouting's many-to-one, many-to-many and combinations forms, and its `global` option.
- **Proof.** The SQL test in `pgrouting.rs`: the two nearest of three ends with their costs, and the path to the nearest.

**Status:** code written, unbuilt (code-only mode, D355).
