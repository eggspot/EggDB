## D485 More of Redis's commands: strings, keys, hashes, lists, sets and sorted sets — Decided by the code-only session (2026-11-11, D355); roadmap M7, D5 and D193

Written from the Redis command reference, each with Redis's argument checks and replies:

- Strings: `GETEX` (`EX`, `PX`, `EXAT`, `PXAT`, `PERSIST`), `SETRANGE` (zero-padded, at most
  512 MiB), `MSETNX`, and `LCS` with `LEN` (`IDX` is refused for now; inputs whose product passes
  64 Mi cells are refused).
- Keys: `TOUCH`, `EXPIRETIME`, `PEXPIRETIME`, `RANDOMKEY`.
- Hashes: `HSTRLEN`, `HINCRBYFLOAT`, `HRANDFIELD` (count, negative counts repeat,
  `WITHVALUES`), `HSCAN` (`MATCH`, `COUNT`, `NOVALUES`).
- Lists: `LPOS` (`RANK`, `COUNT`, `MAXLEN`).
- Sets: `SMISMEMBER`, `SRANDMEMBER`, `SMOVE`, `SINTERCARD` (`LIMIT`), `SINTERSTORE`,
  `SUNIONSTORE`, `SDIFFSTORE`, `SSCAN`.
- Sorted sets: `ZMSCORE`, `ZRANDMEMBER` (`WITHSCORES`), `ZSCAN`, `ZUNION`/`ZINTER`/`ZDIFF`
  (`WEIGHTS`, `AGGREGATE SUM|MIN|MAX`, `WITHSCORES`; plain sets count with score 1) and their
  `...STORE` forms, and `ZINTERCARD` (`LIMIT`).
- Random choices use a per-server xorshift stream seeded with the clock, as Redis's are not
  cryptographic either; counts are capped at 2^20.
- Collection scans page through the collection's sorted members with the cursor as an offset,
  as `SCAN` does for keys.
