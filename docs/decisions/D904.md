## D904 JMAP uploads and `Email/import` — Decided by the code-only session (2026-11-12, D355); roadmap M16, follow-up of D894

- `POST /jmap/upload/<account>/` (RFC 8620 section 6.1) keeps the body as a blob in the mail store (`store::Upload`:
  media type, upload time and bytes, keyed `U<id>`) and answers 201 with `accountId`, `blobId`, `type` and `size`.
  The blob id is `u` and the first 16 bytes of the body's SHA-256 in hex, so uploading the same bytes twice keeps one
  blob. The session now states `maxSizeUpload` as the endpoint's request limit (10 MiB).
- Uploads are kept 24 hours: each upload first removes the ones older than that. RFC 8620 asks for at least one hour
  for a blob nothing references; an imported message keeps its own copy, so removing the upload loses nothing.
- `/jmap/download/` serves an upload with the media type it came with.
- `Email/import` (RFC 8621 section 4.8) creates emails from blobs (an upload, or an email's own blob) in exactly one
  mailbox, with `keywords` as flags and `receivedAt` as the internal date (now when absent), and answers `created`
  with `id`, `blobId`, `threadId` and `size`, and `notCreated` with `blobNotFound` or `invalidProperties` (no mailbox,
  more than one, or one that does not exist).
