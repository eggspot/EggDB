## D757 Synthetic data (eggdb_privacy.synthetic_copy) — Decided by the code-only session (2026-11-12, D355); roadmap M6b, synthetic data, and masked copies for non-production use (D52)

- `eggdb_privacy.synthetic_copy(schema, table, target, rows [, minimum_group])` creates `target` with the source's
  columns and fills it with `rows` made-up rows in one `INSERT ... SELECT` over `generate_series`.
- Each column is drawn on its own from what the source shows, and keeps its share of NULLs:
  - numbers from a normal distribution (Box–Muller over `random()`) with the column's mean and sample standard
    deviation, clipped to its smallest and largest values, and rounded for integer types;
  - dates and timestamps evenly between their first and last value;
  - every other type from its distinct values that occur at least `minimum_group` times (5 by default), at their
    observed frequencies, among the 100 most common; values rarer than that never appear, so a unique value (a name,
    an e-mail address) cannot leak through;
  - arrays and user-defined types are left NULL.
- Columns are independent, so the copy keeps each column's distribution but not correlations between columns, and
  a numeric column's extremes are the source's own. A model that keeps joint distributions (a Bayesian network or
  copula), differential privacy for the statistics it learns, and foreign keys between synthetic tables are left.

**Status:** code written, unbuilt (code-only mode, D355).
