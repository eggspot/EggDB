## D1276 ST_IsSimple and ST_IsRing — Decided by the code-only session (2026-11-18, D355); roadmap M4 geospatial (D204, D1275)

- **What users get.** PostGIS's `ST_IsSimple(geom)`: false for a line that crosses or touches itself (a closed line's meeting ends allowed), for a multiline whose lines meet anywhere but at ends of both, and for a multipoint with a repeated point; points and polygons are simple. `ST_IsRing(line)`: closed and simple; other types are refused with PostGIS's message.
- **How.** `eggdb_geo::is_simple` checks every pair of a line's segments (zero-length ones left out): neighbours may share only their common point, a closed line's first and last segments only the ring's start, and the others must not meet; lines of a multiline may meet only where the meeting point is an end of both. The pairwise check is quadratic in the line's segments, as GEOS's simple check is without an index.
- **Left.** A sweep-line check for very long lines, and polygon validity's own self-intersection rules (`ST_IsValid` keeps its own checks).
- **Proof.** A unit test checks a simple line, a bow tie, a closed triangle, a line folding back on itself, a repeated multipoint, multilines meeting at ends and crossing in the middle.

**Status:** code written, unbuilt (code-only mode, D355).
