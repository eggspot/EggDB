## D981 The `turkish` text search configuration — Decided by the code-only session (2026-11-13, D355); roadmap M8, stemmers for every major language (D30, D195)

- `to_tsvector('turkish', ...)` and the `to_tsquery` family take a `turkish` configuration: lower case words, the
  Turkish stop words PostgreSQL's `turkish` configuration drops, and Snowball's Turkish stemmer, written from its
  published description like the other stemmers (D649 onward), with no third-party code.
- The stemmer works backwards from the end of the word: the nominal verb suffixes (person, tense and copula
  endings) first, then, unless a verb plural ended the word, the noun suffixes (case, possessive, plural and the
  relative `ki` chain). Each suffix is checked against vowel harmony and its optional joining letter (`n`, `s`,
  `y` or a high vowel), and a word of one syllable is kept as it is.
- The stem's final `b`, `c`, `d` and `ğ` read as `p`, `ç`, `t` and `k`, as the algorithm prescribes. The reserved
  words `ad` and `soyad` are not changed.
- Lower casing is Unicode's, not Turkish-specific: `I` becomes `i`, and the combining dot that `İ` lower cases to is
  dropped, so `İ` and `i` stem alike.
- Where the published description is ambiguous about which suffixes are removed together, the removal follows the
  suffix chains the algorithm's state machines describe.
