## D955 Oracle's `AFTER STARTUP` and `BEFORE SHUTDOWN` triggers — Decided by the code-only session (2026-11-13, D355); roadmap M5, follow-up of D873 and D907

- `CREATE TRIGGER ... AFTER STARTUP ON DATABASE` and `... BEFORE SHUTDOWN ON DATABASE` are accepted in Oracle's
  dialect, with Oracle's rules: `BEFORE STARTUP`, `AFTER SHUTDOWN` and either event on a schema are refused.
  `ora_sysevent` answers `STARTUP` or `SHUTDOWN` inside them.
- The instance of Oracle's model is `eggdb serve`: it fires the default database's `AFTER STARTUP` triggers once it is
  ready, before it accepts connections, and its `BEFORE SHUTDOWN` triggers after the last connection has ended and
  before it closes the database. An embedded open is not a startup, since every process that opens the file would
  fire them.
- `Session::fire_startup_triggers` and `Session::fire_shutdown_triggers` are public, for other hosts that act as the
  instance. The triggers of one event run in one transaction, committed when they all succeed; an error rolls them
  back, and `eggdb serve` prints it as a warning and goes on starting or stopping, as Oracle writes a failed startup
  or shutdown trigger to its alert log and carries on.
- Databases of a served directory other than the default one open lazily and do not fire them yet.
