## D1040 Online shard moves copy their data over the replication protocol — Decided by the code-only session (2026-11-13, D355); roadmap M15 (D209, D212)

- `MOVE SHARD <n> FROM 'host:port'` (a superuser, on a server that replicates or runs `--cluster`) moves shard `n` of
  the shard map (D1039) to this server, following `ShardMove`'s steps (D212): copy, catch up, cut over.
- **Copy.** The new owner sends a `SHARD_COPY` greeting (shard and map epoch) to the owner's replication listener or
  cluster port, with the usual HMAC login. The owner refuses unless its map has that epoch and it is the primary (the
  batches it keeps are what the catch-up needs). It notes its last batch sequence, then streams the shard's keys in
  pages: each page is one snapshot scan of up to 4,096 keys of the default key space, filtered by `route`, so the
  owner never holds the shard in memory and keeps serving. The new owner writes each page in one transaction.
- **Catch up.** The new owner then asks round by round for the changes after the sequence it has applied; the owner
  answers with the shard's changes from its batches (falling back to the change archive when batches were dropped,
  as replication does) and whether it is caught up. Pages read at different times converge because every change
  after the noted sequence is replayed in order, as D261 argued for `eggdb follow`.
- **Cut over** once a round finds nothing new: the new owner publishes the map with itself as the owner and the epoch
  raised, by `publish_shard_map`'s compare-and-set, so a map changed meanwhile stops the move. Clients routed with the
  old epoch are refused from then on (D212); one more catch-up round takes the writes the owner accepted before the
  new epoch reached it. A shard that keeps changing faster than 1,000 rounds can copy stops with a message.
- Reserved keys never cross: pages and changes skip the engine's keys, and the receiver refuses them.
- Left: deleting the moved keys on the old owner after the cut-over (they are unreachable through the map, and
  `rebalance` simulations and a cleanup job come with the rebalancing item), and copying a shard of a collection
  (collections are not sharded yet).
