## D415 The rest of MySQL's date and time functions and `STR_TO_DATE` — Decided by the code-only session (2026-11-11, D355); roadmap M10

- New functions in the MySQL dialect (`crates/eggdb-sql/src/mysql_date_functions.rs`, parsed in
  `parser/mysql_dates.rs`): `DATE_ADD`, `DATE_SUB`, `ADDDATE`, `SUBDATE` (with `INTERVAL expr unit`
  for every unit MySQL has, and the days form of `ADDDATE`/`SUBDATE`), `TIMESTAMPADD`,
  `TIMESTAMPDIFF`, `STR_TO_DATE`, `UNIX_TIMESTAMP`, `FROM_UNIXTIME` (with and without a format),
  `UTC_DATE`, `UTC_TIME`, `DATEDIFF`, `TO_DAYS`, `TO_SECONDS`, `FROM_DAYS`, `LAST_DAY`, `MAKEDATE`,
  `MAKETIME`, `MONTHNAME`, `DAYNAME`, `TIME_TO_SEC`, `SEC_TO_TIME`, `PERIOD_ADD` and `PERIOD_DIFF`.
- Result types follow the arguments as MySQL's do, where EggDB's types allow it: `DATE_ADD` of a
  `DATE` by a day, week, month, quarter, year or year-month unit is a `DATE`, of another date or
  time type a `DATETIME`, and of a string a string (date only when the string had no clock and the
  unit is a date unit). `STR_TO_DATE` gives a `DATE`, `TIME` or `DATETIME` from what a literal
  format names (date specifiers, clock specifiers or both); a format that is not a literal gives a
  `DATETIME`.
- Compound interval strings (`'1 1:1:1'` for `DAY_SECOND`) are read right-aligned, as MySQL reads
  a value with fewer parts, and the microsecond part is padded to six digits (`'1.5'
  SECOND_MICROSECOND` is 1.5 s). Simple units other than `SECOND` round a fractional amount;
  `SECOND` keeps it to the microsecond. Month arithmetic clamps the day to the month's last.
- `TO_DAYS` counts days from year 0 as MySQL does (`TO_DAYS('2000-01-01')` is 730485).
  `UNIX_TIMESTAMP` reads a date in the session time zone and gives 0 outside 1970 to 3001-01-19,
  as MySQL 8 does; it gives whole seconds (MySQL gives a decimal for a fractional argument).
- Known differences, all because EggDB has no zero dates: `FROM_DAYS` of a day number below 366,
  `STR_TO_DATE` with a missing or zero year, month or day, and `MAKEDATE`'s out-of-range results
  give `NULL` where MySQL gives `'0000-00-00'` or a zero-in-date value. `STR_TO_DATE` doesn't read
  the week specifiers (`%U %u %V %v %X %x %w`) and gives `NULL` for them. `date + INTERVAL n unit`
  as an operator is not parsed yet.
