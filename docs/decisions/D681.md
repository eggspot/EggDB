## D681 InfluxQL tz() — Decided by the code-only session (2026-11-12, D355); roadmap M16, InfluxQL (D220, D521)

- `tz('America/Chicago')` at the end of a `SELECT` names a time zone, read by the SQL engine's `TimeZone::named`
  (IANA regions and aliases, fixed offsets, POSIX rules); an unknown name is refused with InfluxDB's
  "unable to find time zone".
- `GROUP BY time()` buckets then start at local midnights and hours: the translation buckets
  `"time" AT TIME ZONE 'zone'` from a local `1970-01-01T00:00:00` origin and turns the bucket back into an instant
  with `AT TIME ZONE`, so a `1d` bucket is a local day, across daylight saving changes.
- RFC 3339 times are written in the zone with its offset (`2019-08-18T00:00:00-05:00`), as InfluxDB writes them;
  `UTC` keeps the `Z`. `epoch=` times are unchanged, as they are instants.
- `TimeZone::offset_at` is public in `eggdb-sql` so the observability crate can write the offsets.
- Empty buckets made by `fill()` step from the zone's offset at the series' first point; across a daylight saving
  change inside one query, the filled buckets after the change are an hour off the local boundaries.
