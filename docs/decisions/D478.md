## D478 Cypher's GQL label expressions: `&`, `|`, `!`, `%` and parentheses — Decided by the code-only session (2026-11-11, D355); roadmap M13, D351

- Node patterns and `WHERE n:...` take Neo4j 5's label expressions: `:A&B` (both), `:A|B` (either),
  `:!A` (not), `:%` (any label at all), and parentheses, with `!` binding tighter than `&` and `&`
  tighter than `|`, nested at most 32 deep. `:A:B` and `:A|B` keep their existing forms; mixing `:`
  with the new symbols answers Neo4j's "Mixing label expression symbols" error.
- In `WHERE` and other places where `|` may end the expression (list comprehensions), a top-level
  `|` still ends it; inside parentheses it is an or.
- `CREATE` and `MERGE` keep refusing anything but plain `:A:B` labels, as Neo4j does.
- The tokenizer now reads `&` and `!` as symbols. Relationship types keep `:A|B` only; `&`, `!` and
  `%` on relationship types, and the `CALL (a, b) { ... }` scope form and `IN TRANSACTIONS`, are
  not written.

**Status:** code written, unbuilt (code-only mode, D355).
