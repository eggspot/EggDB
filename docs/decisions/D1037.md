## D1037 Raft membership changes by joint consensus, pre-vote and leader leases — Decided by the code-only session (2026-11-13, D355); roadmap M15 (D211)

- **Joint consensus** (the paper's section 6). A configuration is `Membership { voters, outgoing }`; it is carried by
  log entries of kind `EntryKind::Membership` and takes effect on every node as soon as it is in the log, committed or
  not; a truncated configuration entry gives back the one before it, and snapshots carry the configuration as of their
  index. `Node::propose_membership(voters)` (leader only, one change at a time) appends the joint configuration; while
  it is joint, elections and commitment need a majority of both halves; once it commits, the leader appends the new
  configuration alone, and once that commits a leader that is not a voter steps down and never stands again. Nodes
  join with an empty configuration and learn the cluster's from the log, so they cannot start an election of their
  own. The thesis's single-server changes were not chosen: joint consensus handles any change, including replacing
  several voters at once, which moving a cluster between regions needs.
- **Pre-vote** (the thesis's section 9.6). A node whose election timer fires becomes a `PreCandidate` and asks with
  the term it would use; a node grants only when the candidate's log is at least as up to date and it has not heard
  from a leader within the minimum election timeout. Only with a quorum of pre-votes does it raise its term and stand.
  A node cut off by a partition therefore keeps its term and cannot unseat a working leader when it comes back.
  Nodes also ignore a `RequestVote` while they hear from a leader (section 4.2.3), so a vote request cannot raise their
  term either.
- **Leader leases** (section 6.4.1). Every append carries the leader's heartbeat round, echoed in the reply. A leader
  remembers when it sent each recent round and which round each follower last answered; `lease_read_index` returns
  the commit index while a quorum answered a round sent less than `election_ticks - lease_margin_ticks` ticks ago and
  an entry of the leader's term is committed. Followers do not stand before `election_ticks` without a leader, so no
  other leader can exist during the lease, given clock drift below the margin. A read outside the lease goes through
  the log.
- The entry's kind adds one byte to its stored form, and the snapshot record gains its configuration
  (`docs/format.md`); D1036's layout had not shipped.
- Tested: a three-node cluster replaces its leader with two new nodes through the joint configuration and commits
  afterwards; an isolated node keeps its term and the leader keeps its seat; a partitioned leader loses its lease;
  configurations round-trip and need both majorities while joint.
