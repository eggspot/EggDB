## D498 OTLP/HTTP JSON ingest of metrics, logs and traces — Decided by the code-only session (2026-11-11, D355); roadmap M16, D39

- The observability listener takes OpenTelemetry's OTLP over HTTP in its JSON encoding
  (`http/json`) at `/v1/logs`, `/v1/traces` and `/v1/metrics`, answering `200 {}`, or 400 with an
  OTLP-style error, all in one transaction per request.
- Logs become rows of `metrics.logs` (as Loki pushes do, D495): resource and record attributes as
  label columns (dots and other punctuation turned into `_`, so `service.name` is `service_name`),
  `severity`, the body as `line`, and the record time (or its observed time).
- Spans become rows of `metrics.spans` (as Zipkin spans do, D497): hex ids lowercased, parent id,
  name, kind (`SERVER`, `CLIENT`, `PRODUCER`, `CONSUMER`), `service` from `service.name`,
  `duration_us` from start and end, and the span attributes as JSON `tags`.
- Gauge and sum data points become samples of their metric (`http.requests` →
  `metrics.http_requests` with a `value` column), with resource and point attributes as labels, so
  PromQL reads them. Histograms, exponential histograms and summaries are skipped.
- At most a million records and 128 attributes per record. The protobuf encoding (OTLP's
  default, `http/protobuf`) answers 415, and OTLP over gRPC waits for HTTP/2 (the M6a line).
