## D1277 ST_Covers, ST_CoveredBy and ST_ContainsProperly — Decided by the code-only session (2026-11-18, D355); roadmap M4 geospatial (D204, D1276)

- **What users get.** PostGIS's `ST_Covers(a, b)` (no point of `b` outside `a`, the boundary allowed), `ST_CoveredBy(a, b)` (the same the other way) and `ST_ContainsProperly(a, b)` (`b` inside `a` without touching its boundary), which spatial joins and indexes' callers use to avoid `ST_Contains`'s boundary rule.
- **How.** EggDB's containment test already counts boundary points as inside (every vertex covered, every segment's middle covered, no proper crossing of an edge), which is the covering relation, so `ST_Covers` and `ST_CoveredBy` use it as it is. `ST_ContainsProperly` adds that no vertex of `b` lies on an edge of `a` and no edge of `b` meets one.
- **Left.** `ST_Contains`' own rule that `b` may not lie wholly in `a`'s boundary, and `ST_Touches`, `ST_Crosses` and `ST_Overlaps`, which need the interiors' intersection matrix (DE-9IM).
- **Proof.** A unit test tells a point inside a square from one on its edge, and a line inside from one that starts at a corner.

**Status:** code written, unbuilt (code-only mode, D355).
