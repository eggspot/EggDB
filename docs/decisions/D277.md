## D277 The plan view reads `EXPLAIN`'s answer into a bounded tree: `Ctrl/Cmd+E` explains the statement under the caret in the tab's dialect, `Ctrl/Cmd+Shift+E` measures it with `EXPLAIN (ANALYZE, BUFFERS)` inside a rolled-back transaction when it writes, and the nodes are drawn top-down with cost, rows, time, buffers and hot-node borders — Decided by the build loop (EggAgentCode task 20261103-0137, 2026-11-03); roadmap M6e, applies D9, D10, D11, D15 and D256, builds section 5 of `docs/ux/workspace.md`

Before this the toolbar's `Explain` button did nothing, and a plan could only be read as rows
in the grid. Fable's design (`docs/ux/workspace.md` 5) asks for a tree in the results body that
shows where the time goes before anyone reads the numbers.

- **Reading the answer, not a new format.** The engine has no `EXPLAIN (FORMAT JSON)`. It
  answers `EXPLAIN` with PostgreSQL's text form (one `QUERY PLAN` column), SQLite's `EXPLAIN
  QUERY PLAN` rows (`id, parent, notused, detail`) and MySQL's tabular rows. A real PostgreSQL
  server gives the same text form with costs and times. `Plan::from_result` in
  `crates/eggdb-gui/src/plan_view.rs` reads all three by their columns:
  - **The text form.** A child sits on a `->` line, and its parent is the nearest open node
    whose arrow is further left. Any other line under a node is one of the node's extra lines
    (`Filter:`, `Hash Cond:`), except `Buffers:` lines, whose `hit=` and `read=` add up to the
    node's buffers. After the tree, the first line that is not indented starts the footer:
    `Planning Time`, `Execution Time` and the `Planning:` block. The parser reads
    `(cost=a..b rows=n)`, `(actual time=a..b rows=n loops=n)` and `(never executed)`, and a
    number that does not parse, is negative or is not finite counts as missing.
  - **SQLite's rows.** A row hangs under the row whose `id` is its `parent`, and under a
    `QUERY PLAN` root when there is no such row. A single top-level row becomes the root itself.
  - **MySQL's rows.** One row is one table read: `<type> on <table> using <key>`, under a
    `Nested Loop` root when there is more than one.
- **Bounded, never panicking.** A plan has at most 1,000 nodes (`MAX_NODES`), 64 levels
  (`MAX_DEPTH`), 20,000 lines (`MAX_LINES`) and 64 extra lines per node. Above a limit the
  answer is a `PlanError` that `Messages` shows. The text form is still one `Alt+T` away. Sums
  saturate, and slicing only happens at the ASCII markers the parser found.
- **What a node shows.** Row 1 is the operation as the engine names it. Row 2 is
  `cost 0.29..8.31 · rows 1` under `EXPLAIN`, and the node's own time,
  `rows 1,204 · buffers 38` under `ANALYZE`. A node's own time is its total over all loops
  less its children's totals, and only shows when all of those are known. When the engine
  measures no node, the root's total is the statement's `Execution Time`, which is what EggDB's
  own `EXPLAIN ANALYZE` gives today. `buffers` counts the pages the node read from the cache or
  from disk (`hit` plus `read`). Fields the engine does not give are left out, so EggDB's own
  plans, which have no costs, show row 1 and an empty bar.
- **Hot nodes.** A node's share is its own time divided by the root's total under `ANALYZE`
  (falling back to cost when times are missing), else its own cost divided by the root's total
  cost, clamped to 0 to 100 %. From 20 % a node is `warm` (2 px `warning` border, `· 34 %` in
  `warning`), and from 50 % it is `hot` (`error`), as the design decided. Under `ANALYZE`,
  estimated rows that differ from the actual rows by 10× or more add `· est. 10 → 1,204` in
  `warning`. The 4 px bar under row 2 fills to the share. The share is always printed next to
  the colour, and a screen reader hears it as text: `cost 0.29 to 8.31, rows 1, 62 percent,
  hot`.
- **Layout.** The layout is a top-down tidy tree. Each subtree is as wide as its node or its
  children's subtrees with 24 px between them, whichever is wider, and a parent is centred over
  its children. Levels are 56 px apart and nodes are 40 px tall. Node widths are the wider row
  plus 24, from 120 to 320. They are computed when the plan arrives from an estimate of the
  interface face's advance, so the keyboard's scrolling and the drawing agree without a face.
  The real face cuts text with `…` inside the box. The tree is centred when it fits and
  scrolls to keep the current node in view when it does not. Nodes that are not wholly inside
  the results body are left out of the frame until scrolled to, because the canvas has no clip.
- **Running it.** `QueryTab::explain_request` takes the selection's first statement, else the
  statement under the caret. It runs as `EXPLAIN` in the tab's dialect, or `EXPLAIN QUERY PLAN`
  in SQLite's. A statement that already starts with `EXPLAIN` runs as it is.
  `Ctrl/Cmd+Shift+E` runs `EXPLAIN (ANALYZE, BUFFERS)`, which does run the statement. A
  statement that writes (`INSERT`, `UPDATE`, `DELETE`, `MERGE`, `WITH`) runs between `BEGIN` and
  `ROLLBACK`. When the explain fails after `BEGIN`, the worker sends a `ROLLBACK` of its own, so a
  server session is never left inside the transaction. Oracle's and SQL Server's dialects show
  the CLI's `not_supported` text in `Messages`, and so does `ANALYZE` in a dialect other than
  PostgreSQL's, because the engine measures plans only there. An engine error inside the
  `EXPLAIN` gets its position moved back by the prefix's length, so the squiggle sits under the
  user's own text.
- **The tab.** `ResultView::Plan` adds a `Plan` segment after `Messages` (the summary moves
  right to make room). `Results` keeps the previous rows. The summary reads `3 nodes · 0.4 ms`.
  `Ctrl/Cmd+3` focuses the plan (`TabFocus::Plan`), and the plan also takes the grid's place in
  the tab order. `Up`, `Down`, `Left`, `Right` and `Home` walk the tree. `Enter` or `Space`
  selects a node, which fills it with `selection` and highlights in the editor the first
  whole-word mention of the table the node reads, else the whole statement. The engine gives no
  source ranges, so a table's name is the closest mapping. `Esc` clears the selection, and so
  does an edit. `Alt+T` toggles the text form (the `Text` ghost button).
- **Accessibility.** The plan is a `Plan` group (`NODE_PLAN`, 119) with its node count. Its
  nodes start at `NODE_FIRST_PLAN_NODE` (62,000): named by row 1, row 2 spoken as the value,
  focused and selected. The `Plan` segment is `NODE_RESULTS_SEGMENT + 2`. The platform roles
  for a tree and its items arrive with D278.
- **Scripting.** `Workspace::explain_text` and the binary's `--explain <sql>` (with `--png`)
  explain the way the keys do.
