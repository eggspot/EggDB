## D748 Personal-data classification, differential-privacy budgets and masked copies in `eggdb_privacy` — Decided by the code-only session (2026-11-12, D355); roadmap M6b, classification of personal data (D26), differential privacy (D52, D245) and masked copies (D52)

- Classification: `eggdb_privacy.classify(schema, table [, sample_size, threshold])` samples each text column (1,000
  values by default) and tags it with the category most of its values match, when at least the threshold share
  does: `email`, `payment_card` (12 to 19 digits passing the Luhn check), `iban`, `ip_address` (IPv4) or `phone`.
  Columns with no such values are tagged by name: `national_id` (`ssn`, `passport`, `tax_id`, `cccd`, `cmnd`, ...),
  `date_of_birth`, `person_name`, `address` and `sensitive` (`gender`, `religion`, `health`, ...). Results replace
  the table's earlier ones in `eggdb_privacy.classifications`.
- Differential privacy: `eggdb_privacy.budgets` holds each dataset's total epsilon and what is spent;
  `spend` debits it under a row lock and logs who spent what in `budget_spending`; `dp_count`, `dp_sum` (values
  clamped to given bounds, sensitivity the larger bound) and `dp_avg` (half the epsilon each to a sum and a count)
  answer with `laplace_noise` (D245) and refuse with SQLSTATE `53400` once the budget would be exceeded.
- Masked copies: `eggdb_privacy.masked_copy(schema, table, target, masks)` creates `target` as a copy with each
  column kept or masked (`null`, `redact`, `hash` as MD5, `email` as a stable fake address, `keep_last:N`), for test
  and development data.
- Synthetic data generation, the snapping mechanism and l-diversity are still left. Databases that installed
  `eggdb_privacy` before get these with a new `CREATE EXTENSION`.
