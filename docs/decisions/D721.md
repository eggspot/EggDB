## D721 Cypher temporal arithmetic, `duration.between` and `point.distance` — Decided by the code-only session (2026-11-12, D355); roadmap M13, follow-up of D720

- `temporal + duration`, `duration + temporal` and `temporal - duration` add months first (a day past the new
  month's end becomes its last day, so `2024-01-31` plus a month is `2024-02-29`), then days, then the time. A date
  takes only the duration's whole days; a time wraps around midnight. `duration + duration`, `duration - duration`
  and `duration * integer` work component by component. Overflow is an arithmetic error.
- `duration.between(a, b)` gives whole months, then whole days, then the remaining time, all with the sign of the
  whole difference; when either value has no date, only the time. `duration.inMonths`, `duration.inDays` and
  `duration.inSeconds` give the difference in that unit alone. Zoned datetimes are compared by instant.
- `point.distance(a, b)` (and `distance`, its older name) is the Euclidean distance for Cartesian points and the
  haversine distance on a sphere of radius 6,378,140 m for WGS 84 points, with the height difference added as a
  right-angle leg for 3D points; points of different SRIDs give null.
- The parser reads `namespace.function(...)` calls for these two namespaces (`duration.` and `point.`).
- Still left: fractional duration multiplication and division, `truncate`, and named time zones.
