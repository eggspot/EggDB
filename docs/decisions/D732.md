## D732 Oracle `UTL_ENCODE`, `UTL_I18N` and `UTL_URL` — Decided by the code-only session (2026-11-12, D355); roadmap M10, the other Oracle-supplied packages (D434, D659)

In the Oracle dialect, beside the packages D434 and D659 to D661 added:

- `UTL_ENCODE.BASE64_ENCODE(raw)` and `BASE64_DECODE(raw)` turn `RAW` into its base64 text as `RAW` and back;
  the encoded text has no line breaks, and decoding skips whitespace and fails with ORA-06502 on anything else
  that is not base64.
- `UTL_I18N.STRING_TO_RAW(text [, charset])` and `RAW_TO_CHAR(raw [, charset])` convert between text and bytes in
  `AL32UTF8` (the default, also `UTF8`) or a single-byte Latin-1 set (`WE8ISO8859P1`, `WE8MSWIN1252`,
  `US7ASCII`, where characters above 255 become `?`); other character sets fail with ORA-06502.
- `UTL_URL.ESCAPE(url [, escape_reserved_chars [, charset]])` percent-encodes every character other than letters,
  digits and `-_.!~*'()`, keeping the reserved `;/?:@&=+$,[]` and `%` unless `escape_reserved_chars` is true;
  `UNESCAPE(url [, charset])` decodes `%XX` sequences.
- A `NULL` first argument gives `NULL`.
- Still left: the other packages (`DBMS_UTILITY`'s error and call stacks, `UTL_COMPRESS` (since built, D733), `DBMS_XMLGEN`,
  `UTL_HTTP`, `UTL_FILE` and the rest).
