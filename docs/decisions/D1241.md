## D1241 Collated $sort stages in MongoDB's aggregate — Decided by the code-only session (2026-11-17, D355); roadmap M9, collations (D197, D738, D989)

- **What users get.** An `aggregate` with a `collation` orders every `$sort` stage by the collation, as MongoDB does: `banana` between `Apple` and `cherry` at strength 2, `item 9` before `item 10` with `numericOrdering`, accents ignored at strength 1. The documents themselves come out unchanged.
- **How.** `run_pipeline_collated` in `eggdb-documents` runs the stages between sorts with `run_pipeline` and sorts each `$sort`'s input by the collation keys of the documents (D738's keys over every string, in arrays and embedded documents too), keeping the originals; the sort is stable, as before. `aggregate` uses it whenever the command has a collation.
- **Left.** `$group` keys and later `$match` stages under the collation, and `$sort` inside `$lookup` pipelines and `$facet`.
- **Proof.** A unit test in `collation.rs`: a binary sort puts `Apple` first among capitals, the strength-2 sort orders three names alphabetically, and numeric ordering puts `item 9` first.

**Status:** code written, unbuilt (code-only mode, D355).
