## D611 PromQL's @ modifier, negative offsets, limitk and limit_ratio — Decided by the code-only session (2026-11-12, D355); roadmap M16, D220, D334, D441

Grafana dashboards use `@ end()` to rank series by their value at the end of the range
(`topk(5, rate(x[5m]) @ end())`) and negative offsets to compare against the future of a
backfilled series. Prometheus 3's `limitk` and `limit_ratio` sample series for previews. D441 left
all of them.

### The `@` modifier

- A selector or subquery takes `@ <unix seconds>`, `@ start()` or `@ end()`, before or after
  `offset`, as Prometheus parses them.
- Evaluation reads at that time instead of the step's, less the offset.
  - `start()` and `end()` are the query's range. An instant query's start and end are its time.
    The evaluator learns the range through `Evaluator::over`.
- The read from storage now spans the earliest and latest fixed `@` times as well as the query's
  range (`promql::fixed_times`).

### Negative offsets

- `offset -5m` is read, and a selector's evaluation moves forward by it.
- The read extends past the query's end by the largest forward reach, counting offsets through
  subqueries (`promql::forward_reach`).

### limitk and limit_ratio

- Both are aggregations with a parameter, with `by` and `without`.
- `limitk(k, v)` keeps `k` series of each group. Members are ordered by a stable hash of their
  label set (FNV-1a over names and values), not by value, so the same series stay chosen from step
  to step.
- `limit_ratio(r, v)` keeps a series when its hash's share of the 64-bit range is below `r`, or,
  for negative `r`, at least `1 + r`. `r` and `-(1 − r)` therefore split the series between them.
  A ratio outside −1 to 1 is refused.

A test covers each against the fixture of eight `http_requests` series.
