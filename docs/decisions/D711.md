## D711 read_geotiff — Decided by the code-only session (2026-11-12, D355); roadmap M12, raster and GeoTIFF (D57, D710)

- `read_geotiff(path)` (PostgreSQL dialect) reads the pixels of a TIFF's first image through the session's file
  reader (D703) and returns one row per pixel: `x` and `y` (column and row from the top left), `values` (one `double
  precision` per band), and for a GeoTIFF `map_x` and `map_y`, the map coordinates of the pixel's centre from the tie
  point and pixel scale (D710).
- The decoder in `geotiff.rs` reads strips and tiles; no compression, deflate (through `eggdb-content`'s zlib
  inflate) and LZW (TIFF's MSB-first codes with the early change, written here); the horizontal-differencing
  predictor for integers; 8-, 16-, 32- and 64-bit signed and unsigned integers and 32- and 64-bit floats; both byte
  orders and BigTIFF. An image holds at most 50 million values.
- Refused with a clear message: band-separate (planar) images, the floating-point predictor, JPEG and other
  compressions, and sample sizes other than whole bytes.
- Left: overviews, reprojection, and raster SQL types and operations.

**Status:** code written, unbuilt (code-only mode, D355).
