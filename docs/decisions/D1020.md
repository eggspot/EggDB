## D1020 Differential, encrypted and parallel backups with retention — Decided by the code-only session (2026-11-13, D355); roadmap M14 (D24, D208, D1019)

- **Differential, not chained incremental.** `eggdb backup <file> <directory> --differential` writes the keys whose
  stored values differ from the newest full copy in the directory, and the keys that copy has and the database no
  longer has, found by one merge of both key spaces in key order. The file is in the change archive's format
  (D1019), so restoring is the full copy plus one replay. Each differential is against the last full copy, never a
  chain of incrementals: a restore needs two files at most, and a damaged differential costs only itself. The
  manifest line says `"kind": "differential"` and names its `base`.
- **Encrypted archives.** `--backup-key-file <file>` (64 hexadecimal digits, as `--key-file`) seals each archived
  file: a random salt and nonce prefix, a key derived with HKDF-SHA256, and AES-256-GCM over 1 MiB chunks whose
  nonces carry the chunk number and a last-chunk flag (the STREAM construction of Hoang, Reyhanitabar, Rogaway and
  Vizár), so chunks cannot be reordered, dropped or cut off without the restore failing. The copy is verified
  before it is sealed and the unencrypted file is removed. Files of databases encrypted with `--key-file` (D187)
  are already encrypted page by page; the backup key protects unencrypted ones and differentials.
- **Retention.** `--keep <n>` keeps the newest `n` full copies and the differentials taken against them, removes
  the other files and appends a `"pruned"` line for each to the manifest, which stays append-only; readers skip
  pruned files. Without `--keep` nothing is ever removed (D208). The change archive's segments are not pruned yet.
- **Parallel.** Given a served directory (`<name>.eggdb` files, D200) as the file, `eggdb backup` archives every
  database into its own subdirectory of the archive, up to eight at once, and reports each; one failure does not
  stop the others, and the command fails naming every database that was not archived.
- `eggdb restore <new> <directory> [--at <time>]` restores the newest full or differential entry at or before the
  time (decrypting with `--backup-key-file`), then replays the change archive from that entry's stamp (D1019).
