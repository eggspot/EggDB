## D619 CASE, loops and labels in MySQL trigger bodies — Decided by the code-only session (2026-11-12, D355); roadmap M10, D199, D420

MySQL trigger bodies are compound statements. D199 ran `BEGIN … END`, `DECLARE`, `SET`, `IF`,
`WHILE` and `SIGNAL` in them; `CASE`, `LOOP`, `REPEAT`, labels, `LEAVE` and `ITERATE` failed to
parse, so schemas exported from MySQL with such triggers did not load.

- The trigger body parser reads:
  - `CASE operand WHEN v THEN … [ELSE …] END CASE` and the searched `CASE WHEN condition THEN …`;
  - `LOOP … END LOOP`;
  - `REPEAT … UNTIL condition END REPEAT`;
  - `label: BEGIN|LOOP|WHILE|REPEAT … END … [label]`;
  - `LEAVE label` and `ITERATE label`.
- The body interpreter now returns where control goes after each step: on, out of a named
  label, or round a named loop.
  - A loop that sees `ITERATE` for its own label goes round again. `LEAVE` for its own label
    ends it.
  - A labelled `BEGIN … END` block ends on `LEAVE` of its label.
  - Anything else passes outwards. One that reaches the top of the body is MySQL's error 1308
    ("LEAVE with no matching label").
  - Every loop round checks for cancellation, as `WHILE` already did.
- A simple `CASE` compares its operand with each `WHEN` value by evaluating `operand = value`
  with SQL's equality, so `1` matches `1.0` as in MySQL. A `CASE` that matches nothing and has no
  `ELSE` raises MySQL's error 1339 ("Case not found for CASE statement", SQLSTATE 20000).
- A MySQL-dialect test runs a trigger with a simple `CASE`, a labelled `LOOP` left with `LEAVE`
  and continued with `ITERATE`, and a `REPEAT`, over three inserted rows.

Not done: `DECLARE … HANDLER`, cursors and named conditions in trigger bodies (stored procedures
have them, D418).
