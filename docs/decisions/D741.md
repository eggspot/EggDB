## D741 `ST_Transform` to World Mercator and UTM zones — Decided by the code-only session (2026-11-12, D355); roadmap M12, `ST_Transform` between any projections (D204, D250)

- `eggdb-geo` gains `transform(geometry, from, to)`: each coordinate goes to longitude and latitude and on to the
  target, written from the published map projection formulas (Snyder, *Map Projections: A Working Manual*, USGS
  Professional Paper 1395) with EggDB's own code:
  - geographic: 4326 (WGS 84), 4979, 4258 (ETRS89) and 4269 (NAD83);
  - Web Mercator, 3857 (and 900913), on its sphere as before;
  - World Mercator, 3395, on the WGS 84 ellipsoid (the inverse iterates the latitude);
  - transverse Mercator UTM zones on their ellipsoids: WGS 84 32601–32660 and 32701–32760, ETRS89 25828–25838 and
    NAD83 26901–26923 (GRS 80), with the scale 0.9996, 500 km false easting and 10,000 km false northing in the
    south.
- Datum shifts are not applied: WGS 84, ETRS89 and NAD83 are taken as the same datum, which they are to within
  about a metre to two today. Other coordinate systems (national grids, Lambert conformal conic, equal-area
  projections) are refused as not supported yet.
- `ST_Transform` uses it for every pair of these SRIDs.

**Status:** code written, unbuilt (code-only mode, D355).
