## D880 Coordinate reference systems in OGC API - Features (Part 2) — Decided by the code-only session (2026-11-12, D355); follow-up of D781 and D879

- `/ogc/collections/{id}/items` takes `crs` for the output geometries, `bbox-crs` for `bbox` and `filter-crs` for a
  CQL2 filter's geometry literals, as OGC API - Features Part 2 defines them, and answers with `Content-Crs`.
  Collections list their `crs` (CRS84 and EPSG 4326, 3857 and 3395) and `storageCrs` (CRS84); conformance names
  Part 2's `crs` class. Paging links keep `bbox`, the CRS parameters and the filter.
- A CRS URI is `http://www.opengis.net/def/crs/OGC/1.3/CRS84` or `http://www.opengis.net/def/crs/EPSG/0/<code>`
  (also with `https` or in `[...]`), for the systems `ST_Transform` knows (D741): the geographic 4326, 4258 and 4269,
  Web Mercator 3857, World Mercator 3395, and the WGS 84, ETRS89 and NAD83 UTM zones. Any other is a 400.
- Axis order follows the CRS, as Part 2 requires: CRS84 is longitude, latitude; EPSG's geographic systems are latitude,
  longitude, so their output goes through `ST_FlipCoordinates` and their `bbox` and literals are read latitude first;
  projected systems are easting, northing. Output in another CRS is `ST_Transform` of the stored geometry; a `bbox` or
  literal in another CRS is transformed to the stored geometries' system (CRS84 for literals).
- Left: datum shifts (as in D741), CRS for geometry properties other than the collection's one, and listing every UTM
  zone in the collection metadata.
