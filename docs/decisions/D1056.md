## D1056 Broker benchmarks through one client of our own, starting with Kafka — Decided by the code-only session (2026-11-13, D355); roadmap M16 (D33, D213)

- The roadmap asks for benchmarks of EggDB's messaging against Kafka, RabbitMQ, Mosquitto and EMQX, ElasticMQ and
  nats-server. They share one crate, `eggdb-broker-compare`, with a workspace of its own and never shipped, as the other
  comparison crates are (D94).
- **One client for both sides.** The comparison speaks each protocol with a small client of its own and sends the same
  requests to EggDB and to the other broker. Using each broker's favourite client library would measure the
  libraries as much as the servers; with one client, the ratio is the servers'.
- **Kafka first.** `kafka` creates a topic, produces 2,000 batches of 100 records of 100 bytes (Produce v3, acks=1,
  record batches of magic 2) and fetches them back from offset 0 (Fetch v4, following each batch's last offset), on
  EggDB's `--kafka-listen` and on Apache Kafka. It reports messages per second, p50 and p99 per round trip, and
  EggDB's throughput as a ratio of Kafka's.
- CI's `broker-compare` job builds EggDB, starts `eggdb serve --kafka-listen`, runs Apache Kafka 3.7 as a service, and
  keeps the results as an artifact; the ratio is reported, not a gate (D7, D101), and the job joins `check`'s list.
- The other brokers follow as more subcommands of the same crate.
