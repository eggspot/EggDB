## D1047 The tenant prefix in the key layout, and tenants moved online — Decided by the code-only session (2026-11-13, D355); roadmap M15 (D20, D234)

- D20 reserved a tenant prefix; D234 left moving a tenant online for it. A tenant's key space is now stored under the
  engine's reserved range with its own tag: `FF "COL" 00 08`, the tenant's name length (2 bytes) and name, then the
  key (`eggdb_cluster::tenants`). The length prefix keeps tenant `acme`'s range from containing tenant `acme2`'s, and a
  tenant's keys stay in their own order, so one tenant is one contiguous range: it is counted, copied and moved as a
  unit, and the default key space never shows it (the range is inside the reserved collections prefix). No feature
  flag is needed: an older release reads such keys as reserved and leaves them alone.
- `Database::tenant(name)` gives a `TenantSpace` with `get`, `put`, `delete` and `entries`; `Database::tenant_page`
  reads a tenant's range page by page from one snapshot.
- **Routing.** `Strategy::Tenant { shards, placed }` routes every key of a tenant to one shard: the shard `placed`
  names for it, or the one its name hashes to. `ShardMap::place_tenant` places a tenant on a shard (creating it, owned
  by the given node, when the map has none) and raises the epoch; it is the cut-over of a tenant move. The map's stored
  form gains the strategy (tag 4).
- **Moving a tenant.** `MOVE TENANT '<tenant>' FROM 'host:port'` runs D1040's protocol on the tenant's range instead of a
  shard: the `SHARD_COPY` greeting carries the tenant's name, the old home sends pages of `tenant_page` and catch-up
  changes filtered by the tenant prefix (`tenant_changes`), and the new home publishes the map with the tenant placed
  on a new shard it owns. A large tenant thereby gets a shard of its own without moving its neighbours.
- Left: storing a tenant-isolated SQL table's rows (D234's tenancy by row) in the tenant's range, which needs the SQL
  row key to carry the tenant; until then SQL tenants move with their shard.
