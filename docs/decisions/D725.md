## D725 JetStream `interest` retention — Decided by the code-only session (2026-11-12, D355); roadmap M16, follow-up of D722

- A stream with `retention: interest` now removes a message once every consumer whose filter takes its subject has
  had it delivered and acknowledged it (`+ACK`, `+NXT`, or a later `+ACK` under `ack_policy: all`; `+TERM` counts
  too, as the consumer gives the message up).
- A message published while no consumer's filter takes its subject is removed as soon as it is stored; the
  publisher still gets its `PubAck` with the sequence, as JetStream does.
- Consumers with `ack_policy: none` count a message as acknowledged once delivered, but delivery alone does not
  trigger the check, so with only such consumers the messages are removed on the stream's limits instead.
