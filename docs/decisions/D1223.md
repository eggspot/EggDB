## D1223 Subplans in EXPLAIN of UPDATE and DELETE — Decided by the code-only session (2026-11-17, D355); roadmap M6c, subqueries in `EXPLAIN` (D327)

- **What users get.** `EXPLAIN UPDATE ...` and `EXPLAIN DELETE ...` whose `WHERE` or `SET` values hold subqueries show them as PostgreSQL does for a `SELECT`: `Filter: (hashed SubPlan 1)` and the `SubPlan` or `InitPlan` with its own plan under the scan of the written table, instead of the scan alone.
- **How.** `explain_with_subplans` plans such a write as the `SELECT` it reads: the written table with its `WHERE`, and the `SET` values (not `DEFAULT`) as the select list, so the existing subplan numbering and placement (D327) apply unchanged. A write with no subquery keeps its plan.
- **Left.** PostgreSQL's `Update on t` and `Delete on t` node above the scan (EggDB's plans of writes start at the scan, as before), joined writes (`UPDATE ... FROM`, `DELETE ... USING`), and subplans in derived tables, `WITH` and set operations.
- **Proof.** `explain/session_tests.rs`: a `DELETE` with `IN (SELECT ...)` shows a hashed subplan, and an `UPDATE` whose `SET` reads a correlated subquery shows one `SubPlan`.
