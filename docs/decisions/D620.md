## D620 t-digest sketches for APPROX_PERCENTILE_CONT — Decided by the code-only session (2026-11-12, D355); roadmap M5, D40, D542

D542 answered `APPROX_PERCENTILE_CONT` (SQL Server) and `APPROX_PERCENTILE` (Oracle) exactly,
through `PERCENTILE_CONT`, which sorts every value of a group. The point of the approximate
functions is to answer over huge groups in bounded memory, as a latency dashboard's p99 over a
billion rows needs.

- New `tdigest` module: a merging t-digest (Dunning and Ertl).
  - Compression is 100, with the `k₁` scale function (`δ/2π · asin(2q − 1)`), so centroids are
    smallest at the tails, where p99 and p1 are read.
  - Values are buffered 500 at a time. A full buffer is merged into the centroids in one sorted
    pass, which bounds the sketch at a few hundred centroids whatever the group size.
  - Quantiles interpolate between centroid centres, and between the true minimum or maximum and
    the outermost centroids.
  - While nothing has been merged (fewer than 500 values), the buffer answers exactly, with
    `PERCENTILE_CONT`'s interpolation. Small groups therefore give the same answers as before.
- `APPROX_PERCENTILE_CONT` and `APPROX_PERCENTILE` now name their own ordered-set aggregate,
  `ApproxPercentileCont`.
  - It takes numeric input (intervals stay with `PERCENTILE_CONT`) and returns `double precision`.
  - Its accumulator is the digest, held at a fixed size against the query's memory.
  - The fraction is read from the first row and checked to lie between 0 and 1, as before.
- `APPROX_PERCENTILE_DISC` stays exact through `PERCENTILE_DISC`. A discrete answer from a sketch
  would have to be one of the input values, which a t-digest does not keep.
- A test checks that four values give the exact median, and that 100,000 values give p1, p50 and
  p99 within 1% of the range while holding fewer than 400 centroids.

Not done: merging digests across groups or partial aggregates, `APPROX_PERCENTILE_DISC` from a
sketch, and Oracle's `ERROR_RATE` and `CONFIDENCE` outputs.
