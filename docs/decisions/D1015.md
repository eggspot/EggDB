## D1015 The graph measured against Neo4j with LDBC-shaped data — Decided by the code-only session (2026-11-13, D355); roadmap M13 (D7, D206)

- A new compare crate, `eggdb-ldbc-compare` (its own workspace, never shipped, like the Redis, MongoDB and
  OpenSearch comparisons), loads the same network into `eggdb serve --bolt-listen` and into Neo4j 5 Community in a
  service container, through one Bolt 5.0 client of its own (PackStream and chunking written from the Bolt
  documentation), then measures paired operations on both and reports the ratios, per run, as an artifact and in
  the job summary. CI's new `ldbc-compare` job runs it; like every comparison it reports and never gates (D101).
- The data has the shape of the LDBC Social Network Benchmark's core, generated by the crate from seeds: persons
  with names, birthdays and creation dates, skewed `KNOWS` (a few people with many friends), posts with
  `HAS_CREATOR`, and `LIKES`; 2,000 persons by default (`LDBC_PERSONS`). The operations follow the Interactive
  workload's descriptions: IS1 (profile), IS2 (recent posts), IS3 (friends), IC1 (friends within three hops by
  first name), IC2 (friends' recent posts), IC13 (shortest path between two persons) and IU8 (add a friendship),
  plus the load itself.
- It is not an audited LDBC result and is never published as one: LDBC's Datagen (a Spark program) and its driver
  are not run, only a subset of the queries is, and the scale is far below SF1. LDBC's rules require an audit to
  call a result an LDBC benchmark; the README and reports say "LDBC-shaped". An audited run at SF1 and above is a
  later step that needs the owner's decision on cost.

**Status:** code written, unbuilt (code-only mode, D355).
