## D892 An email store with an SMTP receiver — Decided by the code-only session (2026-11-12, D355); roadmap M16, D56

- A new workspace crate, `eggdb-mail`, holds the email store of D56 as a front-end over the one core: mailboxes and
  messages kept on EggDB's key-value transactions through `eggdb_keyspace::Keyspace`, like the graph (D206) and RDF
  (D886) stores, in the collection `mail0` of the database file. It depends on `eggdb-keyspace`, `eggdb-crypto` and
  `eggdb-platform` only.
- Each mailbox keeps IMAP's `UIDVALIDITY` (from a counter, so a recreated name gets a new one), its next UID and its
  subscription; each message its UID, flags, internal date and raw bytes. `INBOX` is one name in any case and cannot
  be deleted; renaming it moves its messages and leaves it, as IMAP says.
- `message.rs` reads RFC 5322 messages: unfolded header fields, the body's start, RFC 2047 encoded words (B and Q in
  UTF-8, US-ASCII, ISO-8859-1), address lists with display names and groups, and dates both ways (RFC 5322, IMAP's
  internal-date form, RFC 3339).
- `eggdb serve --smtp-listen <address>` (default port 2525, loopback only until it has TLS, refused with
  `--read-only`) receives mail over SMTP (RFC 5321): `EHLO`/`HELO`, `MAIL FROM` (with `SIZE=`), `RCPT TO` (at most 100),
  `DATA` with dot-unstuffing and a 25 MiB limit, `RSET`, `NOOP`, `VRFY` (252), `QUIT`, advertising `SIZE`, `8BITMIME`
  and `PIPELINING`. Every accepted message is stored in `INBOX` with `Received`, `Delivered-To` (one per recipient)
  and `Return-Path` fields; nothing is relayed, so the store is one account's (the server's user) for now.
- Lines are read across one-second read time-outs without losing bytes, so a stop is noticed while a slow client is
  still served.
- Next: IMAP and JMAP to read the store; later: TLS (`STARTTLS`), SMTP `AUTH` for submission, several accounts with
  their own mailboxes, and delivery rules.
