## D1157 One shared listener crate for the protocol servers — Decided by the code-only session (2026-11-14, D355); roadmap M6a (D122)

- **The pieces.** D122 left four pieces copied three times, in `eggdb-pgwire`, `eggdb-mysqlwire` and
  `eggdb-tdswire`. They now live once in the new workspace crate `crates/eggdb-listener`, which depends on nothing
  but the standard library:
  - **The loopback rule (D102).** `ListenError`, `listen(address, tls_configured)` and `listen_on_loopback`.
  - **Connection slots.** `Slot` and `take_slot` (now on the stable `fetch_update`).
  - **The login throttle.** `AuthenticationThrottle`: 250 ms for each failure in the last minute, at most 5 s, at
    most 1,024 failures remembered.
  - **Log-safe text.** `printable` takes TDS's stricter set: every control character, the line and paragraph
    separators, and every bidirectional mark, embedding, override and isolate (U+200E, U+200F, U+061C,
    U+2028 to U+202E, U+2066 to U+2069). The PostgreSQL and MySQL listeners therefore also stop a client from
    reordering a log line with an isolate.
- **No API change for callers.** Each wire crate re-exports `ListenError`, `listen` and `listen_on_loopback` from
  its root as before, so `eggdb serve` and other callers are unchanged. Each crate imports the slot, throttle and
  `printable` instead of defining them.
- **Lock files.** The workspace lock file and the lock files of the excluded crates that pull in the wire crates
  (`eggdb-sqlite-compare`, `eggdb-redis-compare`, `eggdb-tpcc-compare`) list the new crate.
- **Later work.** The other listeners keep their own loops until the completion-queue event loop (the next M6a
  item, D103, D165), which will accept for every protocol and take its slots and throttle from this crate.
- **Tests.** The crate has its own tests: the loopback refusal, slots running out and coming back, the throttle's
  growth and cap, and escaping.

**Status:** code written, unbuilt (code-only mode, D355).
