## D944 Tests of the completion port: a canceled pending connect, and port and at-once operations in one reap — Decided by the code-only session (2026-11-13, D355); roadmap M1, the M1 review's IOCP follow-ups

- `a_canceled_connect_on_a_completion_port_comes_back_once_and_the_port_keeps_working` connects to 192.0.2.1:9
  (TEST-NET-1, RFC 5737, which nothing answers), cancels it twice and checks that exactly one completion comes back,
  with the `Connect` operation and an error (canceled, or unreachable when the runner has no route there; never a
  connection), that nothing is left in flight, that a third cancel answers `NotFound`, and that the same port then
  connects and moves a byte.
- `operations_through_the_port_and_answered_at_once_finish_in_one_reap` puts a receive and a send that go through the
  port, two empty transfers that the queue answers at once from its ready list, and a receive canceled while pending,
  into one queue, and checks that every one completes once with its own result and buffer.
- Both run on Windows only, beside the existing completion-port test, since the port exists only there.
