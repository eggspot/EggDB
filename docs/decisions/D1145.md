## D1145 libsqlite3's online backup: sqlite3_backup_init, step, finish, remaining and pagecount — Decided by the code-only session (2026-11-14, D355); roadmap M5 (libsqlite3's calls, D786's sibling items)

- **What programs get.** The five calls of SQLite's online backup API, declared in `include/sqlite3.h`. They are most
  often used to save a `:memory:` database to a file, or to load one into memory, as in SQLite's own examples.
- **How.** The backup copies the source connection's database into the destination connection's, replacing its
  contents, in one write transaction of the destination. It copies every key of the default key space, which
  holds SQL's tables and catalog, and every collection with its entries, after dropping the destination's own.
  EggDB copies everything at the first `sqlite3_backup_step`, whatever page count it asks for, and answers
  `SQLITE_DONE`. `remaining` is then 0 and `pagecount` reports the source's pages when the backup began.
  A destination whose writer is busy answers `SQLITE_BUSY`, as SQLite does, and may be stepped again. Any other
  failure is recorded on the destination connection and returned by `finish`.
- **Refused as SQLite refuses.** The same connection on both sides ("source and destination must be distinct"), and
  database names other than `main` (`temp` and attached databases).
- **Not kept.** Keys' times to live are not carried over. SQL tables carry none, and the key-value side is outside
  SQLite's API.
