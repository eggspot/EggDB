## D893 IMAP over the email store — Decided by the code-only session (2026-11-12, D355); follow-up of D892

- `eggdb serve --imap-listen <address>` (default port 1143, loopback only until it has TLS, refused with `--read-only`)
  serves the store of D892 over IMAP4rev1 (RFC 3501) with `UIDPLUS` (RFC 4315), `MOVE` (RFC 6851), `LITERAL+` (RFC
  7888) and `ID`, written from the RFCs in `eggdb-mail/src/imap.rs`.
- Sign-in is `LOGIN` or `AUTHENTICATE PLAIN` (initial response or continuation) as the server's user; every other
  command waits for it. Mailbox commands: `LIST` and `LSUB` with `*` and `%` wildcards, `/` as the hierarchy
  separator and `\HasChildren`/`\HasNoChildren`; `CREATE`, `DELETE`, `RENAME`, `SUBSCRIBE`, `UNSUBSCRIBE`, `STATUS`
  (`MESSAGES`, `RECENT`, `UIDNEXT`, `UIDVALIDITY`, `UNSEEN`); `APPEND` with flags, date and a literal, answering
  `APPENDUID`. `INBOX` is made on first use.
- Selected state: `SELECT`/`EXAMINE` (`EXISTS`, `UNSEEN`, `UIDVALIDITY`, `UIDNEXT`, `PERMANENTFLAGS`, read-write or
  read-only), `FETCH` (`UID`, `FLAGS`, `INTERNALDATE`, `RFC822.SIZE`, `ENVELOPE`, `BODY`/`BODYSTRUCTURE` for one-part
  messages, `RFC822`, `RFC822.HEADER`, `RFC822.TEXT`, `BODY[]`, `[HEADER]`, `[TEXT]`, `[HEADER.FIELDS (...)]`,
  `[HEADER.FIELDS.NOT (...)]`, `.PEEK` and `<start.count>` partials, the `ALL`/`FAST`/`FULL` macros; a non-peek body
  fetch sets `\Seen` and reports the new flags), `STORE` (`FLAGS`, `+FLAGS`, `-FLAGS`, `.SILENT`), `SEARCH` (flags,
  keywords, address and text fields, `HEADER`, `BODY`, `TEXT`, sizes, internal and sent dates, `UID`, sequence sets,
  `NOT`, `OR`, groups), `EXPUNGE`, `CLOSE`, `UNSELECT`, `COPY`/`MOVE` with `COPYUID`, and the `UID` forms of `FETCH`,
  `STORE`, `SEARCH`, `COPY`, `MOVE` and `EXPUNGE`.
- Sequence numbers follow the mailbox as it is at each command; changes by other sessions show at the next command
  rather than as unsolicited updates, and `\Recent` is always empty. Multi-part messages are described as one part,
  and `BODY[n]` beyond the text is refused, until MIME parsing comes. Literals are read across time-outs and limited
  to the message size limit; commands nest at most 16 deep.
- Left: `IDLE`, `CONDSTORE`/`QRESYNC`, MIME structure and parts, `STARTTLS`, several accounts, and IMAP4rev2.
