## D1265 The Update, Delete and Insert nodes at the top of PostgreSQL's plans of writes — Decided by the code-only session (2026-11-18, D355); roadmap EXPLAIN subqueries (D327, D1223)

- **What users get.** In the PostgreSQL dialect, `EXPLAIN UPDATE t ...` and `EXPLAIN DELETE FROM t ...` start with PostgreSQL's `Update on t` or `Delete on t` line (with the table's alias, as PostgreSQL shows it), the scan that finds the rows under it as `  ->  Seq Scan on t` and its filter and subplans indented below, as tools that read PostgreSQL's plans of writes (pgAdmin's explain view, `auto_explain` readers) expect. `EXPLAIN INSERT`, refused before, now shows `Insert on t` above `Result` for one row of values or `DEFAULT VALUES`, `Values Scan on "*VALUES*"` for several, and the plan of an `INSERT ... SELECT`'s query. `UPDATE ... FROM` and `DELETE ... USING`, also refused before, show their node above the join they read, planned as MySQL's joined writes already were.
- **How.** Planning a write for `EXPLAIN` notes the top node from the write and its first source; the text form nests the read plan under it, the first line behind `->` and every later line six columns further in, the same nesting the plans' child nodes use.
- **Later the same night.** `FORMAT JSON`, `YAML` and `XML` show the node too, as PostgreSQL's `ModifyTable` with its `Operation` (`Update`, `Delete`, `Insert`), `Relation Name` and `Alias`, the read plan its child.
- **Left.** The node in `EXPLAIN ANALYZE`, `INSERT` from a query with `WITH`, set operations, `ORDER BY` or `LIMIT`, and Oracle's and SQL Server's own forms.
- **Proof.** `explain/session_tests.rs`: a `DELETE` with a subquery shows `Delete on tenk1` above its nested scan, filter and subplan, an aliased `UPDATE` shows `Update on tenk1 t`, a `SELECT` keeps its scan on top, the three kinds of `INSERT` show their child, and the joined `UPDATE` and `DELETE` show their node above the join.
