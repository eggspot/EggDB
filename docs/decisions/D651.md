## D651 Swedish, Norwegian and Danish stemmers — Decided by the code-only session (2026-11-12, D355); roadmap M8, D30, D649, D650

PostgreSQL ships `swedish`, `norwegian` and `danish` text-search configurations. Snowball stems
the three languages with the same short shape of algorithm, so one module, `scandinavian`, holds
them, with a `Language` value choosing each one's vowels, suffix lists and rules.

- R1 starts after the first non-vowel that follows a vowel, at least three letters in.
- Main suffix: the longest of the language's list, removed when it is in R1. `s` goes only after
  a valid s-ending (Norwegian: also `k` not after a vowel). Norwegian `erte`/`ert` become `er`.
- Consonant pairs in R1 lose their last letter: Swedish `dd gd nn dt gt kt tt`, Norwegian `dt vt`,
  Danish `gd dt gt kt`.
- Other suffixes in R1:
  - Swedish: `lig ig els` are removed, and `löst`/`fullt` become `lös`/`full`;
  - Norwegian: `leg … hetslov` are removed;
  - Danish: `igst` loses `st`, then `ig lig elig els` are removed (and the pair step runs again),
    and `løst` becomes `løs`.
- Danish only: a double consonant at the end of R1 is halved.
- Each language's Snowball stop words are dropped. `Config::Swedish`, `Norwegian` and `Danish`
  are named `swedish`, `norwegian` and `danish`.
- Test: definite and plural endings (`flickorna`, `kraftigt`, `bilene`, `hoppet`, `bilerne`,
  `løbende`) and stop words. Snowball's vocabulary files should be checked when the build runs.
