## D683 Native histogram buckets and exemplars from remote write 2.0 — Decided by the code-only session (2026-11-12, D355); roadmap M16, remote write 2.0 (D220, D665)

- A native histogram of a remote write 2.0 request is now kept in full, not only as `_count` and `_sum` (D665): its
  buckets become classic cumulative `<name>_bucket` points with an `le` tag, so `histogram_quantile` and dashboards
  built for classic histograms read them. Bucket `i` of schema `s` has the upper bound `(2^(2^-s))^i`; the spans'
  offsets are relative after the first, integer histograms' counts are delta-encoded and float histograms' are
  absolute. Negative buckets come first (largest magnitude first), then the zero bucket at the zero threshold, then
  the positive buckets, then `+Inf` with the histogram's count. Schema `-53` (custom buckets) takes its bounds from
  `custom_values`. A histogram may have at most 65,536 buckets.
- Each exemplar becomes a `<name>_exemplars` point with the series' labels as tags, its value as `value` and its
  labels (such as `trace_id`) as string fields `exemplar_<label>`. The response's
  `X-Prometheus-Remote-Write-Exemplars-Written` header now counts them. Exemplar tables are not listed as metrics.
- `GET` and `POST /api/v1/query_exemplars?query=<selector>&start=&end=` answers in Prometheus's shape (`seriesLabels`
  and `exemplars` with `labels`, `value` and `timestamp`), matching series with the PromQL selector's matchers; at most
  100,000 exemplars are read per metric.
