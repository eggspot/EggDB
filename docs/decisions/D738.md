## D738 MongoDB collations in `find`, `count` and `distinct` — Decided by the code-only session (2026-11-12, D355); roadmap M9, collations (D197)

- `find`, `count` and `distinct` take a `collation` document. `locale: "simple"` (or none) keeps binary comparison;
  any other locale compares strings by EggDB's own keys, the same for every locale:
  - strength 1: base letters only, ignoring case and accents (Latin-1, Latin Extended-A and the Vietnamese letters
    of Latin Extended-B and Latin Extended Additional are folded to their base letters);
  - strength 2: accents count, case does not;
  - strength 3 (the default) to 5: case counts too, lower case ordering before upper case;
  - `numericOrdering: true` compares runs of digits as numbers (`item 9` before `item 10`).
- The filter's compared strings (equality, ranges, `$in`, `$nin`, `$all`, `$not`, `$elemMatch`, in `$and`, `$or`
  and `$nor`) and every string of each document are turned into those keys before matching; `$regex`, `$type` and
  other operators keep their arguments. Sorting and `distinct`'s de-duplication and order use the keys too; the
  documents returned are unchanged.
- A query with a collation reads the whole collection and filters it in memory, as no index carries collation keys
  yet. Other commands (updates, deletes, `findAndModify`, `aggregate`) still ignore `collation`, and locales'
  own tailorings (such as Swedish `å` after `z`) are not applied.
