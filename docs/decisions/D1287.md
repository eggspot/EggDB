## D1287 ST_Touches, ST_Crosses and ST_Overlaps — Decided by the code-only session (2026-11-18, D355); roadmap M4 geospatial (D204, D1277)

- **What users get.** PostGIS's `ST_Touches(a, b)` (they meet, but only at boundaries; points never touch), `ST_Crosses(a, b)` (two lines crossing at a point, or a lower-dimension geometry with parts both inside and outside the other's interior) and `ST_Overlaps(a, b)` (same dimension, interiors meet, neither covers the other).
- **How.** Instead of a full intersection matrix, `eggdb-geo` classifies sample points: every vertex, the middle of every piece of each segment once it is cut where the other geometry's edges meet it (so a line half inside a polygon is sampled on both sides), and the centroid. A point is in a geometry's interior by its dimension: a point's own position, a line without its mod-2 ends, a polygon without its rings. Interiors meet when a sample lies in both (or, against a polygon, in the polygon's open interior from either side), or when edges cross properly. Every case the unit test lists follows the Simple Features definitions; inputs whose interiors meet only away from all samples (two polygons crossing like a plus sign without a vertex inside either) are caught by the proper-crossing test.
- **Left.** The full DE-9IM (`ST_Relate`), collections of mixed dimensions, and the exact `ST_Contains` boundary rule.
- **Proof.** A unit test: squares sharing an edge touch and do not overlap, shifted squares overlap and do not touch, lines meeting at an end touch, equal points do not, crossing lines cross, a line leaving a square crosses it while one inside does not, a square inside another does not overlap it, and collinear lines sharing a stretch overlap.

**Status:** code written, unbuilt (code-only mode, D355).
