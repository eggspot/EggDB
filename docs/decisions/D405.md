## D405 MongoDB's `$facet`, `$bucket` and `$switch` — Decided by the code-only session (2026-11-10, D355); roadmap M9

- **`$switch`** joins the expression operators: the first branch whose `case` is truthy gives its
  `then`; with none, `default`, or MongoDB's error `$switch could not find a matching branch for an
  input, and no default was specified.`
- **`$bucket`** is read as the `$group` and `$sort` it stands for, as `$sortByCount` already is:
  the group key is a `$switch` over `boundaries` (each bucket `[lower, upper)`, keyed by its lower
  bound, with `default` as the last branch), the accumulators are `output` (default `{count: {$sum:
  1}}`), and the buckets are sorted by `_id`. Fewer than two boundaries, or boundaries not strictly
  ascending in BSON order, are MongoDB's errors; a value outside every bucket with no `default`
  fails through `$switch`'s error.
- **`$facet`** runs each named sub-pipeline over the same input documents and returns one
  document whose fields are the sub-pipelines' results as arrays; a sub-pipeline may not hold
  `$facet`, `$out` or `$merge`, as MongoDB refuses them.
