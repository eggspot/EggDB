## D394 RESP3 double replies for `GEOPOS` and `WITHCOORD` coordinates — Decided by the code-only session (2026-11-10, D355); roadmap M7, closes one of D330's leftovers

Redis 7 sends geo coordinates as RESP3 doubles (`,13.361389338970184`) to a client that said
`HELLO 3`, and as bulk strings with up to 17 digits after the point in RESP2
(`$18 13.36138933897018433`). EggDB sent the bulk strings in both. A new reply kind,
`Reply::DoubleWithText(value, text)`, encodes the value as a RESP3 double (`format_double`, as
the other doubles) and the given text as a RESP2 bulk string, so RESP2 output keeps Redis's own
coordinate text byte for byte. `GEOPOS` and the `WITHCOORD` coordinates of `GEOSEARCH`,
`GEORADIUS` and friends use it; distances (`WITHDIST`) keep Redis's four-decimal bulk strings,
which Redis 7 also sends as doubles in RESP3 (left for a later change, with a decision on their
text).
