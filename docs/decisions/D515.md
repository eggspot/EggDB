## D515 Elasticsearch `query_string`, `simple_query_string` and highlighting — Decided by the code-only session (2026-11-11, D355); roadmap M8

- **`query_string`:** Lucene's classic syntax compiled to the query DSL EggDB already scores:
  `field:value`, `field:(group)`, `"phrases"`, `prefix*`, `[a TO b]` and `{a TO b}` ranges with
  `*` for an open end, `>`, `>=`, `<`, `<=` comparisons, `_exists_:field`, `+` and `-`, `AND`,
  `OR`, `NOT`, `&&`, `||`, `!`, parentheses and `\` escapes. Juxtaposed clauses follow
  `default_operator` (OR unless `AND`); terms without a field search `fields`, else
  `default_field`, else every field. A string that does not parse fails with
  `query_shard_exception`, as Elasticsearch does. `?q=` on `_search` reads the same syntax, with
  `df` and `default_operator`.
- **`simple_query_string`:** its own operators (`+` and, `|` or, `-` not, `"phrases"`, `prefix*`,
  parentheses) and no `field:`; a string that does not parse is searched as plain words instead
  of failing, as Elasticsearch's lenient parser does.
- **Highlighting:** `highlight.fields` (an object or an array, names may end or start with `*`)
  with `pre_tags`/`post_tags` (first tag of each; `<em>` and `</em>` by default). Each hit gets a
  `highlight` object with, for each asked field the query searched, every value holding a
  matched word or prefix, with those words wrapped. Words in `must`, `should` and `filter`
  clauses count; `must_not` does not.
- **Wildcard fields:** the expansion of `*` to every top-level field now reaches into `bool`
  clauses and also covers phrases, prefixes and ranges.
- **Left out:** fuzzy (`~`), proximity, boosts (`^`), regular expressions, wildcards inside words,
  fragments (`fragment_size` and `number_of_fragments`: each value is one fragment),
  per-field tags, the `unified`/`plain`/`fvh` highlighter choice and `highlight_query`.
