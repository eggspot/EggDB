## D1016 Gremlin's branching steps — Decided by the code-only session (2026-11-13, D355); roadmap M13 (D48, D1011)

- `where`, `not`, `and`, `or`, `union`, `coalesce`, `optional` and `repeat` compile into the same single Cypher query
  as every other step (D1011). An anonymous traversal inside a branch becomes a Cypher list: a pattern
  comprehension (`[(v)-[:knows]->(w) WHERE w.age > $p | w]`) when it walks edges, a list comprehension over the
  current element when it only filters or projects. `where` and `not` test the list's size, `and` and `or` combine
  the tests, `union` unwinds the concatenated lists, and `coalesce` and `optional` unwind the first non-empty list
  through `CASE`. `where(P.eq('a'))` and `where(P.neq('a'))` compare with a step label.
- Inside a branch: `out`, `in`, `both`, their edge forms with `inV`, `outV` and `otherV`, `has`, `hasLabel`,
  `hasNot`, `hasId`, `is`, `values` (one key), `id`, `label`, `constant` and `identity`.
- `repeat()` of one `out`, `in` or `both` step becomes a variable-length `MATCH`: `times(n)` walks exactly `n`
  steps, `emit()` keeps every step from 1 to `n`, and `until(t)` walks up to 15 steps and keeps the vertices where
  `t` holds. A `repeat` with neither `times` nor `until` is refused, so every walk has an end. Cypher's
  variable-length patterns never reuse an edge within one path, while Gremlin's `repeat` may; walks that need to
  revisit an edge give fewer results here.
- Left: `repeat` of longer traversals, `emit` before `repeat`, `choose`, `local`, `sideEffect`, `group` and `sack`.
