## D550 `Tab` stops on the keys tab's `Back` and `Next 100` — Decided by the code-only session (2026-11-11, D355); roadmap M6e, issue #29, D329

`docs/ux/workspace.md` 10.10 orders the results header's `Tab` stops as segments, `Key prefix`,
`Back`, `Next 100`, grid; `Tab` from the field went straight to the grid. The keys tab now keeps
`paging_focus` (`Back` or `Next 100`) beside the field's focus: `Tab` from the field goes to the
first enabled button, from `Back` to `Next 100` when it is enabled, and from the last one to the
grid; `Shift+Tab` walks back, from the grid to the last enabled button and from `Back` to the
field. A disabled button (`Back` on page 1, `Next 100` on the last page, both while a page is
read) is skipped, as disabled buttons are elsewhere in the client. `Enter` or `Space` on a focused
button pages as `Alt+PgUp` or `Alt+PgDn` does and the focus stays on it; `Esc` goes to the grid.
The focused button gets the focus ring and its accessibility node reports `focused`. Any other
focus change (a click, the field, the tree) clears it.
