## D765 Spreadsheet-style tables with formula columns (eggdb_sheets) — Decided by the code-only session (2026-11-12, D355); roadmap M11, spreadsheet-style tables with formula columns (D38)

- `spreadsheet_recalculate(cells jsonb) RETURNS jsonb` is a formula engine of our own: cells named `A1` to `XFD1048576`
  hold numbers, text, booleans or formulas starting with `=`, with `+ - * / ^ & %`, comparisons, references with or
  without `$`, ranges, and `SUM`, `AVERAGE`, `MIN`, `MAX`, `COUNT`, `COUNTA`, `IF`, `IFERROR`, `AND`, `OR`, `NOT`,
  `ABS`, `SQRT`, `ROUND`, `CONCAT`/`CONCATENATE`, `LEN`, `UPPER` and `LOWER`. Errors are spreadsheet errors kept as
  values (`#DIV/0!`, `#VALUE!`, `#REF!`, `#NAME?`, `#NUM!`), and a cell in a reference cycle is `#CYCLE!`.
- Cells are evaluated in topological order (Kahn's algorithm over the references), never by recursion over the
  chain, so a 100,000-cell chain cannot exhaust the stack; formulas nest at most 100 deep, a range covers at most
  100,000 cells and a sheet at most 100,000 cells (54000 beyond).
- `CREATE EXTENSION eggdb_sheets` keeps sheets in `eggdb_sheets.cells` (one row per entered cell) and formula
  columns in `formula_columns`: a formula with `{row}` that fills a column down from `first_row` to the sheet's last
  row wherever no cell was entered, like a spreadsheet's calculated column. `cell_values(sheet)` and `grid(sheet)`
  (one JSON object of values per row) recalculate on read; `set_cell` enters or clears a cell and `import_table`
  loads a query's rows under a header row.
- Recalculation happens on read, so nothing is cached and no trigger runs on write; caching values for very large
  sheets, more functions (lookups, dates, text), and the grid view in the client are left.
