## D249 Text extraction from Office and OpenDocument files with our own DEFLATE and ZIP in `eggdb-content` — Decided by the build loop (by judgement, 2026-10-31); refines D1, D9 and D56

Track 4.X-21 asks for text extraction from OOXML, ODF, PDF and PST, OCR, email stores with IMAP,
JMAP and SMTP, CalDAV, CardDAV and WebDAV, digital asset management, and enterprise search that
respects permissions. This slice does extraction from OOXML and ODF.

- **A crate with no dependencies.** `eggdb-content` holds `inflate` (RFC 1951: stored, fixed and
  dynamic Huffman blocks), `ZipArchive` (PKWARE's APPNOTE: the central directory, stored and
  deflated entries, CRC-32 checked) and `extract_text`. DEFLATE will be needed again (gzip over
  HTTP, PNG, Parquet's GZIP codec), so it lives in this crate rather than inside one reader.
- **Hostile files are expected.** Decompression stops at a limit given by the caller (64 MiB per
  part in SQL), every offset is checked, encrypted and ZIP64 archives are refused rather than
  half-read, and a test feeds thousands of noise streams to `inflate`. It joins the fuzz targets
  in the build step.
- **Text, not layout.** Paragraphs become lines; Word tabs and breaks, ODF's `text:s` spaces,
  and character references are kept; styles, comments and tracked changes are not. Spreadsheets
  give their shared strings, and slides come in slide order.
- **In SQL as `extract_text(bytea)`**, so a generated column or a trigger can keep a document's
  text next to it for `to_tsvector`.

Left: PDF (its own content streams and fonts), PST, OCR on D31's models, IMAP, JMAP and SMTP,
CalDAV, CardDAV and WebDAV, EXIF and XMP metadata and renditions, and permission-aware
enterprise search.
