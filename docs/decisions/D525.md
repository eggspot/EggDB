## D525 Kafka consumer groups — Decided by the code-only session (2026-11-11, D355); roadmap M16

The Kafka listener now coordinates consumer groups, so clients that subscribe with a `group.id`
(the Java client, librdkafka and confluent-kafka, kafka-python) share a topic's partitions and
resume from committed offsets. The protocol is Apache-licensed and documented publicly.

- **APIs:** FindCoordinator v0-2 (the one broker is every group's coordinator), JoinGroup v0-2,
  SyncGroup v0-1, Heartbeat v0-1, LeaveGroup v0-1, OffsetCommit v2-3 and OffsetFetch v1-3 (v2+
  with no topics returns every committed partition). Versions are the non-flexible ones, like the
  APIs already served; ApiVersions lists them, so clients pick them.
- **Rebalancing:** a coordinator shared by the listener's connections keeps each group's members,
  generation, chosen protocol, leader and assignments in memory. A join (or a member leaving or
  timing out on its session) starts a round; each join waits, up to its rebalance timeout
  (capped at 60 s), until every member of the round has joined, then the generation advances,
  the first member by id leads, the protocol is the leader's first one that every member offers,
  and only the leader is sent the members' metadata. SyncGroup waits for the leader's
  assignments and gives each member its own. Heartbeats answer `REBALANCE_IN_PROGRESS`,
  `ILLEGAL_GENERATION` and `UNKNOWN_MEMBER_ID` as Kafka does. Session timeouts must be 6 s to
  30 min; groups (10,000) and members per group (1,000) are bounded.
- **Offsets:** committed offsets and their metadata live in the Kafka keyspace under their own
  prefix, so they survive restarts; a commit with generation -1 (a simple consumer) is taken
  without a member, others must match the current generation. Group membership itself is not
  kept across restarts: members rejoin, as they do when a Kafka coordinator moves.
- **Left out:** static membership (`group.instance.id`), incremental cooperative rebalancing
  (KIP-848's new protocol), ListGroups, DescribeGroups and DeleteGroups, offset retention, and
  transactions.

**Status:** code written, unbuilt (code-only mode, D355).
