## D577 Oracle's `USER_SCHEDULER_JOBS` — Decided by the code-only session (2026-11-12, D355); roadmap M10, D524

D524 keeps `DBMS_SCHEDULER` jobs in `eggdb_internal.oracle_scheduler_jobs`, but SQL Developer,
Toad and DBA scripts look for jobs in the dictionary. `USER_SCHEDULER_JOBS`, `ALL_SCHEDULER_JOBS`
and `DBA_SCHEDULER_JOBS` now answer from that table, read once into the catalog snapshot as
D575 reads MySQL's events: `OWNER` (the `ALL_` and `DBA_` forms), `JOB_NAME` in upper case,
`JOB_TYPE`, `JOB_ACTION`, `START_DATE`, `REPEAT_INTERVAL`, `END_DATE`, `ENABLED` and `AUTO_DROP`
as `TRUE`/`FALSE`, `STATE` (`SCHEDULED` or `DISABLED`), `LAST_START_DATE` and `COMMENTS` (empty).
Jobs belong to the database rather than a schema in D524, so every job shows under the
connecting user. `NEXT_RUN_DATE`, run counts and the job log views are not built.

**Status:** code written, unbuilt (code-only mode, D355).
