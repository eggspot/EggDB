## D533 MongoDB TTL indexes — Decided by the code-only session (2026-11-11, D355); roadmap M9

`createIndexes` with `expireAfterSeconds` on a one-field index, and a time-series collection's
`expireAfterSeconds` on its `timeField`, now expire documents, as apps use them for sessions,
caches and event logs.

- **Rule:** a document expires once the date in the indexed field (the earliest date when the
  field holds an array) plus the seconds is past; documents without a date in the field never
  expire, as in MongoDB. A negative or fractional value, or a TTL on a compound index, is refused
  with `InvalidOptions`.
- **Hidden at once, removed on the next insert:** MongoDB's TTL monitor deletes expired documents
  about once a minute, so they can be read for a while; EggDB filters them out of every read and
  write that selects documents as soon as they expire, and deletes them physically the next time
  the collection takes an insert. A collection that is only read keeps them stored, hidden.
- **Left out:** `collMod` to change `expireAfterSeconds`, partial TTL indexes, and a background
  sweep for collections that are never written.

**Status:** code written, unbuilt (code-only mode, D355).
