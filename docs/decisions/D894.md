## D894 JMAP over the email store — Decided by the code-only session (2026-11-12, D355); follow-up of D892 and D893

- `eggdb serve --jmap-listen <address>` (default port 8443, loopback only until it has TLS, refused with `--read-only`)
  serves the store over JMAP (RFC 8620 core, RFC 8621 mail), written in `eggdb-mail/src/jmap.rs` with a small JSON
  reader of its own (`json.rs`, nesting limited to 64) so the crate stays free of the SQL engine. Requests sign in with
  HTTP Basic as the server's user; the one account's id is `a`.
- The session resource (`/.well-known/jmap`, also `/jmap/session`) names the core and mail capabilities and their
  limits; `/jmap/api` takes `using` and up to 64 `methodCalls`, answers `methodResponses` and `sessionState`, and
  resolves result references (`#ids` with `resultOf`, `name` and a JSON-pointer `path`, `*` mapping over arrays).
  Unknown capabilities, methods and accounts give the RFC's error types.
- Methods: `Core/echo`; `Mailbox/get` (ids, names, parents from `/` hierarchy, roles from well-known names, counts,
  rights, subscription), `Mailbox/query`, `Mailbox/set` (create, destroy); `Email/query` (filters `inMailbox`, `from`,
  `to`, `cc`, `subject`, `body`, `text`, `hasKeyword`, `notKeyword`, `before`, `after`, `minSize`, `maxSize`, and `AND`,
  `OR`, `NOT` conditions; sort by `receivedAt` or `size`; `position` and `limit`); `Email/get` (headers decoded,
  addresses, `messageId`/`inReplyTo`/`references`, `sentAt`, `receivedAt`, `preview`, keywords, the text body as
  part 1 with `bodyValues` on request, and a `properties` choice); `Email/set` (keywords as a whole or by
  `keywords/<name>` patches, moving by `mailboxIds`, destroying; creating is `forbidden` until uploads come);
  `Thread/get` (each message its own thread).
- Ids are made from the mailbox name (hex) and the UID, so they stay stable while the message stays put; keywords
  map to IMAP's flags (`$seen` is `\Seen`, and so on). `/jmap/download/a/<blobId>/<name>` returns the raw message.
- Left: uploads, `Email/import` and `Email/set` create, `EmailSubmission`, `/changes` methods and push
  (`eventSourceUrl` is named but not served), MIME parts and attachments, threads by references, and TLS.
