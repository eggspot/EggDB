## D767 Stream-stream joins and late data (eggdb_streams) — Decided by the code-only session (2026-11-12, D355); roadmap M16, stream-stream joins and late data (D55)

- `create_join(name, left_stream, right_stream, condition, within, select_list, target)` is an interval join of two
  streams (D766): pairs that meet `condition` (over `l` and `r`) and whose event times are at most `within` apart.
  A pair is emitted into `target` once both streams' watermarks have passed the later of its two times, and each pair
  exactly once, since `emitted_until` moves to the smaller watermark after each run. `advance` on either stream runs
  the joins that use it.
- Late data is kept aside (D766) and can be taken in later: `replay_late(stream)` inserts the stream's late events
  (with the late check turned off for that transaction through the `eggdb_streams.replaying` setting) and, for each
  tumbling or hopping window that had already closed over them, deletes and recomputes the affected windows in the
  target, so the target shows the corrected counts. Each replayed event leaves `late_events`.
- Left: late events replayed into session windows and joins (their emitted rows stay as they were), retractions
  sent downstream for corrected windows, and joins over more than two streams.
